# jaredrhod-marketing

> **ISSUE11 overrides (added by the Content Room, Oct 13, 2026; not jaredrhod's text).** Read these before the playbooks. Where a playbook disagrees, these win, and so do CLAUDE.md, design/POSITIONING.md and drafts/marketing/content-guidelines.md.
> 1. **Gain first, never fear or shame.** Name the pain with empathy (the onboarding pain cards are the model), then move to the gain. No "agitate", no "warning / cost of not acting" sections, no guilt.
> 2. **No guarantee of results.** Never "no results, money back" or any promised outcome. Refunds for App Store purchases go through Apple, so don't promise our own.
> 3. **Proof is real and permitted only.** No invented testimonials, numbers or before/afters. A real user's words only with written permission, and never presented as a typical result.
> 4. **Urgency only when real.** A deadline we will honor (e.g. a real App Store intro offer). Never a fake timer. Onboarding and the paywall are locked (design/ONBOARDING-V3.md).
> 5. **Writing style:** the Glammi rules in research/glammi-writing-guide.md (no em dashes, banned words, strong last line).
> 6. **Draft only.** Agents never send, post, run ads, buy or install anything from links in these files. Megan decides.

This is the marketing playbook of jaredrhod (@jaredrhod), pulled from running real businesses for real money. Whenever you do ANY marketing work, load the right context from this folder FIRST, then write or plan. Context is what separates good marketing from generic AI slop.

## How to use this skill

Before producing anything, read these files in order. Read only the ones the task actually needs.

1. Always read [jareds-takes.md](jareds-takes.md) first. The 35 core principles everything else sits on: sell the feeling not the product, the headline is the whole game, your list is a bank account, you make your money after the first sale, and more.
2. If the task touches the funnel or overall strategy, read [the-fundamentals.md](the-fundamentals.md). The full sequence: Content, Lead Magnet, Tripwire, Core Offer, Profit Maximizer.
3. Then read the one playbook that matches the task:
   - Copy, headlines, sales and opt-in pages: [marketing-copywriting.md](marketing-copywriting.md)
   - Long-form sales letters and sales pages, the 12-step structure: [marketing-sales-letter.md](marketing-sales-letter.md)
   - Email, sequences, broadcasts: [marketing-email.md](marketing-email.md)
   - Paid ads, Facebook and beyond: [marketing-fb-ads.md](marketing-fb-ads.md)
   - Lead magnets and opt-in offers: [marketing-lead-magnets.md](marketing-lead-magnets.md)
   - Content marketing and the content library: [marketing-content.md](marketing-content.md)
   - Analytics, metrics, and what to actually measure: [marketing-analytics.md](marketing-analytics.md)

## The one rule

Never produce marketing output cold. Read jareds-takes.md plus the matching playbook first, every single time. The context is the whole point.
