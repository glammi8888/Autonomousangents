# Anti-slop checklist (Megan, Nov 4, 2026: "i think it's slop" · "let's make sure that all content moving forward is anti-slop please")
Applies to every article, gift, email, caption and page, for both ISSUE11 and Glammi. Run it before showing Megan anything.

1. **Specific beats generic.** Every line should only fit this brand. If it could sit in any Amazon journal or any agency blog, rewrite it with a concrete detail (a number, a time, an object, a scene).
2. **Fewer, sharper.** Cut quantity padding. 11 strong prompts beat 40 okay ones. No filler sections.
3. **Never invent Megan's story or voice.** No "Hi, it's Megan" letters, quotes or anecdotes she didn't write. Use the brand voice, or ask her for the real story.
4. **Break the template.** No mirror-image sections of equal length, no tidy groups of 3 or 7 everywhere, no "Quick Takeaways" tables when they add nothing.
5. **No AI tells.** No "it's not X, it's Y", no "no X, no Y, no Z", no "in today's fast-paced world", "unlock", "elevate", "journey", "game-changer", "dive in", "whether you're… or…", no em dashes, no sentences that trail into extra comma clauses.
6. **Sound like a person talking.** Contractions, short sentences, a little attitude. Read it out loud. Rewrite anything you'd never say.
7. **Real proof only.** Real sources and real numbers. No fake stats, reviews or stories.
8. **Use the brand's own copy as the model.** ISSUE11: design/landing/FRAMER-COPY-FINAL.md ("You've saved enough. Do something with it."). Glammi: glammi.ai.
