## Image prompts (one hero image per aesthetic)
Paste the **style anchor** first, then the aesthetic line. Same anchor every time, so all 8 tiles feel like one magazine.

**Style anchor:** Editorial fashion photograph for a bold women's magazine. One woman, confident, styled head to toe. Shot on medium format, crisp detail, rich colour, a touch of pink somewhere in the frame. Portrait 4:5, subject centred so it still works cropped square. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

1. **Animal Safari:** Golden hour on the savanna, she wears a tailored safari linen set with a leopard-print scarf and pink-tinted sunglasses, giraffes and acacia trees in the soft-focus background.
2. **Tropical Oasis:** She lounges by a turquoise pool tiled in pink, giant monstera and palm shadows across her, hot pink swimsuit and gold jewellery, midday sun.
3. **Space Cowboy:** She stands in a moonlit desert in a chrome-silver cowboy hat, rhinestone boots and a fringed jacket, a starry sky and a glowing pink horizon behind her.
4. **Surrealist Fashion Editorial:** She sits on an oversized pink chair inside a room filled with soft clouds, giant pearls floating around her, sculptural white gown.
5. **Chrome Future:** She wears a liquid-chrome pink dress and wraparound silver sunglasses, lit with direct flash against a glossy metallic backdrop.
6. **Matcha Latte:** Morning light in a cream linen kitchen, she holds a ceramic cup of matcha, sage-green knit set, fresh flowers and a stack of magazines on the table.
7. **Everything Glitter:** She is covered head to toe in pink and silver sequins, glitter on her cheekbones, a giant disco ball reflecting light spots across the room.
8. **Minimalism:** She stands in a bare white gallery space in a sharply cut ivory column dress, one pink detail (a single earring), soft shadow, lots of empty space.
