## The idea in one line
Same magazine engine, pointed at someone you love: a shorter issue about another person, for an occasion, that you can download or print as a gift.

## Where it starts
- Issue tab › "New issue" (and You › My Issues) asks: **For me** (today's flow, unchanged) or **As a gift**.
- Your own issue keeps going. One gift issue at a time, next to it.

## What she sees and does
1. **Who + occasion:** their name, and Birthday · Anniversary · Graduation · Best friend · Just because. Optional "needed by" date.
2. **Pages:** same 3-step page maker (content → layout, "Editor decides" → keep). Content = photos she uploads, her own words, Explore images. Never her journal or actions (those stay private and about her).
3. **The Editor** suggests page prompts for the occasion ("A memory only we have", "Why I love you", "Your year in 5 photos"). Her words are never changed.
4. **Cover:** their name + occasion, in the ISSUE11 cover style.
5. **Finish (12 or 20 pages):** Download PDF (share sheet) · Order the print on issue11.app (no in-app payment, no Apple cut).

## Underneath
- Stays on the phone like everything else; photos go to the printer only when she orders the print.
- Doesn't count toward "Actions done" and doesn't change her pace or her 40-page issue.
- "Needed by" date → one reminder 10 days before; print timing shown as an estimate, never a promised date (Lulu rules in lessons.md).
- Members only (part of the membership), so nothing new in the paywall.

## Why it fits
- Same engine (pages, layouts, PDF, print), so it's a mode, not a new app.
- Brings in a new buyer (people making gifts, incl. men 18–24 per TikTok) and print revenue on the website.
- Seasonal moments: Valentine's Day (Feb 14), Mother's Day, graduation season.
