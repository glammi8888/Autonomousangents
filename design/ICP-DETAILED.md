A woman of 24–38 who believes in manifestation (or did), tried the affirmations, vision boards and scripting, didn't see it change her life, and now wants **one calm, beautiful practice that gets her doing things**, not more content to consume. [Megan, Nov 9 + Reddit]

## 4. Her psychology (the skill's ladder)
**Main need: competence, with autonomy.** She doesn't lack desire or belief. She lacks proof she can follow through. She wants to feel capable, on her own terms, without a guru telling her "the one way". [Reddit: loop + gurus quotes · Guess on the ranking]

**Identity she protects:** a tasteful, self-aware woman with a rich inner life. Spiritual *and* smart. [Megan]
**Identity she refuses:** delusional, cringe, "girl boss", someone who talks about manifesting but never changes anything. [Megan + Reddit]
**Identity she's reaching for:** the woman already living the life she sees: calm, consistent, quietly doing it. [Megan]

Her rule of thumb (her words): "Doing to get = trying. Doing to remember = being." Detachment ≠ doing nothing; it's "inspired action" from a calm place. [Reddit]

## 7. What she's really buying
1. **Proof she's moving:** pages she kept, actions she did, an issue that fills to 40. [Megan + v1 logic]
2. **Something beautiful and real:** her own magazine, then a printed copy for the "depth of paper". No competitor gives her digital consistency *and* paper. [Reddit]
3. **Calm, not pressure:** release the outcome, keep the practice. [Reddit, Megan's thesis]
4. **Her attention back on her own life**, away from watching other people's. [Megan, Oct 15]

## 8. Her daily rhythm (where the app fits) [Reddit]
- **Morning:** visualizes half asleep, 3 gratitudes → Home: today's question + this week's action.
- **During the day:** affirmations while cooking or driving → Listen: sessions keep playing when the phone locks.
- **Evening:** scripting, revising the day → Journal + tonight's session.

## 9. When she's ready to download (triggers)
1. December / New Year reset, vision board season. **This is our launch window (Dec 11).** [Megan + Reddit]
2. A birthday, breakup, move or new job. [Guess]
3. Right after a burst of motivation fades. [Reddit]
4. Seeing a magazine reveal from someone like her. [Guess: test in content]

## 10. Her words vs. ours
- **She says:** dream life, living in the end, self-concept, the shift, inspired action, the 3D, scripting, SP, delulu, "that girl". [Reddit]
- **We say back:** your issue, your cover, your pages, this week's action, the Editor, release the outcome. [Megan]
- **Never:** "becoming her", "move", "girl boss", guarantees, therapy words, mocking belief. [Megan]

## 11. What this means for content and the app
1. Hook on the pain (the loop, the hows, plan killers), then show the magazine as step one, then the action.
2. Pinterest + long YouTube over hype short-form. Substance, not hyperbole.
3. Founder-led is the most believable proof (your details are yours to share, never written for you).
4. Session scripts (your weekend writing): calm, present tense, one small action near the end. Matches her evening routine.
