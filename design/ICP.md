## 5. What she's buying
- The feeling of becoming the woman she sees, made **tangible** (her own magazine) and **doable** (one small step a week). [Megan + Research]
- Proof she's moving: "You wanted this 8 months ago. Is it part of your life now?" [Megan]
- Something beautiful she'd be **proud to share**. [Megan]

## 6. Taste (design for this) [Megan]
- **Yes:** Soho House × Pinterest × OPEN × Glitché × fashion magazine × self-development. Editorial, flat, printed feel. Big type, real photos, restraint.
- **No:** purple galaxies, angel wings, generic Canva affirmations, "GIRL BOSS" energy, cheesy quotes, tacky AI vision boards, glossy 3D.

## 7. Her vocabulary
- **She says:** dream life, future self, next chapter, self-concept, alignment, higher self, "that girl", manifest. [Megan]
- **We say back (brand words):** your Issue, cover, edition, art direction, your next move, process, rehearse. [Megan]
- **Avoid in our copy:** "becoming her" (overused, Megan Oct 7), TikTok slang overload, "girl boss", outcome promises ("you'll be rich"), therapy/mental-health language. [Megan]

## 8. Triggers (when she's ready to download)
1. **New Year / December** reset: vision board season. [Megan + Research]
2. A **birthday, breakup, move or new job**: "new chapter" moments. [Assumed]
3. Seeing a beautiful **magazine reveal** from someone like her. [Assumed: test in content]
4. Feeling **stuck after a burst of motivation** fades. [Research]

## 12. What this means for the Content Room
1. **Lead with the gap, not the dream.** Hook = the feeling of "I can see it, I can't reach it". Then the magazine as step one, then the small move.
2. **Show, don't promise.** Magazine reveals, cover flips, "my move this week" (tiny, real). Never "this will make you rich".
3. **Two angles to A/B test** (the open question): *Dream-life experience* ("art-direct your future") vs. *Follow-through* ("one small move a week"). Same visuals, different hook.
4. **Founder-led is the strongest proof.** Megan building her own Issue is the most believable content we have. Personal details are Megan's to share. Never write them for her.
5. **Pinterest + Instagram first, TikTok for reveals.** [Assumed: confirm with waitlist sign-up sources]

## 13. What's NOT proven yet (validate after waitlist / beta)
- She'll pay for a subscription. · She wants an AI-built magazine. · She prefers process audio over outcome affirmations. · Age range and income are right.
How: waitlist question ("What made you sign up?"), 5–10 beta interviews, then run the Customer Research Kit ICP skill on real answers.
