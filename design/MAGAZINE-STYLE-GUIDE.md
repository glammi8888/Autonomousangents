## 0. Megan's board (Oct 19, 2026) · the look to aim for
Megan shared 5 screenshots of her Pinterest board: "This is how I want the Magazines to look like" + "I don't want it to be an editing tool like canva" + "no need to design templates yet but i would like the app to reflect that logic and keeping it simple".
What repeats on her board (use these for every layout):
- **Indie / experimental print**, not glossy: risograph pink duotone, halftone, overprint, paper and fold texture.
- **Giant type** cut off at the page edge; mixed fonts (condensed bold sans + serif italic + typewriter mono).
- **Collage grids** of 9–16 small images; torn/cut-out photos; stickers and stars.
- **Color blocks:** hot pink, red, acid yellow, black; often one color per spread.
- **Spreads** (two pages side by side), page numbers, real margins.
- Fashion/beauty portraits, close-up faces, lots of white space next to dense pages.
**Logic (decided):** a fixed library of page templates in this look. Lucky Star only picks the template, her images and the color treatment. "↻ Another look" = another template. She never moves things around. Templates are designed later (Megan in Canva or a hired magazine designer); until then the app uses a few placeholder templates.

## 2. Rhythm (the "edited" feeling)
- Never the same layout twice in a row; max 3 of one type per 40 pages.
- Alternate loud (full-bleed, huge type) and quiet (white space, small type).
- Alternate image-heavy and text-heavy spreads.
- Openers and closers are fixed: cover → contents → editor's letter … note → coming next → back cover.

## 3. Type
- Headlines: Archivo Black, uppercase, tight, max ~6 words.
- Captions/kickers: IBM Plex Mono, tiny, uppercase.
- Body: Archivo Narrow, short lines, lots of air. Restraint over decoration.
- Voice: headlines say "she"; her words stay "I" in quotes; never rewrite her words.

## 4. Photos
- Crop bold: tight, off-center, unexpected; never cut through a face; never put text on a face.
- Pick the strongest image (sharp, well lit); skip blurry or tiny images (print needs high resolution).
- Max 2 photos from the same shoot per spread.

## 5. Color
- Page colors only from the theme: white, ink, cream, blush, pink, red (rare), green (rare).
- One accent per spread.

## 6. Editor's check (automatic, before a page appears)
Text fits its slot · photo resolution OK for print · no face covered · colors from the theme · rhythm rules respected. Fails → try another approved layout; never show a broken page.

## 7. Print (US Letter 8.5×11, glued spine, Lulu)
- Every layout has a print version: 300 dpi, bleed, safe margins, nothing important near the spine.
- Test: 10 sample Issues from 10 made-up users; print 2. "Would I buy this?" before beta.

## Inspiration, not copying
Study what makes indie art/fashion/beauty magazines feel indie (white space, odd crops, tiny captions, big type). Design ISSUE11's own layouts from those principles; never copy a magazine's page.
