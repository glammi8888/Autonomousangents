## Purpose
**One line (Megan, Oct 2, 2026):** "ISSUE11 is a manifestation app rooted in behavioral change with the creation of your own Magazine."

> "ISSUE11 is fundamentally about helping someone turn the life they want to manifest into a life they actually move toward."

> "Manifestation is the emotional/psychological framework, but behavioral change is what differentiates ISSUE11."

> "The personal magazine is the container."

The outcome isn't "I made a beautiful magazine." It's:
> **"I became the person I was imagining."**

## The loop (locked, Megan, Oct 3, 2026)
**DESIRE → IMAGINE → EMBODY → BECOME**: the behavioral logic *underneath* Your World, Audio, Scribe, Publish and Lucky Star. **Not navigation, not extra features, not a goal tracker.** Full text: design/PRODUCT-ARCHITECTURE.md.

| Stage | Meaning | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **DESIRE** | Define what you actually want | Scribe, Your World, Lucky Star (clarify) |
| **IMAGINE** | Make that future vivid | Your World, Audio, Scribe, Publish (the magazine) |
| **EMBODY** | Practice being that person | Audio + rituals, Scribe, Lucky Star's one Next Move per week |
| **BECOME** | Notice the future becoming real | Proof ("Add Proof"), proof in her Issue |

Superseded: DESIRE → IMAGINE → EMBODY → ACT → BECOME (Oct 2) and DESIRE → IMAGINE → BECOME → PROVE (Oct 3, earlier).

## Lucky Star's role
> "Lucky Star should serve ISSUE11's purpose—not become the purpose."

> "Tell me what you want → I'll help you envision it → let's translate it into behavior → I'll remember what you're working toward → let's notice when it starts becoming real."

> **"The AI is infrastructure. The transformation is the product."**

## Rules that follow from this (Design Room)
1. **Every feature names its stage.** If it doesn't move her along the loop, it waits.
2. **Close the loop, lightly.** Imagining leads to embodying (one Next Move per week, never task lists), and what becomes real can be added as Proof.
3. **The issue fills with evidence over time.** Proof (wins, "ADD PROOF") goes into her magazine, so later issues show the future becoming the present.
4. **Measure the transformation, not the output.** Product success = Next Moves tried and Proofs added per user, not issues made or minutes in the app.
5. **Trust:** no promised results ("you will get rich"). We help her imagine and act; we never guarantee outcomes, in the app or in marketing.

## The one test (Megan, Oct 2, 2026)
> "That's not just messaging. It can become the logic behind the entire product."

> "Every Lucky Star feature now has to pass one test: **Does this help her move from wanting the future to becoming it?** If not, you probably don't need it."

> "The magazine stays the hero. Lucky Star just makes it intelligent."

**Lucky Star's job:** figure out where she is in the loop, and help her move to the next step.

Every feature and every Lucky Star command must pass this test before it is specced or built (the pipeline Planner checks it).

## Clarity first (Megan, Oct 2, 2026)
> "Clarity should beat cleverness at the beginning, while the editorial personality can sit on top."

In the first 60 seconds she must understand:
1. **What is ISSUE11?** A manifestation practice that turns the future you want into something you see, hear and act on.
2. **What do I do here?** Tell ISSUE11 what you want → build your future Issue → practice it → take action → collect proof as it becomes real.
3. **What is Lucky Star?** Your AI guide that helps you do all of the above.

**The 60-second test.** After onboarding she can tell a friend:
> "It's a manifestation app where you create a magazine about your future life, practice becoming that person, and actually take steps toward it. The little 11 is an AI that helps you."
