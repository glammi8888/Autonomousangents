# Visual QA: before anything visual is called done

Set by Megan on Nov 15, 2026, word for word. This applies to every agent doing visual work (pins, lead magnets, magazine pages, wallpapers, website and Framer pages, ads, Glammi pages).

> Before anything visual is called done:
> 1. Screenshot it and actually look, on desktop and on phone.
> 2. Faces and heads: nothing cut off at the forehead, chin or mouth.
> 3. No stray logos or text: no brand logos, car badges or readable signs from the source photo, and our own logo always fully visible.
> 4. Readable: enough contrast, text not too small, nothing running off the edge on a phone.
> 5. The image fits the topic: the tone matches what the page is about.
> 6. On-brand: the site's own fonts, colors and spacing, with the ISSUE11 logo at its fixed size, or Glammi's chrome look.
> 7. Compare against what you asked for, or against the reference you shared.
> 8. Re-check after publishing: what looks right in the editor can differ on the live site.

Megan: "always ask it to self-improve and self-correct if things seem odd, cropped, or not relevant (example of non relevant content: A money picture for a beauty prompt)".

How to run it:
- **Look at your own screenshots** (Playwright, deviceScaleFactor 1 for your own checks). Do the phone check at 390px wide. For pins, also check the ~236px feed width.
- **Self-correct.** If anything seems odd, cropped or not relevant, fix it and look again before reporting. Don't hand over something you'd flag yourself.
- **Image-to-topic match.** Each photo must fit its chapter or prompt (beauty → face, skincare or vanity; money → money or work; love → friends). When no photo fits, use the closest honest one and ask Megan for the specific shot (she offered to create them).
- **Report.** Say what you checked and what you fixed. "Done" without screenshots doesn't count.
- **Previews.** This replaces the old "never render or screenshot unless Megan asks" rule. Checking is now always required. Sending previews to Megan is still only when it helps her decide.
