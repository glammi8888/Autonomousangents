# Brief for Claude Design · ISSUE11 app re-skin (Oct 21, 2026)
Paste this into Claude Design, with the images listed at the bottom.

## What ISSUE11 is
An iPhone app where she builds her dream life into her own magazine (her "Issue", 40 pages, printable). One small real-life move a week becomes a page. The magazine is the hero. An AI "Editor" (the 11 mark) designs her pages from her saves; it is not a chatbot.

## The problem
The old look felt like a cartoon (glossy pink bubbles, rounded stickers, drop shadows). New look = indie creative studio / art-book / gallery. Calm, lots of white space.

## Locked by the founder (don't change)
- **Logo:** the wavy ISSUE11 wordmark (the only "melting"/funky element; titles stay straight).
- **Colors:** white/off-white (#F6F6F4) base, black (#0D0D0D), baby pink (#F7C6D9) only in small touches (the 11 square, a tag, one tile). No hot-pink blocks, no gradients, no glass.
- **Fonts (Google Fonts):** titles = Archivo Condensed 800 UPPERCASE · text = Inter Tight (800 tight for bold sentences) · labels = IBM Plex Mono 500 uppercase, small.
- **Shapes:** flat, square corners, hairline rules, no shadows.
- **Photos:** real magazine photos (printed issues in hands, open spreads). Never a drawn/fake 3D magazine. File-name captions like "issue01_cover.jpg" are on-brand.
- **Tabs:** home · explore · issue · journal · you (lowercase, active = bold + baby-pink underline). The 11 mark lives in the header and can be dragged.
- **Words:** "My saves" (not My World), "the Editor" (not Lucky Star).

## Screens & logic (keep the flows)
- **Home:** calm long page. Top = poster-style Issue title + "open my issue · 26 to print" + a strip of her magazine. Then only: 01 this week's move ("I made room for it" / "not yet"), 02 tonight's session. Ends "THAT'S TODAY. see you tomorrow."
- **Explore:** 3 tabs: for you (gallery of real images + sessions with a small "▶ session" tag) · sessions (organized rows by type, "see all") · my saves (her vibe; "+ make a page", "+ add photo").
- **Issue:** her Issue as a real magazine + all 40 pages numbered 01–40; empty pages say what fills them; "+ make a page"; at 40/40 → approve & order the print ($29.99).
- **Make a page:** pick 1–3 images → write one line → the Editor designs it from templates → "↻ another look" / "keep page". No Canva-style editing.
- **Journal:** her words appear live on a magazine page; "keep page".

## What we need from you
3–4 numbered options for Home first, then Explore and Issue, in this look. Lots of white space. Nothing loud or cartoony.
