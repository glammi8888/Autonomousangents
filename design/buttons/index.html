Button lab

Eight button ideas for ISSUE11. Tap and hold each one. Pick the ones you love; the app keeps two or three, not all eight.

No. 01Print press
My pick: main buttons

Pushes into the page

A hard black shadow, like ink offset on a printed page. On tap it sinks flat, which feels physical with a haptic tap.

Use for: Continue, Keep my issue, Start free trial
No. 023D extruded

Same depth as the logo

Pink and red layers underneath, tilted slightly back. Matches the 3D splash logo. Very bold: best for one hero moment.

Use for: Splash "Enter", Print my issue
No. 03Wave fill
My pick: brand signature

The logo's wave sweeps in

Outline button. On tap, a pink wave rises through it, borrowed from the wavy wordmark. Nobody else has this.

Use for: Save, Add to my world, secondary actions
No. 04Liquid glass
My pick: on photos

Frosted glass on photos

Apple's iOS 26 "Liquid Glass" look. Blurs the photo behind it, so it stays readable on any image. Apple likes apps that use its newest design language.

Use for: Player controls, Skip on onboarding photos, hero buttons
No. 05Marquee

Words run through it

Text scrolls inside the button like a fashion-week banner. Speeds up when you press. Great energy, but only once per screen.

Use for: "Enter issue", launch moments
No. 06Split arrow

Label + round arrow

Straight from your identity kit's mockups. The pink circle turns forward on tap. Calm and editorial.

Use for: "Next page" links, list rows, Explore
No. 07Hold to confirm

Hold it to make it real

Fills green while you hold, then confirms. Turns a tap into a small ritual, fitting for manifesting. Also prevents accidental taps.

Use for: "Yes, it happened", Print my issue
No. 08Sticker

Magazine price sticker

A round, tilted sticker like on a magazine cover. Wiggles on tap. Playful, for small extras.

Use for: "New" badges, Share the cover, offers

My recommendation

  1. Print press (01) for every main action. One consistent, confident button.
  2. Wave fill (03) for secondary actions. It's the brand's signature.
  3. Liquid glass (04) only on top of photos and in the Player.
  4. Plus one ritual: Hold to confirm (07) for "Yes, it happened".
