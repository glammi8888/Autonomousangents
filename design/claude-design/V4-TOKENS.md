## Type
- Display: **Archivo Black**, uppercase, tight tracking (-0.03 to -0.065em). Hero 58px / line-height .86 · cards 38px · section 26–28px.
- Body/labels: **Archivo Narrow** 400/600/700.
- Utility: **IBM Plex Mono** 11px, uppercase (VOL. 01, page counts, card kickers, tab labels).

## Shape and effects
- Radius: images and segments 3px (near-square, editorial) · cards 16px · pills 22px · capsule 33–48px.
- Glass pill ("Your issue"): white gradient 24%→6%, 1px white 50% border, inset highlight, blur 16px + saturate 180%.
- Hero: full-bleed photo 560px tall, 8px side margin, dark gradient top + bottom for text.
