## The 7 beliefs

### 1 · Manifestation is participation
- **Old belief we fight:** manifesting = wishing, visualizing and waiting.
- **ISSUE11 believes:** imagination starts it; taking part makes it real. Manifestation is not the enemy; passive manifestation is (Bible §01: "The objective is evolution, not debunking").
- **Why:** Megan, Nov 9 (lessons): "The problem is that « manifestation » doesn't work but that's because they're doing it wrong. Issue11 is rooted in behavioral change psychology and is process based" · "we are giving tools outside of « wishful thinking and passive meditations »". Research anchor with its limits: Oettingen's mental contrasting work, pairing the picture with the obstacle (Playbook §05; never "visualization is harmful").
- **Her line:** "You don't have to see it to believe it. But you have to participate to create it." (EDITORIAL-BIBLE.md, copy registry)
- **Carousels:** "How to not fall for "wishful thinking" when manifesting" (Megan's hook, lessons Nov 17) · "Manifestation doesn't work. At least not the way you've been doing it." (live landing line Megan kept, lessons Nov 12; the second line always sits under the first) · "The manifestation loop" (Bible §09 diagram; ref diagram 1)

### 2 · The process is yours, the outcome isn't
- **Old belief:** obsess over the finish line: when the money, partner, body or job will arrive.
- **ISSUE11 believes:** rehearse the process of becoming, not only the end result. Outcomes can't be promised; the next step can.
- **Why:** Megan, Oct 7: "Visualizing outcomes don't work, focusing on what you can control: the process, does." USP example: instead of "Imagine having your dream body", imagine getting up, putting on workout clothes, walking into the gym (POSITIONING.md). Pain points 4 and 5. Bible §01: "You are not the controller of everything that happens to you. You are a participant in what happens next."
- **Her line:** "You can't control the outcome. You are in control of the process." (POSITIONING.md, Oct 7; marked "Megan to confirm")
- **Carousels:** outcome affirmations vs process affirmations (*working*, Old vs New table; C2 in drafts/social/2026-11-17-informative-carousels.md) · "Feeling like you've arrived is not the same as moving forward." (Bible §04) · "The aesthetic of change is not the experience of change." (Playbook hook 07)

### 3 · A dream needs a next step (the board is step one)
- **Old belief:** make the board, look at it, the rest follows.
- **ISSUE11 believes:** keep the dream big and give it a first move. The Issue is where the dream gets seen, revisited and lived toward.
- **Why:** Reddit research Megan saved Oct 10: "Vision boards become forgotten pictures" → "Turn visions into small actions and revisit them meaningfully". Pain 6 ("inspires me for five minutes, then I forget about it"). Guardrail: say "most vision boards get forgotten", never "vision boards don't work" (POSITIONING.md).
- **Her line:** "The goal isn't to make the dream smaller. It's to make the path feel possible." (POSITIONING.md, Oct 10)
- **Carousels:** "Your vision board needs a feedback loop." (Playbook campaign example) · "Your Pinterest board knows your taste. Does it know your obstacles?" (Playbook hook 08) · "Your vision board needs a Tuesday" (C1, informative-carousels.md)

### 4 · A setback is not a verdict
- **Old belief:** if it didn't manifest, you blocked it; your mindset failed.
- **ISSUE11 believes:** real change is uneven. After a slow month you can keep going, adjust, pause or stop, and stopping can be agency. Nobody manifests their own hardship.
- **Why:** Bible §07–08 (nonlinear change; "Stopping can be an expression of agency rather than a failure of belief"); Bible §04: "You did not manifest every terrible thing that happened to you." Megan, Nov 17: "Content needs to be polarizing" (take a side against the doctrine, never the women who believe it). Pains 4 and 8.
- **Her line:** "The path to your desired life is not obligated to resemble your desired life." (EDITORIAL-BIBLE.md, adversity thesis) · also "Transformation is not the absence of interruption. It is the capacity to resume."
- **Carousels:** "it didn't manifest. and no, you didn't block it" (C3 v2, Megan's pick) · "A setback is not a verdict." (Bible card 04) · keep going, adjust, pause or stop (*working*, decision tree, Bible §15)

### 5 · Become YOU, starting before you feel ready
- **Old belief:** become someone new ("become her"), and wait until you feel aligned or ready.
- **ISSUE11 believes:** the goal is your true self, and practice builds the readiness.
- **Why:** Megan, Oct 7: "I don't like "becoming her" .. it's been overused". Lexicon (30s millennial): "Becoming YOU / Becoming me again". Bible concept bank, "The perfect alignment trap: Waiting to feel completely ready can postpone meaningful experiments." Playbook Myth vs Reality: "Myth: confidence must come first. Reality: confidence can also grow through practice."
- **Her line:** "I'm the art director of my life. I create it page by page. I make my own opportunities." (BANK.md, Megan's own) · brand line "Become your own dream girl" (BANK.md)
- **Carousels:** "The perfect alignment trap" (Bible §18) · "You do not need a new personality. You need a next step." (Playbook hook 05) · "Current You vs. Future You" (Lexicon audience phrase; ref diagram 5, "The versions of you")

### 6 · You're manifesting before you possess it
- **Old belief:** progress only counts when the thing arrives.
- **ISSUE11 believes:** count what changed in you and your conditions. That proof belongs in your Issue.
- **Why:** Bible §06 (possibility accumulation; guardrail "Possibility is not certainty"). Product loop BECOME = Proof added to her Issue (NORTH-STAR.md). Pain 8 ("I don't feel like I'm making progress").
- **Her line:** "You can be manifesting something long before you possess it." (EDITORIAL-BIBLE.md, copy registry)
- **Carousels:** "What has changed in your reality that makes your imagined future more possible today than it was yesterday?" (Bible §01 central question) · Proof before the outcome (*working*; Bible career example: portfolio, mentors, applications) · the Issue as a record of who you're becoming (*working*; PRODUCT-ARCHITECTURE.md)

### 7 · Your dream life should be yours, not the feed's
- **Old belief:** manifesting = consuming more manifestation content and living through other people's lives.
- **ISSUE11 believes:** turn attention back to your own desires, life and pace. Warm, never shaming social media.
- **Why:** Megan, Oct 15: "it's also a way to shift the focus from external (numbing through social media, living vicariously through others, etc..) and get back to yourself and your own goals" (POSITIONING.md). Pain 9. Bible concept "Authorship illusion: Some desires are borrowed from culture or algorithms rather than deliberately chosen."
- **Her line:** "Your future is not a spectator sport." (EDITORIAL-BIBLE.md, carousel close)
- **Carousels:** "Authorship illusion" (Bible §18) · "Is This Building Me... Or Blocking Me?" (Lexicon audience phrase) · "Your future is not a spectator sport." (Bible card 05)

**Ethic under all 7 (Attraction vs Devotion):** "A cult asks you to surrender your agency to a vision. ISSUE11 asks you to use your agency to pursue your own vision." No guarantees, easy to leave, disagreement welcome.

## Carousel formats: one design, three platforms

| Platform | Canvas | Ratio | Slides |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram carousel | 1080×1350 | 4:5 | up to 7 for us (5 is the CAR5 template) |
| TikTok photo mode | 1080×1920 | 9:16 | same 5–7 |
| Pinterest carousel pin | 1000×1500 | 2:3 | 2–5 max, so mark ✂ slides from the start (lessons Nov 17) |

**Master layout:** design every slide on 1080×1920. The **center 1080×1350 band** (y 285–1635) holds all content; the bands above and below are background colour only. Export: IG = crop that band · TikTok = full canvas · Pinterest = scale to 1000 wide (band becomes 1000×1250) and add 125 px of background top and bottom.
**Text-safe box inside the band (approximate):** left 65 px, right 180 px (TikTok action rail), top 0, and nothing but background or image in the band's **bottom ~200 px** (TikTok caption and UI start around 320–480 px up from the bottom edge). Logo top centre and small issue11.app sit inside the text-safe box.
**Safe-zone sources (third-party, not official TikTok/Instagram, they disagree):** aiCarousels (bottom 422, right 173, top 154 px), SocialRails (bottom ~480), OpenClip (bottom ~320, right ~120). So treat as approximate and check one slide on a phone in each app before the batch (Visual QA, design/VISUAL-QA.md).
**Style:** Megan's 11 framework diagrams, black/cream with blush-pink lines, one diagram per slide (drafts/social/CAROUSEL-FRAMEWORK-REFS.md), inside the CAR5 family (Inter Tight, framed photos, n / 5).
