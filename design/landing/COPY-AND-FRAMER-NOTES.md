**5 · FAQ** (visible text = schema text; change both together)
- **What is ISSUE11?** ISSUE11 is a process-focused manifestation app for iPhone. It turns your dreams into a personal magazine and helps you take the small steps that make them real. You save images, listen to short audio sessions, write in your journal and pick one small move a week, and it all becomes pages in your own 40-page Issue.
- **When can I get it?** A private beta starts on November 11, 2026, with invites for people on the waitlist. ISSUE11 launches on the Apple App Store for iPhone on December 11, 2026. Join the waitlist to hear first.
- **How much does it cost?** Joining the waitlist is free. The app is a subscription at $9.99 a month, and your digital magazine is included. A printed copy of your finished Issue is optional and costs $29.99 with free standard shipping in the US.
- **Does ISSUE11 promise results?** No. ISSUE11 is about the process, not a promised outcome. You can't control when something arrives. You can choose one small move this week, and ISSUE11 helps you notice and keep the proof when something shifts.
- **Is there AI in it?** Yes, and it is optional. Lucky Star is the app's helper: it lays out your magazine pages and suggests what to do next. Your own words are never rewritten, and you can switch Lucky Star off in settings. The app still works without it.
- **How is it different from a vision board or a journal?** A vision board shows what you want and a journal holds what you write. ISSUE11 puts both into one magazine about your own life and adds one small move a week, so your dreams turn into steps you actually take.

## Rebuild in Framer (sections · effects · components)
Fonts: Archivo Black (headlines, uppercase, letter-spacing −6%, line-height 0.86) · Archivo Narrow (body) · IBM Plex Mono 11px uppercase (labels, buttons). Colors: ink #0D0D0D · white page · pink #F65AAD · blush #FCD2DD · cream #F5EEE0 · red #D40806 · small pink text #C2207E (contrast).
**Components to make once:** Pink pill button (mono label in ink, pink glow shadow) · Email pill (input + button inside, like the Journal "Keep writing… / KEEP PAGE" field; stacks under 440px) · Glass chip · Phone frame (image fill, 44px radius, black bezel) · 11 sticker (cream rounded square, pink 11, peel on hover) · FAQ row.
1. **Nav:** sticky floating glass pill (blur 16, white 80%). Links hide on mobile.
2. **Hero:** Stack centered. Wordmark SVG fills width (max ~940px). Effects: wordmark "Appear" clip-reveal from bottom on load (one orchestrated moment); phones "Appear" slide up with stagger; phone group uses **Scroll transform**: rotateX 18°→0° and scale 0.94→1.02 while the hero scrolls out; chips get Scroll parallax (speed 0.16–0.24). Desktop: phone group follows the cursor slightly (Framer "Mouse" tilt, max 14°).
3. **Ticker:** Framer Ticker component, black band rotated −1.2°, speed slow.
4. **Credibility:** grid (founder card spans 4/6, two fact tiles, 3 dashed slots hidden on live).
5. **Value:** 3 full-width cards, each **Sticky** (top 96 / 118 / 140px) so each card slides over the previous one (Pillo's stacking effect). Phone inside each card: scroll-linked rotateY ±14°.
6. **How it works:** 2 columns. Right column phone is **Sticky** (centered); its screen swaps per step using Framer "Scroll Variants" (step 1→4 = saves, journal, move, Issue). Mobile: no sticky, each step shows its own phone.
7. **Print band:** 3D magazine: cover layer rotates 0°→−178° (Scroll transform) to open onto the pink page. Simplest Framer route: two image layers + Scroll transform rotateY with perspective 2000.
8. **FAQ:** Accordion, one open by default.
9. **Final CTA:** red rounded card (36px), giant cream 11 with Scroll parallax (0.08), form repeated.
10. **Floating 11:** fixed bottom right; on click opens a small glass card with the same CTA.
**Motion rules:** no scroll hijacking (sticky + scroll transforms only); turn off loops/parallax under "Reduce motion" (Framer: Site settings → Accessibility); on phones keep parallax at about one third.
