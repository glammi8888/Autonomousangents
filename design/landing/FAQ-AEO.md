Headline: **Common questions about ISSUE11.**

1. **What is ISSUE11?**
   ISSUE11 is a process-focused manifestation app for iPhone that turns your dreams into a personal magazine and helps you take the small steps that make them real.
2. **How is ISSUE11 different from a vision board app?**
   A vision board shows what you want, and most get forgotten. ISSUE11 turns it into a magazine about your life and holds you accountable to one small move a week.
3. **Can I turn my digital vision board or Pinterest saves into a magazine?**
   Yes. Upload your board or your Pinterest saves as images, or pick curated editorial images. Everything you save can become a page.
4. **Does manifestation actually work?**
   No app can promise results. ISSUE11 focuses on what you can control: the process. One small move a week, meditations that rehearse the steps, and a place to keep proof when something shifts.
5. **Does ISSUE11 include manifestation meditations and journaling?**
   Yes. Immersive meditations rehearse the steps toward what you want, and they keep playing with your phone locked. Your journal entries land on a real page of your Issue as you type.
6. **When can I get ISSUE11?**
   A private beta starts on November 11, 2026, with invites for the waitlist. ISSUE11 launches on the App Store for iPhone on December 11, 2026.
7. **How much does ISSUE11 cost?**
   Joining the waitlist is free. The app is $9.99 a month, digital magazine included. The first 1,000 on the waitlist get 50% off their first year. A printed Issue is optional: $29.99 with free standard shipping in the US.
8. **Is there AI in ISSUE11?**
   Yes, and it's optional. The Editor lays out your magazine pages, never rewrites your words, and can be switched off in settings.

Not included on purpose: Android (not announced) and a privacy question (user data needs Megan's explicit approval before we make claims).
