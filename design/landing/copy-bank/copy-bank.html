07How it works (3 steps)

  • 1 · Gather what you want. Pick from curated editorial images, or upload your own. All the life you want, in one place.
  • 2 · Make it yours. Write a few honest lines and pick one small move a week. The Editor lays out the pages. Your words stay exactly as you wrote them.
  • 3 · Your 40-page Issue. Watch it fill, page by page. At 40 pages, keep it in the app or order it printed.
  • Missed a week? Pick up where you left off. There are no streaks to break.
  • What's the life you're making room for? Give it a title and a mood.

11Quotable answers (FAQ, PR, AI search)

  • What is ISSUE11? ISSUE11 is a process-focused manifestation app for iPhone that turns your dreams into a personal magazine and helps you take the small steps that make them real.
  • Does it promise results? No app can promise results. ISSUE11 gives you the process that moves you toward them: one small move a week, and a place to keep proof when something shifts.
  • Is there AI in it? Yes, and it's optional. The Editor lays out your magazine pages, never rewrites your words, and can be switched off in settings.
  • How is it different? A vision board shows what you want, and most get forgotten. A journal holds what you write. ISSUE11 puts both into one magazine about your life and adds one small move a week.
  • Price: $9.99 a month, digital magazine included. Printed Issue $29.99 with free standard shipping in the US (optional).
