P. 08Questions

Asked and answered.

What is ISSUE11?+

ISSUE11 is a process-focused manifestation app for iPhone that turns your dreams into a personal magazine and helps you take the small steps that make them real. You save images, listen to short audio sessions, write in your journal and pick one small move a week, and it all becomes pages in your own 40-page Issue.

When can I get it?+

A private beta starts on November 11, 2026. ISSUE11 launches on the Apple App Store for iPhone on December 11, 2026. Beta invites go to people on the waitlist.

How much does it cost?+

Joining the waitlist is free. The app is a subscription at $9.99 a month, and your digital magazine is included. A printed copy of your finished Issue is optional and costs $29.99 with free standard shipping in the US.

Does ISSUE11 promise results?+

No. ISSUE11 is about the process, not a promised outcome. You can't control the outcome. You are in control of the process: one small move a week, and a place to keep proof when something shifts.

Is there AI in it?+

Yes, and it's optional. Lucky Star is the app's AI helper. It lays out your magazine pages, never rewrites your words, and can be switched off in settings. The app works without it.

How is it different from a vision board or a journal?+

A vision board shows what you want, and most get forgotten. A journal holds what you write. ISSUE11 puts both into one magazine about your life and adds one small move a week, so it's where you start, not where you stop.
