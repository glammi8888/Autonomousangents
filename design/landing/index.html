Inside the app

A magazine about your life. Written by you.

Your Issue

Your dreams, laid out like a magazine

Save images, write a few lines and add proof. They become pages in your own 40-page Issue. Lucky Star lays out each page, and your words stay exactly as you wrote them.

Your pace

One small move a week

Pick one step toward what you want and take it this week. No streaks and no guilt if a week gets away from you.

Your practice

Sessions for the process

Short audio sessions, voice over soft music with visuals. You rehearse the everyday steps toward what you want, like the breath before you walk into the meeting. Then write a line in your journal, straight onto a page of your Issue.

How it works

Four steps. One Issue.

  1. Step 1

    Save

    Collect images of the life you want in My saves. Everything you save can become a page in your Issue.

  2. Step 2

    Write

    Write a few honest lines in your journal. You watch them land on a real magazine page as you type.

  3. Step 3

    Move

    Take your one small move for the week. When something shifts, add proof to your Issue.

  4. Step 4

    Read

    Turn the pages of your Issue like a real magazine and watch it fill, page by page, toward 40.

Questions

Asked and answered.

What is ISSUE11?+

ISSUE11 is a process-focused manifestation app for iPhone. It turns your dreams into a personal magazine and helps you take the small steps that make them real. You save images, listen to short audio sessions, write in your journal and pick one small move a week, and it all becomes pages in your own 40-page Issue.

When can I get it?+

A private beta starts on November 11, 2026, with invites for people on the waitlist. ISSUE11 launches on the Apple App Store for iPhone on December 11, 2026. Join the waitlist to hear first.

How much does it cost?+

Joining the waitlist is free. The app is a subscription at $9.99 a month, and your digital magazine is included. A printed copy of your finished Issue is optional and costs $29.99 with free standard shipping in the US.

Does ISSUE11 promise results?+

No. ISSUE11 is about the process, not a promised outcome. You can't control when something arrives. You can choose one small move this week, and ISSUE11 helps you notice and keep the proof when something shifts.

Is there AI in it?+

Yes, and it is optional. Lucky Star is the app's helper: it lays out your magazine pages and suggests what to do next. Your own words are never rewritten, and you can switch Lucky Star off in settings. The app still works without it.

How is it different from a vision board or a journal?+

A vision board shows what you want and a journal holds what you write. ISSUE11 puts both into one magazine about your own life and adds one small move a week, so your dreams turn into steps you actually take.
