### What repeats [Inference from the table]
1. **The paywall sits right after something personal**: a plan, a pet, her own words, a "with vs without" picture. 10 out of 10.
2. **A commitment moment just before the ask**: Flo's "tap and hold the logo to commit" is the same move as our A17 "Hold to make it yours". Fabulous and Duolingo do versions of it too.
3. **Yearly is preselected and carries the trial.** Monthly, if shown, often has no trial (Calm).
4. **The trial is explained step by step and a reminder is promised**: I am, Motivation, Headspace, Duolingo, Finch.
5. **The yearly price is translated to a smaller unit**: per month (I am, Calm) or per week (Stardust, below).
6. **Trial length is 7 days in our category, 14 days to a month at the top** (Flo, Headspace, Duolingo).

## 2. What the evidence says converts

### Facts (published results, with sources)
| # | What was tested | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hard paywall vs freemium (115k+ apps) | Day-35 download-to-paid **10.7% vs 2.1%**; yearly retention about equal (27% vs 28%) | [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/) |
| 2 | Trial length | Median trial-to-paid **25.5% (≤4 days) · 37.4% (5–9 days) · 42.5% (17–32 days)**; 3-day trials: 55.4% cancel on day 0 vs 39.8% for 7-day | RevenueCat SOSA 2026 |
| 3 | **Stardust** (period-tracking app): added a 7-day trial to the $24.99/yr plan | Paywall conversion **+131%**, proceeds per user **+78%**, paying subscribers +45% | [Superwall case study](https://superwall.com/blog/how-stardust-increased-ios-app-revenue-by-4x-in-six-weeks) |
| 4 | Stardust: yearly shown with **% discount + weekly equivalent** vs "yearly first + More Pricing" vs "yearly only" | Winner: conversion **+50%**, proceeds per user **+62.5%**; **yearly-only and hidden-monthly both lost** | same |
| 5 | Stardust: added a **trial timeline** | Trial starts **+13.6%**, no drop in trial-to-paid | same |
| 6 | **Blinkist**: added a free-trial timeline after exit surveys showed users didn't know when they'd be charged | **+23%** (stated only in the article's link, no detail) | [RevenueCat blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/paywall-tests-grow-app-revenue) |
| 7 | **Mojo**: yearly as the default and only visible plan, monthly behind "view all plans" | Yearly take-up **+15–20%**, slightly more cancellations | same |
| 8 | Mojo: yearly shown as **$4.99/month billed annually** instead of $59.99/year | Revenue per paywall view up "significantly", more in lower-income countries (no number) | same |
| 9 | FitnessAI / Rootd: paywall moved earlier in onboarding | FitnessAI install-to-trial **doubled** (trial-to-paid unchanged); Rootd revenue **5x** | same |
| 10 | Number of plans on a paywall (32M paywall opens) | 2 plans beat 1 by **61%**; 3 beat 2 by **44%** (not controlled for price, trial or default) | [Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/how-many-products-should-you-offer-on-your-paywall) |
| 11 | Multi-page vs single-page onboarding paywalls (40M opens, Feb–May 2026) | **12.41% vs 9.07%** (+37% relative, +3.3 points). Observational, not a randomized test | [Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/new-postmulti-page-onboarding-paywalls-convert-37-better-than-single-page-heres-why) |
| 12 | "Transaction abandon" discount (shown when she starts paying but doesn't confirm) | 17% of revenue in an 18-company cohort, **but Apple called it a source of "unintentional purchases"**; real gain unknown | [Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/17-revenue-boost-with-transaction-abandon-paywalls-a-case-study) |
| 13 | Apple rule (Superwall's account of a conversation with Apple, Jun 3 2026, not an official guideline) | "**Free trial toggles on paywalls are not allowed.** Full stop." The trial must belong to the product and be described on it | [Superwall](https://superwall.com/blog/external-checkout-a-b-testing-and-trial-toggles-confirmed-apples-rules-for-ios) |

### Inference (our reading)
- **Trial timeline + real reminder is the safest win.** It helped in two separate tests (Stardust, Blinkist) and costs nothing in trust. It answers the main fear: "I'll forget and get charged."
- **Show two plans, don't hide monthly.** Hiding monthly raised yearly share at Mojo but lost at Stardust. Two visible plans with yearly preselected is the middle path, and Superwall's data says 2 beat 1.
- **Per-month framing over per-week for us.** Weekly framing won at Stardust, but Megan and the Design Room ruled weekly pricing out for a calm, quarterly brand (Oct 8). The per-month equivalent (Mojo, Calm, I am) gives most of the same effect.
- **Longer trials convert better at the top** (Flo, Headspace 14 days; RevenueCat median rises with length). For ISSUE11, 7 days is the right start because the first Issue and first action land in week one. 14 days is a test.
- **Two-step paywalls look promising, but the evidence is weak** (observational, from the vendor that sells them). Test it; don't build it as the default.
- **Social proof works only when it's real.** The big earners use it (Flo, Stoic, Calm's brand). At launch ISSUE11 has none, so the slot stays empty until it's real.

## 3. ISSUE11 paywall check: row A19

### Keep (the evidence supports it)
- **Headline in her own words** (from A7). No competitor does this on the paywall itself; it is the strongest form of the "personal result before the ask" pattern (commitment & consistency).
- **Cover on top, rest of the Issue softly blurred, "Page 1 of 40 is waiting."** Same job as Flo's "Without vs With" and Aya's "future self", but built from her real cover (endowment + open loop).
- **A17 hold-to-commit before the paywall.** Flo, a ~$8M/mo app, uses the same "tap and hold to commit" right before its paywall.
- **Yearly with 7 days free, preselected + Monthly.** Matches Calm, Finch, Rosebud and the trial-length data.
- **Timeline: Today · Day 5 "we'll remind you" · Day 7 first charge.** Backed by Stardust (+13.6%) and Blinkist (+23%). The Day-5 reminder must really send.
- **Visible Close, Restore, Terms (EULA), Privacy, no countdowns, no fake scarcity, founding rate only while it's really on.** These meet the App Store checklist and keep trust.
- **No trial toggle.** A19 has none. Keep it that way (Apple, fact #13).

### Change (max 3, recommended)
1. **Price framing: add the per-month line under the yearly plan.** E.g. "$39.99 for your first year · about $3.33/month", then "renews at $69.99/year". The billed amount stays the biggest number on the plan. Show "Save X%" only if it's worked out from our real monthly price. Why: Mojo, Calm and I am all do it; it makes the yearly plan feel small without hiding the real charge.
2. **Write the trial in one honest line right under the button, and label Monthly "no free trial".** E.g. "7 days free, then $39.99 for the first year. Auto-renews until you cancel." Use real dates in the timeline (e.g. for a trial started Tue Nov 24: "Today · Sun Nov 29 we'll remind you · Tue Dec 1 first charge") instead of only "Day 5 / Day 7". Why: Apple wants the trial described on the product, the Blinkist exit surveys show the charge date is what people miss, and Calm's "No Free Trial" group makes yearly the easy pick without any trick.
3. **Leave a real-proof slot, empty at launch.** One line under the benefits, filled only with something true: a real beta tester's quote used with her written OK (beta starts Dec 11), or the real App Store rating once there are enough reviews. Why: every top earner shows proof (Flo, Stoic, Calm); a fake one would break Megan's trust rule.

### The 3 first A/B tests after launch (one at a time, judged on revenue per paywall view and refunds, not trial starts alone)
1. **Trial timeline layout: current 3-step timeline vs a bigger, date-based timeline with a reminder bell** (Motivation, Headspace, Duolingo). Lowest risk, two published wins behind it, and it protects reviews.
2. **Trial length: 7 days vs 14 days free on yearly.** RevenueCat's medians and Flo/Headspace point to longer; the risk is waiting longer for revenue. Needs the trial-to-paid number from the first few weeks to judge.
3. **One screen vs two steps.** Step 1 = her words + blurred Issue + 3 benefits ("Your Issue is ready"); step 2 = plans + timeline. Superwall's +37% is observational, so our own test decides. Run it only after test 1 settles, so we don't stack changes.

Not tested at launch (and why): weekly plans (Megan's brand call, refund complaints), a third tier (we have one product), countdowns or "special offer" urgency (Flo, Noom), the transaction-abandon discount (App Review risk). The Close → one offer card (A20) stays as written: only if Megan approves a real offer, shown once.
