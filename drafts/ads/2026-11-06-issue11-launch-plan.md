### The money math (what a customer is worth)
**Assumptions (change any of them and the math changes):**
- Apple keeps 15% (App Store Small Business Program; check that you're enrolled in App Store Connect). If you're not enrolled it's 30%.
- First-year cash only: no renewals, no printed Issues, no taxes or refunds counted.
- Monthly subscribers stay 3 months on average (**my guess**, no data yet).

| Plan | Price | You keep (85%) | Most you can pay for 1 paying customer and still break even in year 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch offer (yearly, first year) | $39.99 | **$33.99** | **$33.99** |
| Yearly (7-day trial) | $59.99 | $50.99 | $50.99 |
| Monthly (no trial) | $9.99 | $8.49/mo | ~$25.47 (if they stay 3 months) |

**What that means per step of the funnel (launch-offer price):**
- **Per waitlist sign-up:** unknown until we see how many waitlist people pay. If 5% pay, break-even is **$1.70** per sign-up. If 10% pay, **$3.40**. If 20% pay, **$6.80**. (Formula: $33.99 × share who pay.) The beta will show us the real share.
- **Per free trial (after launch):** $33.99 × trial-to-paid rate. At 26.2% that's **$8.91** per trial, at 39.9% it's **$13.56**.
- **Per install (Apple Ads):** $33.99 × install-to-trial 13.8% × trial-to-paid 26.2% = **~$1.23**, or ~$1.87 with the 39.9% rate. Reported Apple Ads Health & Fitness installs in the US cost ~$3.77. **So paid installs probably don't pay back in year 1 at the launch price.** They'd need renewals in year 2 or a better funnel. That's why Apple Ads starts small and capped.

**One rule to remember:** an ad is paying for itself in year 1 when one paying customer costs less than **$34** (launch offer) or **$51** (full yearly).

## 5. First A/B test
- **Question:** which hook gets cheaper waitlist sign-ups: **Concept 1 "Your saves, printed"** or **Concept 2 "Your cover story"**? Both are the magazine hero, from two different angles.
- **Setup:** Meta Ads Manager → A/B Test. Same audience, same budget split ($5/day each), same 10 to 15 second Reel format, same caption length, same link. Only the hook and visual change. 14 days.
- **Measure:** cost per waitlist sign-up (checked against the Google Sheet). Tie-breaker: how many people watched past 3 seconds.
- **Call a winner only if:** there are at least ~30 sign-ups across both ads AND Meta shows one ad with ≥80% chance of winning. Otherwise it's "no clear winner": keep both and test a new hook. With ~$140 we can only spot big gaps (around 2x). We won't call small differences.
- **Undo:** pausing either ad stops it right away. Nothing is lost.
