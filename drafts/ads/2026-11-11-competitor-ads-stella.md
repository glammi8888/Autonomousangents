## 1. Stella - Manifest Anything (the one to watch)

**Who:** ✅ App Store: "Stella - Manifest Anything", subtitle "Manifest your dream life", seller Perl Productions LLC, 5.6K ratings at 4.7 (up from 5.2K on Oct 4). Free with in-app purchases from $6.99 to $29.99 (one review mentions a $7 weekly plan). Terms live on downloadstella.com. Facebook Page: "Stella - Manifest Anything", Page ID 1350078938178141.
**What the product promises:** ✅ App Store text: personalized AI audio stories "designed to help your mind experience your goals as if they're already happening", across career, relationships, money and growth.

**Ads:**
- ✅ **6 active ads in the US, and the "all ads" search also returned only those 6.** Stella's Meta spend is small and steady, not a big push.
- ✅ **How long they've run:**
  - 1 ad since **Aug 24** (79 days). This is their likely winner.
  - 4 ads since **Sep 18** (54 days), launched together. 🟡 Most likely one test batch where all 4 survived.
  - 1 ad since **Nov 6** (5 days), a fresh test.
- ⚪ Format, text, CTA and landing page were not returned. 🟡 Likely an App Store install link (there's no web page to sell on: the site only holds the terms).

**Where Stella's growth really comes from:** 🟡 Reported, not verified. Stork.ai and a podcast transcript say the founder already had a big personal audience (reported as 1.2M on Instagram and 2.8M on TikTok). They say she started a new TikTok account for the app (40K+ followers), runs a second high-volume posting account (700K+), and that the app reached about $300K to $340K a month. Those revenue figures are self-reported by the founder. Either way, the point stands: **Stella is mostly an organic creator and TikTok machine. Meta ads are a small side channel.**

## 4. What else shows up for "manifestation" on Meta (category noise, not apps)

✅ The searches for "manifestation app", "manifest app", "law of attraction app", "vision board app", "affirmations app" and "manifestation journal" returned mostly unrelated advertisers, and the ones that were related are not apps:
- Money-spell sellers, for example "All Debts Wiped Thanks To The Money Spell - $17.17" and "I should've kept this ritual a secret - it made me $4,250".
- "Want him back? Do this..." relationship funnels.
- Psychic and astrology pages.
- Printable journals and planners, for example "90% OFF 2027 Witchy Planner".
- Coaching (Mindvalley masterclasses, "Recode Your Wealth Identity").

🟡 **What this means:** in a Meta feed, "manifestation" sits next to a lot of get-rich-quick and spell ads. Women who've seen these ads may already distrust anything that sounds like a promise. That makes our honest, process-first voice stand out more, and it means any promise-sounding line will get lumped in with the scammy ads.

## What this means for ISSUE11

1. **Nobody is running our angle.** Both apps that are advertising (Stella, Future You AI) sell an AI that makes the dream for you, outcome first. Nobody we saw sells "do something about it" or "your own magazine". Hook A (doing over wishing) and Hook B (magazine) both stand apart. Keep both in the A/B test as planned.
2. **The money in this niche comes from organic posting, not Meta.** Stella reportedly makes six figures a month and runs only 6 Meta ads. Keep the $10/day test small, as already planned, and treat organic Reels and TikTok as the main engine. Easiest next step: Megan reposts the winning Reel organically (no extra cost).
3. **Don't echo their outcome language.** Avoid "dream life in 60 seconds", "as if it's already happening", AI-made audio of you, or anything that sounds like a money or love result. That's their ground, and it sits next to the spell ads. Our "Less wishing. One small move a week." is the right contrast.
4. **Hook A needs care in this feed.** "Manifestation doesn't work." stands out among the spell ads, but it can also read as mocking the reader. The kit's second line ("At least not the passive kind.") has to land within 2 seconds, so keep both lines on screen together. Hook B is the safer, more ownable one because no competitor shows a magazine.
5. **Copy one tactic, not the content: weekly batches.** Future You AI launches a fresh set of creatives every week under one headline. After our 14-day test, keep the winning hook as the headline and swap in 2 to 3 new videos a week from Megan's phone footage. No new tools, no extra budget.

**Policy note (applies to all our ads):** no health or healing claims (no anxiety, healing, body or "rewire your brain"). No promised results: no money, love or body outcomes, and no "actually works". Don't name the viewer's beliefs ("you manifest", "you believe"), which Meta's personal-attributes rule covers. **Never mock belief:** the passive method is what failed, never the woman or her faith. Never name or show competitors (Stella, I am, Future You) in our ads. No copying their creative.
