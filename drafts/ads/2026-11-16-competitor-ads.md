## What it tells us (facts only)
- **I am is spending heavily right now.** Over 30 active ads, all launched in the last two days, in a burst of creative testing. Their headline is generic ("You'll love this app!"), so the visuals and the copy inside the ad are what we need to look at.
- **Richfest uses the same angle as ours.** "The Only Manifestation App That Actually Works…", launched today. That is the "actually works" claim our Customer Panel flagged as a turn-off, and it means our "Manifestation doesn't work" hook isn't unique.
- **Free trials are common in ads** (for example "FREE 7-day trial"). Our 7-day trial isn't in our ad or on the homepage.
