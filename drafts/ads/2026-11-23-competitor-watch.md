## Read (facts vs inference)
- Fact: Mindvalley switched to a discount offer (60% off). Inference: Black Friday week. Discounts will be loud in this niche through Dec 1.
- Fact: I am launched a second 30+ ad burst a week after the first. Inference: it refreshes creative weekly at scale; we can't win on volume.
- Fact: Richfest's "actually works" ads are no longer active. Inference: the claim didn't hold up or the test ended. It supports dropping "works" claims.
- Fact: a "personalized magazine" gift advertiser (Magazine Of You) and several print shops are running gift ads now. Matches Megan's TikTok trend data (gifts, Christmas). Nobody pairs it with an app or with your own life.

## Ideas for ISSUE11 (max 3, nothing copied)
1. **Recommended: "your 2027 issue" angle, not a discount.** While everyone shouts % off, show the real magazine (your own photos, the printed copy) with "Make your own personal magazine from the life you want." It's the gap nobody fills and it matches the ASO description. Test when ads start (after launch).
2. Keep the gift angle for Valentine's (Gift issue, after launch). Gift magazine ads exist and run now, so demand is real; ours would be the only one built from an app.
3. Founding price ($39.99 first year) framed as "founding member", never "% off" (Megan's rule), so we don't join the discount noise.
