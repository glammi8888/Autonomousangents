# Keyword bank: hundreds of small wins (started Nov 14, 2026)

**Why (Megan, Nov 14, word for word):** "Also if we can rank for a bunch of smaller searches in volume (like 1000-2000-5000-10000) per month it adds up" · "Especially if we rank for 100´s of them".

**How it works:** one article per cluster. Each article targets a main keyword (1k to 10k US searches a month, KD 30 or lower when we can) plus the small variants around it, so one page can rank for 20 to 100 searches. US data, from our keyword research (Nov 14). Never name our tools in public content.

**Rules for the workers (daily run + 2-hour blocks):**
1. Take the top row marked NEXT for your site, write it, mark it DONE with the date and slug, mark the next row NEXT.
2. Each article: answer in the first 2 sentences, an H2 per question, 4 to 5 FAQs whose schema matches the visible text, 2 to 3 internal links, real references, a cover.
3. ISSUE11: self-help, not therapy. No clinical or money promises. Honest product mentions only ("it's ours"). No founder name.
4. Glammi: no em dashes, no Oxford comma. Link the matching industry page (/aeo-for-med-spas, /aeo-for-jewelers, /aeo-for-hair-salons, /aeo-for-dermatology-clinics, /aeo-for-skincare-brands) when it fits.
5. Seasonal rows (🎄) must be live before Dec 20, so Google has time to index them before the January peak.
