If your camera roll is half screenshots of a life you want, hi.

ISSUE11 gathers all of it into your Issue: a personal 40-page magazine about the life you're creating, with you on the cover. Then it helps you take one small step a week toward it.

ISSUE11 is built on behavior-change psychology. You can't control the outcome. You are in control of the process, and that is where the app puts your attention.

HOW IT WORKS

MY SAVES
Collect what you want your life to look and feel like. Pick from curated editorial images or add your own photos. All the life you want, in one place.

AUDIO
Guided visualizations and affirmations: a calm voice over soft music, with visuals. Each session has you rehearse the everyday choices of future you, like the morning routine, the first message you send, the work you finally share.

JOURNAL
A fresh prompt each day if you want one. Write a few honest lines. Your words stay exactly as you wrote them, and any line you keep can become a page in your Issue.

YOUR ISSUE
The Editor lays out your pages from your saves and your words, in editorial layouts. Pick a layout yourself or let the Editor decide. Your Issue starts on day one and fills page by page until it reaches 40.

ONE SMALL MOVE A WEEK
Each week, choose one small move or write your own. When something you pictured shows up in real life, add it as proof. Proof goes into your Issue, so your magazine becomes a record of what you actually did.

Missed a week? Pick up where you left off. There are no streaks to break.

ABOUT THE EDITOR
The Editor is the AI inside ISSUE11. It stays in the background and acts only when you tap the 11. Everything it makes is a preview until you keep it. You can switch it off in settings and the app still works.

IS IT FOR YOU?
ISSUE11 is for you if you've tried manifesting, you still believe in it, and you're ready to pair the dreaming with small, real steps. Your dreams, your words, your pace. Become YOU.
