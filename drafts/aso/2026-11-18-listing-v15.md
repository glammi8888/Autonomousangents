## 🎯 Big + 50 small keyword map
This is Megan's YouTube framework from Oct 5 ("5 big + 20 small", drafts/aso/aso-recheck-2026-10-04.md), scaled to about 50 small ones. Big head terms go in the strongest fields. Small long-tails are phrases Apple can build from the words in the name + subtitle + keyword field: Apple indexes all three fields (Apple's docs). That it matches multi-word searches across them is the standard ASO understanding, not something Apple spells out. That's why the v15 keyword field holds single connector words (prompts, scrapbook, dream, mood, magazine, scripting, daily, manifesting) instead of phrases.

**How to read the numbers.** Applyra = traffic / difficulty (0–100). ASOMobile = popularity / complexity (0–10), from Megan's Sheet. "not measured" = that tool wasn't asked about the phrase (no ASOMobile tokens were spent today). **Applyra's traffic floor is 8.** A phrase showing 8 has very little measurable demand. It's still worth covering, because it costs no extra characters and has almost no competition, but it's not a volume play.
**Field codes:** N = name · S = subtitle · K = keyword field · **MX ⚠️** = needs a word that only lives in the es-MX listing (law, attraction, loa, digital, self, love, gratitude, subliminal, angel, numbers, 2027, new, year, inspiration, visualization, mindset). That only works if Apple indexes the es-MX listing in the US store (**unverified**) · CPP = custom product page keywords · Later = a future update.
