## The 3 levers that speed it up most after Dec 11
1. **Launch-week install velocity.** Get waitlist downloads and any ads in the same 3–4 days (Dec 11–14). A burst of installs is the strongest early ranking signal (ASO industry experience, not Apple-confirmed).
2. **The first 20–50 ratings in two weeks.** Use Apple's built-in rating prompt right after the cover reveal (Apple allows it max 3 times a year), and ask beta testers to rate honestly on Dec 11. No rewards for ratings (Apple rule).
3. **A metadata update every 2–4 weeks (App Masters).** Keep words ranking 1–3, add weight to words climbing 4–20, swap words stuck past 20. First update ~early January: also swap the seasonal words (2027, new, year).
