You're writing the in-app content for ISSUE11, an iPhone app launching Dec 11, 2026.

**What ISSUE11 is:** "It's like Pinterest meets a habit tracker, except your progress becomes a personalized magazine." You save images you love, write a few lines in a journal, and pick one small action at your own pace (daily, every 3 days, weekly or monthly). Every action and page fills your own 40-page magazine, with your name and photo on the cover. No streaks. Audience: women 18–38 who make vision boards and like manifestation, plus women who just want to make a personal magazine.

**Voice:** warm, editorial, a little witty, like a good magazine editor talking to a friend. Short sentences. Sounds like a real person, never like AI. "A soft voice. A radical mind."

**Hard rules (any line that breaks one gets thrown out):**
- Talk to her as "you". Never "she" or "her".
- Say "action", never "move".
- Never "becoming her" or "become her". OK: "Become YOU", "future you", "your true self", "Become your own dream girl."
- No promised outcomes ("you will get rich", "this will work"), no dates, no specific people.
- No health, medical, therapy or mental-health claims. No faith or religion words.
- No "this is X but should be Y" phrasing. No "it's not X, it's Y". No em dashes.
- If AI is mentioned: "an AI provider", never a brand name.
- Every action must be small, real and doable by one person: under 30 minutes, no money needed, nothing risky.

**Topics:** Love · Money & freedom · My own thing (business/creative) · A home I love · Glow-up · Peace · Travel.

**Write these 4 lists:**
1. **Daily journal questions (60).** One line each, plus a 1-line explanation under it. Mix: future you, what changed, what you noticed, gratitude, small wins. Example style: "What would future you do today?" / "You know, the version of you who's actually doing the things you've been talking about."
2. **First actions per topic (30 per topic = 210).** Each one: the action (max 12 words) + a "smaller version" for days it feels like too much. Example: Money & freedom → "List 3 ways you could earn $100 this month" / smaller: "Write down one skill people already ask you about."
3. **Editor fallback suggestions (15 per topic = 105).** Shown when AI is off or offline: one gentle, specific next step or question, max 20 words. Must never pretend to be personalized AI.
4. **Daily session titles (40).** Names for short audio sessions (visualization, calm, confidence, sleep). Max 6 words. Example: "I am my own dream girl."

**Format:** plain tables, one list per table, numbered. No intro or outro text.
