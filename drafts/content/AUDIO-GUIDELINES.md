## 3. How the words sound (tone)
- **Her best friend with taste, not a guru.** Calm, warm, a little editorial. Never preachy, never hype.
- **Present and process-focused:** rehearse the steps, not the outcome. "You're practicing…", "Notice…", "Imagine walking in…".
- **Believable over big.** If a line would make her think "that's not true", soften it into a process line. "I'm learning to back myself" beats "I am limitless".
- **Specific and sensory:** light, fabric, sounds, small actions. Magazine detail, not abstract energy talk.
- **Short lines with air.** One idea per line, a 3–4 s pause after affirmations, room for the music.
- **Inward focus** (Megan, Oct 15): her life, her taste, her pace; not comparison or living through others.

## 4. Writing rules (every script)
1. Second person for guidance ("you"), first person for affirmations ("I").
2. No promised outcomes: never "you will attract / manifest / get".
3. No health, therapy, sleep, anxiety or body-change claims. No "reprogram your mind", "works while you sleep", "frequency heals".
4. Money lines are about decisions and habits ("I make clear money decisions"), never income promises.
5. Every session ends with one small move or one journal prompt ("Keep a line from this?" card in the player).
6. Avoid: "becoming her" (overused, Megan Oct 7), "girl boss", purple-galaxy words (universe/vibration overload), guilt and homework language.
7. Check every script against design/POSITIONING.md before it reaches Megan (Megan, Oct 10).

## 5. Session formats (unchanged from the recording list, music added)
| Type | Length | Structure | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guided visualization | 10–12 min | arrive → scene → walk through it → identity line → one small action → journal | World A or B |
| Affirmations | 5–6 min | intro → 12–15 lines said twice with pauses → "pick the truest one" | World C or A |
| Subliminal | 10 min | honest spoken intro → 15–20 affirmations mixed **quietly but audibly** under the ambient bed → close | World D |
| Angel numbers "11:11" | 6 min | what it means (a pause to notice, not a prediction) → reflection → 6–8 lines | World C |

**Subliminals, honestly done:** affirmations are always audible if you listen closely, and the full affirmation list shows in the player ("What's underneath"). Nothing hidden, no claims it works without effort. That protects trust and App Review (store copy must not promise results).
