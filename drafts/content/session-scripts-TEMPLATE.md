# Session scripts (musical manifestation sessions) · writing template

Write on your phone (Notes is fine), then paste here or send to OPS. Megan's own words, never rewritten.

|---|---|---|

## Template for each script
- **Title:**
- **Length:** (about 130 spoken words per minute, so 6 min ≈ 600–800 words with music pauses)
- **Opening (30 sec):** settle in: breath, where you are, phone on Do Not Disturb.
- **Imagine (middle):** the scene of the future you, in present tense, with sensory details (what you see, wear, hear, feel).
- **Act (near the end):** one small action that version of you would take this week (ties to Focus).
- **Close (30 sec):** one line to carry, then let the music finish.
- **Music notes:** mood / tempo for the track.

## House rules
- "You", never "she / her". "Action", never "move". No "becoming her".
- No guilt, no urgency, no promises of guaranteed results.
- Present tense, short sentences, room for pauses.
