The other is making your business show up when people ask ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews or Perplexity for a recommendation. The second one is where your next clients are going, so that's what this guide is mostly about.

The Two Meanings of AI SEO

AI for SEO: using AI to research keywords, draft pages, write meta descriptions or audit a site. Useful, fast and easy to overdo.

SEO for AI: getting your business cited and recommended inside AI answers. You'll also hear it called AEO (answer engine optimization) or GEO (generative engine optimization). Same goal, different labels. If you want the longer version, read what AEO is and how GEO compares to SEO.

What Actually Changed in Search

People used to type "med spa near me" and scroll ten blue links. Now many of them ask a full question, like "which med spa in Beverly Hills is good for natural-looking filler?", and read one answer. That answer is assembled from sources the AI trusts: your website, your Google Business Profile, reviews, directories, press and forums.

So the question shifts from "do I rank?" to "does AI know who I am, what I do and why someone should pick me?"

Ranking gets you on the list. Being understood gets you recommended.

What Stays the Same

Google says its AI features draw on the same index and the same quality systems as regular search, and that there's no special markup required to appear in them. That's good news. A fast site, clear pages, real reviews and helpful content still do most of the work. AI SEO builds on SEO.

Five Things to Do First

  1. Say who you are, plainly. Your homepage should state in one sentence what you do, for whom and where. "Medical spa in Beverly Hills for injectables and laser" beats "Where beauty meets science."
  2. Give every service its own page. One page for Botox, one for lip filler, one for laser hair removal. AI can't recommend a service it can't find.
  3. Answer real questions on the page. Price ranges, downtime, who it's for, what to expect. Short FAQs with matching FAQ schema help both people and machines.
  4. Keep your facts consistent everywhere. Same name, address, phone and hours on your site, Google Business Profile, Yelp and directories.
  5. Earn mentions you can't write yourself. Reviews that name a treatment, local press, a podcast interview. AI leans on what others say about you.

Pro tip: ask ChatGPT and Google's AI Mode "who is the best [your service] in [your city]?" once a month and write down who gets named and which sites they cite. That list is your to-do list.

Using AI to Do the Work

AI is great for first drafts, outlines and spotting gaps. Google's guidance is clear that it rewards helpful content however it's made, and penalizes content made mainly to manipulate rankings. In practice: let AI draft, then add what only you know. Your prices, your process, your before-and-after policy, what clients actually ask at the front desk. That part is what makes a page worth citing.

Pro tip: never publish an AI draft with claims you can't back up. Wrong prices or invented results are worse than no page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI SEO?

AI SEO means either using AI tools to do SEO work, or optimizing your business so AI search tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews cite and recommend it. The second is also called AEO or GEO.

Does AI SEO replace regular SEO?

No. AI search draws on the same signals as regular search, like clear pages, a fast site and trusted mentions. AI SEO adds clarity and consistency on top of good SEO.

Do I need special code to show up in AI Overviews?

Google says no special markup is required. Pages that are indexed and eligible for regular search can appear, and structured data like FAQ schema helps machines read your page.

Is AI-written content bad for SEO?

Not by itself. Google rewards helpful content however it's made, but content made mainly to manipulate rankings can be penalized. Edit AI drafts and add what only you know.

How do I know if AI search recommends my business?

Ask the questions your clients ask in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity each month and record whether you're named and which sites are cited. Tracking tools can automate this.
