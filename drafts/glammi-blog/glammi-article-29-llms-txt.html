What llms.txt Is

The idea was proposed in 2024 as a simple, standard place for a site to explain itself to large language models. The format is Markdown: your name as a heading, a one-line summary, a few sentences of context, then lists of links to the pages that matter most, each with a short note. Think of it as the cheat sheet you'd hand a new employee on their first day.

What It Doesn't Do

This part gets oversold, so let's be direct.

  • It isn't a ranking signal. Google has said it doesn't use llms.txt for Search or AI Overviews, and that normal SEO is what matters for its AI features.
  • It doesn't block or allow crawlers. That's robots.txt.
  • It doesn't guarantee citations. Analyses so far haven't found that having the file predicts how often AI tools cite a site.

An llms.txt file is a clear introduction. The pages it points to still have to earn the citation.

Why We Still Add One

It's cheap, it can't hurt and it forces a useful exercise: writing down, in plain words, what your business is and which pages prove it. Some AI agents and developer tools do read it when they fetch a site, and writing it often exposes gaps, like a service you offer that has no page of its own. We keep one on glammi.ai for exactly that reason.

Pro tip: if you can't describe your business in the one-line summary, fix your homepage before you fix anything else. AI tools need that same sentence. Our guide to brand positioning for AI search helps you find it.

A Template You Can Copy

Save this as a plain text file named llms.txt and upload it to the root of your site. Replace everything in the example with your own real details.

# Aurelia Med Spa

> Medical spa in Beverly Hills offering injectables, laser and skin treatments, led by board-certified nurse injectors.

Founded in 2019. Open Tuesday to Saturday. Consultations are free.

## Services
- [Botox and Dysport](https://example.com/botox): pricing, results timeline, who it's for
- [Lip filler](https://example.com/lip-filler): natural-looking results, aftercare
- [Laser hair removal](https://example.com/laser): skin types, number of sessions

## About
- [Our team](https://example.com/team): credentials and training
- [Reviews](https://example.com/reviews): client reviews and before-and-after policy
- [Contact and booking](https://example.com/contact): address, hours, phone

Aurelia Med Spa is a made-up example. Every line in yours should be true and match your website, your Google Business Profile and your directory listings.

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Stuffing keywords. Write it for a smart reader, not a robot from 2009.
  • Linking to pages that don't exist or redirect. Check every link.
  • Letting it go stale. Update it when you add a service, change your hours or rename the business.
  • Treating it as the strategy. Clear service pages, reviews and consistent listings do the heavy lifting. See what AEO is for the full picture.

llms.txt vs robots.txt vs Sitemap

FileWhat It's ForWho Reads It
robots.txtTells crawlers what they may and may not fetchSearch engines and most AI crawlers
sitemap.xmlLists every page you want foundSearch engines
llms.txtExplains your business and points to your best pagesSome AI tools and agents; not used by Google Search

Frequently Asked Questions

What is llms.txt?

llms.txt is a plain text file in Markdown at the root of a website that summarizes the business and links to its most important pages, written for AI tools to read.

Does Google use llms.txt?

Google has said it doesn't use llms.txt for Search or AI Overviews. Regular SEO, like clear pages and good content, is what matters for Google's AI features.

Does my business need an llms.txt file?

You don't need one, but it's cheap to add and can't hurt. The bigger benefit is the exercise of writing down clearly what you do and which pages prove it.

Where do I put llms.txt?

Upload it to the root of your domain so it opens at yourwebsite.com/llms.txt. Most website builders let you add it as a static or custom file.

Is llms.txt the same as robots.txt?

No. robots.txt tells crawlers what they can access, while llms.txt describes your business and points AI tools to your best pages. They do different jobs.
