SEO gets your page into the search results. AEO (answer engine optimization) gets your business named inside the answer itself, when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews who to book. You need both, and most of the work is shared, so the real question is what the other 20% looks like for a clinic or a jeweler.

The Short Version

SEO asks: will Google show my page for this search? AEO asks: when a client asks an AI tool a full question, will it mention me, and will it get my details right? A page can rank on Google and still never be named by ChatGPT. It also works the other way: a business with a thin website but strong reviews and press can be recommended by AI before it ever reaches page one.

You'll also see GEO (generative engine optimization). It's the same goal with a different label. We compared those two in GEO vs SEO.

Where AEO and SEO Are the Same Work

Google says its AI features use the same index and quality systems as regular search, with no special markup required. So the boring basics carry over:

  • A fast site that search engines and AI crawlers can actually read
  • One clear page per service, with real prices or price ranges
  • Content written for the client, by someone who knows the work
  • Links and mentions from sites people already trust

If those are weak, AEO tricks won't save you. Fix them first.

Where They Split

Answers beat pages. AI tools pull one or two sentences, not your whole page. A service page that answers "how long does lip filler last?" in its first line gets quoted. One that opens with three paragraphs about your philosophy gets skipped.

Off-site matters more. When we ask AI tools to recommend med spas in Los Angeles, the sources they cite are often Google Maps listings, Yelp, Instagram, YouTube and "best of" roundups. Your own site is one voice in a crowd. Reviews that name the treatment ("my Morpheus8 with Dr. Lee") give AI something specific to repeat.

Consistency matters more. If your hours, address or founder's name differ between your site, Google Business Profile and Yelp, AI tools hedge or skip you. Classic SEO forgives small mismatches. AI answers repeat them.

You measure it differently. SEO has rankings and clicks. AEO is how often you're named across the questions your clients ask, and whether the details are right.

SEO earns you a spot on the list. AEO decides whether anyone says your name out loud.

One Question, Two Results

Picture a client in Beverly Hills searching for a jeweler to redesign her grandmother's ring.

On Google, she types "custom engagement ring redesign beverly hills" and sees ten links, a map and a few ads. Ranking well here depends on your page, your Google Business Profile and your reviews.

In ChatGPT, she asks "who can redesign an heirloom ring in Beverly Hills without losing the original stones?" She gets two or three names with a sentence on why. Being one of them depends on whether anything online says you do exactly that, in words close to hers. A page titled "Heirloom Ring Redesign" with a short FAQ about keeping the original stones is often the difference.

Pro tip: write down the five questions clients ask at your front desk. Each one deserves a direct answer on your site, in the client's own wording.

What to Do First

  1. Check what AI says about you today. Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Mode the three questions clients ask most. Note who gets named and which sites are cited.
  2. Fix your facts everywhere. Same name, address, phone, hours and founder name on your site, Google Business Profile, Yelp and social profiles.
  3. Put the answer in the first line of every service page, then the details.
  4. Ask for specific reviews. "Mention the treatment if you're comfortable" gets you reviews AI can actually use. Never write or buy them.
  5. Add FAQ schema that matches your on-page FAQs word for word.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between AEO and SEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in search results. AEO helps your business get named and described correctly in AI answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Is AEO replacing SEO?

No. AI search draws on the same signals as regular search, like clear pages, a fast site and trusted mentions. AEO builds on SEO and adds a focus on direct answers, reviews and consistent facts.

Do I need both AEO and SEO?

Yes. Most of the work is shared, so doing SEO well covers much of AEO. The extra AEO work is answering client questions directly and keeping your details consistent across the web.

Is AEO the same as GEO?

They describe the same goal: being cited and recommended in AI-generated answers. AEO stands for answer engine optimization and GEO for generative engine optimization.

How do I measure AEO?

Track a set of questions your clients ask in AI tools each month and record how often your business is named, whether the details are correct and which sources are cited.
