How ChatGPT Picks Who to Recommend

When ChatGPT searches the web, OpenAI says it uses third-party search providers plus content from partners, and its own crawler (OAI-SearchBot) helps surface sites in those results. Then it reads what it finds and writes an answer. So a recommendation depends on two things: whether your pages show up in what it searches, and whether what it reads makes you the obvious fit for the question.

That second part is why reviews, listings and articles about you matter as much as your own website. ChatGPT repeats what several sources agree on.

Google ranks pages. ChatGPT picks names. Make your name the easy pick.

Step 1: Make Sure ChatGPT Can Find You

  • Don't block its crawler. Check your robots.txt. If it blocks OAI-SearchBot, your pages can't be surfaced in ChatGPT search. Most website builders allow it by default.
  • Get indexed by Bing as well as Google. Set up Bing Webmaster Tools (it imports from Google Search Console in a click) and submit your sitemap. Search engines beyond Google feed many AI tools.
  • Keep key info in plain text. Prices, services and location written on the page, not only inside images or PDFs.

Step 2: Say What You Do in One Sentence

Your homepage, Google Business Profile, Instagram bio and directory listings should all say the same thing in plain words: "Medical spa in Beverly Hills for injectables, laser and skin treatments." Not "Where beauty meets science." If ChatGPT can't tell what you do and where, it can't match you to the question.

Pro tip: use the exact same business name, address and phone everywhere. Small differences make AI hedge.

Step 3: Answer the Questions Clients Actually Ask

People ask ChatGPT full questions: "Who's good for natural-looking lip filler in Beverly Hills?" or "Which jeweler can reset my grandmother's diamond?" Give each service its own page, and put the direct answer in the first two sentences: what it is, who it's for, price range, downtime or timeline. Add a short FAQ. Step 4: Get Mentioned Where ChatGPT Looks

When we check which sources AI tools cite for beauty businesses in Los Angeles, the same places keep coming up: Google Maps listings, Yelp, Instagram and YouTube, and "best of" roundups. So:

  1. Complete your Google Business Profile with every service, real photos and your hours.
  2. Ask happy clients for specific reviews. "Mention the treatment if you're comfortable" gets you reviews ChatGPT can repeat. Never write or buy them.
  3. Pitch the roundups. If a local "best med spas" list keeps getting cited, ask to be considered, with real reasons.
  4. Show up on video. Short clips answering client questions give AI another source that says what you do.

Step 5: Check Your Results Every Month

Pick five questions your clients ask. Once a month, ask them in ChatGPT and write down who gets named, how you're described and which sources are cited. That list tells you exactly where to show up next. Results usually move over weeks to months, as new pages and mentions get picked up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rank in ChatGPT?

Make sure ChatGPT search can find your pages, describe your business clearly and the same way everywhere, answer client questions on your service pages and earn mentions on sites it trusts, like Google Maps, Yelp and local roundups.

What is ChatGPT SEO?

ChatGPT SEO is optimizing your business so ChatGPT finds it, understands it and recommends it in answers. It's part of AEO, answer engine optimization.

Can I pay to be recommended by ChatGPT?

Not inside the answer. OpenAI has said that any ads in ChatGPT are labeled and kept separate from answers. Being recommended comes from clear information about your business and trusted sources that mention you.

How long does it take to show up in ChatGPT?

Usually weeks to months. New pages need to be found and indexed, and mentions on other sites take time to build and get picked up.

Should I block ChatGPT from my website?

If you want to be recommended, no. Blocking OAI-SearchBot in robots.txt keeps your pages out of ChatGPT search results, according to OpenAI.
