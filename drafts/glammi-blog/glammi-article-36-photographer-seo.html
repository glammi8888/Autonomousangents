Photographer SEO comes down to one problem: your work is images, but search engines and AI tools mostly read words. Your portfolio can be stunning and still invisible. To get booked from Google and from tools like ChatGPT, give each shoot type its own page, describe your images in text, keep a complete Google Business Profile, collect reviews that say what the shoot was and answer the questions clients ask before they book.

Why Beautiful Portfolios Don't Rank

Most photography sites are a gallery, an About page and a contact form. That works for people who already know your name. It doesn't help someone searching "elopement photographer Big Sur" or asking an AI tool for a brand photographer in Austin who shoots on location, because nothing on the site says those words.

Skip the keyword-stuffed captions. Add the text a client would want anyway: what you shoot, where, what it costs, how it works and what past clients said.

One Page per Shoot Type and Place

If you shoot weddings, elopements and brand sessions, each one gets its own page. If you work in two cities or a few well-known venues, those can have pages too, as long as each one has something real to say.

A strong service page includes:

  • A plain first line: "Wedding and elopement photographer in Big Sur and Carmel."
  • 8 to 15 of your best images from that type of shoot, not the whole archive.
  • What's included, how long it runs and a starting price or range.
  • How booking works, from inquiry to gallery delivery.
  • An FAQ with the questions you answer by email every week.

Add a short story to each gallery or blog post: the couple, the place, the light, what made it work. That text is what search engines and AI tools index.

Image SEO in Plain Words

Google can't fully see a photo the way a client does. Help it with words and speed:

  • File names that describe the image. "big-sur-elopement-bixby-bridge.jpg" beats "DSC_4821.jpg."
  • Alt text that says what's in the shot. "Couple walking on cliffs above the ocean at Bixby Bridge at sunset." Short and literal.
  • Text near the image. A caption or sentence next to it gives Google context.
  • Fast images. Export for the web (often under 500 KB) instead of uploading full-resolution files. Slow galleries lose visitors before they see your best work.

Your Google Business Profile

Even if you travel to clients, a Google Business Profile helps you show up for "photographer near me" searches. Set a service area instead of showing a home address, choose the most specific category Google offers (like "wedding photographer"), list each service and post new work regularly. Google bases local results on relevance, distance and prominence, so a complete, active profile matters.

Reviews and Vendor Links

Ask every client for a review once they've seen their gallery and are excited. Ask them to mention the type of shoot and the place: "our elopement at Point Lobos." Those details help both future clients and the tools that recommend you.

Photographers also have a built-in link source: other vendors. Planners, venues, florists and stylists often feature the photographer when they share a wedding or shoot. Send them your images with credit and ask for a link back to the matching page on your site. Getting featured on a respected wedding or design blog does the same.

Getting Named in AI Answers

When someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI for a photographer, the answer usually draws on Google profiles, review sites, directories, vendor features and photographers' own sites. To be named:

  • Describe your specialty the same way everywhere: website, Google, Instagram bio and directories.
  • Make sure your service pages answer the questions people ask AI ("how much does an elopement photographer cost in Big Sur?").
  • Get listed in the directories and vendor lists couples and brands actually use in your area.
  • Ask AI tools those questions yourself every month and note who gets named and why.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is photographer SEO?

Photographer SEO is making your work findable in Google and AI search by adding the text search engines read: service pages, image file names and alt text, a complete Google profile and reviews.

Should a photographer have a page for each type of shoot?

Yes. A page for weddings, one for elopements and one for brand sessions lets each rank for its own searches and answer the questions that type of client asks.

How do I make my photos show up in Google?

Use descriptive file names, short literal alt text and a caption or sentence near each image. Export images for the web so pages load fast.

How do photographers get recommended by ChatGPT?

By being described clearly and consistently across their site, Google profile, reviews, directories and vendor features, with service pages that answer what clients ask.

How long does photographer SEO take?

Profile and review work can help within weeks. New service pages usually take a few months to rank, depending on how competitive your area is, and no one can guarantee a position.
