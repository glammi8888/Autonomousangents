Plastic surgery SEO works when your website answers the questions a patient asks before booking a consultation: who is the surgeon, what does the procedure involve, what does recovery look like, what does it cost and what do real patients say. Practices that publish those answers clearly, show credentials, keep a complete Google Business Profile and earn detailed reviews are the ones Google ranks and AI tools like ChatGPT name when someone asks for a surgeon in their city.

Why Trust Matters More Here

Google treats health topics as "Your Money or Your Life" content, where a wrong answer can hurt someone. Its quality guidelines put extra weight on experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust for these pages. For a plastic surgery practice, that means named surgeons with verifiable credentials, honest procedure information and real patient feedback count for more than clever copy.

Patients feel the same way. Someone considering a rhinoplasty or a breast procedure reads a lot before they call. The practice that answers more of their questions, more honestly, usually gets the consultation.

One Page per Procedure

A single "Services" page listing twenty procedures can't rank for any of them. Give each core procedure its own page, written or reviewed by the surgeon, that covers:

  • Who the procedure is for, and who it isn't a good fit for.
  • How it's done, in plain language, including anesthesia and time in surgery.
  • A realistic recovery timeline: time off work, when bruising settles, when results are visible.
  • Risks, stated honestly.
  • A cost range or what affects the price, plus whether financing is available.
  • An FAQ built from the questions your coordinators hear every week.

Add a "Reviewed by" line with the surgeon's name and the date the page was last updated. It helps patients and signals expertise.

Make the Surgeon Easy to Verify

Each surgeon needs a real bio page: training, board certification and the board that issued it, hospital privileges, the procedures they focus on and how long they've practiced. Use the same name and credentials on your site, Google profile, directories and social media so patients and search engines can connect them. A short video of the surgeon explaining their approach helps people feel they know them before the consultation.

Before and After Galleries

Galleries are often the most visited pages on a plastic surgery site, but they're usually just images. Add a short description to each case: the procedure, the patient's goal and the time since surgery. Organize galleries by procedure and link each one from its procedure page.

Patient photos are protected health information. Only publish them with the patient's written authorization, follow your privacy and compliance requirements and never use stock photos as if they were your results.

Local SEO and Reviews

Most patients look for a surgeon near them. Complete your Google Business Profile with the right categories, real photos of the practice, accurate hours and each procedure listed as a service. Google bases local results on relevance, distance and prominence, so a full, active profile matters.

For reviews, ask patients once they're happy with their results, and make it easy with a direct link. Reviews that mention the procedure and the surgeon by name ("Dr. Lee did my revision rhinoplasty and explained every step") help future patients far more than "Great experience." Reply to reviews without confirming that the reviewer is a patient or sharing any health details.

Getting Named in AI Answers

AI answers about surgeons usually draw on practice websites, Google profiles, review sites, physician directories and local "best of" articles. To be included:

  • Keep the surgeon's name, credentials and specialties identical everywhere.
  • Make procedure pages answer the questions people ask AI ("how long is recovery from a tummy tuck?").
  • Keep directory listings complete and current.
  • Check what ChatGPT, Google AI and Perplexity say about your practice every month and fix anything wrong or missing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is plastic surgery SEO?

Plastic surgery SEO is making a practice easy to find and trust in Google and AI search through procedure pages, surgeon credentials, a complete Google profile, reviews and well-described galleries.

What should a plastic surgery practice fix first?

A detailed surgeon bio with board certification, then individual pages for the top procedures, then the Google Business Profile and a steady review routine.

Can we post patient before and after photos?

Only with the patient's written authorization and in line with your privacy and compliance rules. Never present stock images as your own results.

How do plastic surgeons get recommended by ChatGPT?

By having consistent, verifiable information across their site, Google profile, directories and reviews, with procedure pages that answer patient questions clearly.

How long does plastic surgery SEO take?

Profile and review work can help within weeks. Procedure pages usually take a few months to rank in competitive cities, and no one can guarantee a position.
