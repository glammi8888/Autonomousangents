Be Known for Something Specific

Patients rarely search for "a plastic surgeon." They search for a surgeon who does natural-looking rhinoplasty, mommy makeovers or facelifts that don't look done. A practice that tries to market twenty procedures equally ends up memorable for none of them. Choose the two or three procedures you most want to grow, then make them the center of your website, your social content and your referral conversations.

That focus also helps search engines and AI tools. When your site, profiles and reviews keep connecting your name with the same procedures, you're easier to recommend for them.

Turn Inquiries Into Consultations

Most practices lose more patients between the first inquiry and the consultation than anywhere else. A few fixes make a big difference:

  • Reply to web and social inquiries within the same business day.
  • Offer virtual consultations for patients who live farther away or want to start privately.
  • Explain what the consultation includes, how long it takes and whether there's a fee that's credited toward surgery.
  • Share a realistic price range or what affects the cost, plus financing options, before patients have to ask.

The Patient Coordinator Is Part of Marketing

For many patients, the coordinator is the practice's voice until the day of surgery. They answer anxious questions, explain the timeline and follow up after the consultation. Give your coordinator a simple routine: a same-day thank-you with the quote and next steps, a personal check-in a week later and a calendar for answering questions over the following months. Patients often take a long time to decide, and the practice that stays helpful without pressure is usually the one they choose.

Social Media Without Overpromising

Instagram and TikTok are where many patients first see a surgeon's work. What tends to build trust:

  • The surgeon explaining procedures and recovery in their own words, on video.
  • Real before and after results, posted only with the patient's written authorization.
  • Honest recovery content: what week one actually looks like, not only the final result.
  • Answers to common questions, like how long swelling lasts or when patients can return to work.

Avoid heavily filtered images, guarantees and anything that pressures people with countdowns or "only this week" pricing on surgery.

Advertising Rules for Cosmetic Surgery

Ad platforms treat cosmetic procedures as a sensitive category. Google and Meta both review healthcare ads and restrict certain claims, images and targeting, and many states have their own rules on medical advertising. Professional bodies also publish advertising standards for their members. Before you run ads, check the current policies, keep claims factual, show results only with consent and send people to a procedure page that answers their questions, not just a form.

Choosing a Plastic Surgery Marketing Agency

An agency helps when your surgical schedule has room but your team has no time for marketing. Look for one that already works with aesthetic medicine, understands patient privacy and medical ad rules, shows real past work and reports on booked consultations rather than clicks or followers. Be cautious with any agency that promises a set number of new patients, guarantees the top spot on Google or wants you to post results without patient consent.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best marketing for plastic surgeons?

Focus on a few procedures, answer cost and recovery questions on your website, follow up with patients after consultations and share consented results on social media. Ads and search work better once those are in place.

How can a plastic surgery practice get more consultations?

Reply to inquiries the same day, offer virtual consultations, explain what the consultation includes and share a realistic price range and financing options up front.

Can plastic surgeons post before and after photos on Instagram?

Only with the patient's written authorization and within your privacy and compliance rules. Avoid filters and never present stock or edited images as real results.

What should a plastic surgery marketing agency do?

It should know aesthetic medicine, respect patient privacy and ad rules, report on booked consultations and never guarantee patient numbers or rankings.

How do plastic surgeons show up in ChatGPT answers?

AI tools tend to name surgeons whose credentials, procedures and reviews are described clearly and the same way across their website, Google profile and medical directories.
