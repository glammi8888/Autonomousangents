## Website card (draft copy)

**Two ways to get found in AI search**

**Option 1: Start now, save more**
$499/month from day one. 3-month minimum.
The lower monthly price, because you're in from the start.

**Option 2: Pay when AI names you**
$0 until you show up. Then $899/month.
We agree on 10 real client questions together. You start paying the month ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI names your business in at least 3 of them.

Both include: AI visibility audit, website and listing fixes, content built for AI answers, and a monthly report with screenshots of what AI says about you.

## Definitions (contract)
- **Tracked questions:** 10, chosen together before work starts; swaps only by mutual agreement.
- **AI tools:** ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode (US, logged-out).
- **"Shows up":** the client is named in an answer to at least 3 of the 10 questions in one monthly check, with screenshots and the tracking report as proof.
- **Billing (Option 2):** starts the first month the bar is met; then monthly, 3-month minimum from that point, 30 days' notice after.
- **Window:** if the bar isn't met within 120 days, either side can end with nothing owed, or switch to Option 1.
- **Client's part:** create or give access to Google Business Profile, Yelp and site, and ask real clients for reviews. If the client doesn't do these, the 120-day window pauses.
- **No promises beyond the bar:** we never promise #1 or permanent mentions; AI answers change.

## Recommendation notes
- $899 vs $499 = the client pays about 80% more for removing their risk, and Glammi earns more for carrying it.
- Option 2 is for clients with a real chance in 120 days (some reviews, a real location or product). Brand-new businesses get Option 1.
- Optional safety net: a $250 setup fee on Option 2 to cover the audit. Dropping it makes the headline stronger ("$0 until you show up"); keeping it protects Megan's time. Megan decides.
