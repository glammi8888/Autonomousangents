## 1. What this agreement covers
Glammi helps your business become easier for AI search tools (like ChatGPT, Google's AI answers, Gemini and Perplexity) and regular search engines to understand and recommend. This is called AEO (answer engine optimization) and GEO (generative engine optimization). Schedule A describes the AEO/GEO Essentials work. Schedule B applies only if you also choose a Glammi Site.

## 2. Scope and deliverables: AEO/GEO Essentials
**Schedule A: what's included each month**
1. **Baseline AI-visibility audit (month 1).** We ask AI tools a set of questions your future clients are likely to ask (agreed with you at kickoff, up to [NUMBER] questions), record which businesses come up and what is said about you, and review your business listings and website structure. You get a written baseline report.
2. **Business information fixes.** We check that your name, address, phone, hours and services match across your Google Business Profile, website and [NUMBER] key listings. We make the fixes we have access to and send you exact instructions for the ones only you can make.
3. **Site structure for search.** We add or suggest structured data (schema markup) and clear service and FAQ content so search engines and AI tools can read your site. If we don't have edit access, we send the code and steps for your web person.
4. **Monthly answer content.** [NUMBER — not set] articles or FAQ answers per month, each answering one real question about a service you actually offer. Sources are cited. Each article ends with "Written by: Glammi Studio" and a link to glammi.ai, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
5. **Monthly re-audit and report.** We repeat the baseline questions each month and send a plain-English report on what changed and what we're working on next.

**Limits (so we both know what "included" means)**
- **Revisions:** up to [NUMBER — proposed 2] rounds of changes per article or deliverable. Extra rounds or new requests are quoted first.
- **Response times:** we reply to emails within [NUMBER — proposed 2] business days ([BUSINESS HOURS AND TIME ZONE]). Business days exclude weekends and US federal holidays.
- **Not included:** paid ads, social media management, review responses, photography or video, booking or CRM setup, third-party tool costs, integrations, or work on other websites or locations. We can quote these separately.
- **Your approval comes first:** nothing goes live under your name until you (or your claims approver) approve it in writing. Email counts.

## 5. No guarantees
No one controls what Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or any other search or AI tool shows. **Glammi does not guarantee any ranking, AI mention, citation, recommendation, traffic, leads, bookings or revenue.** We do the agreed work, measure what happens and report it honestly. AI tools change often and can give different answers to the same question, so results can go up and down.

## 6. Your responsibilities
- **Accurate information.** You give us correct details about your business, services, providers and prices, and tell us when something changes.
- **Medical and treatment claims.** You (through your claims approver) review and approve every statement about treatments, products, results, safety, providers or credentials before it's published. You're responsible for making sure published content follows FTC advertising rules, your state medical board's advertising rules and any other rules for your profession (for example FDA rules about how products and devices are described). We'll flag anything that looks risky, but we're not your legal or medical compliance advisor.
- **Before/after photos and patient content.** You only give us before/after photos, testimonials or patient stories if you have documented, written consent from each patient for that specific use. You keep those consent records. We won't publish any of this without your written confirmation that consent exists.
- **No health information through Glammi.** Don't send us patient health information, and don't use any Glammi-built form to collect it. Glammi forms are for general inquiries only (like name, email and preferred service). Anything about a patient's health, history or treatment must go through your own HIPAA-compliant booking or intake tool. If health information reaches us by mistake, we'll tell you and delete it.
- **Access.** You give us the access in Section 9 in a reasonable time. Delays on your side may delay deliverables; the monthly fee still applies.
- **Your materials.** You confirm you have the rights to any logos, photos, text or other materials you give us.
