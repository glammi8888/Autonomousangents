# Part 1 · Welcome to Glammi Studio

You're in. Thank you for trusting us with how AI talks about your business.

Here's the short version of what happens next. We find out what ChatGPT, Google's AI answers, Gemini and Perplexity say about you today. We fix the things they rely on. Then every month we check again and tell you what changed. You approve anything that goes out under your name.

One honest note before we start: nobody can promise a spot in an AI answer. So we don't promise. We measure, we fix and we show you the numbers.

## Your first 30 days

**Day 1 · Signed and set up**
Your agreement is signed and your first payment is through. You'll get a short email from us with a link to book your kickoff call and a link to your intake form.

**Week 1 · Kickoff call and intake**
A [30]-minute video call. We'll agree on the questions your future clients are likely to ask AI (things like "best lip filler near me" or "who sells lab-grown engagement rings in [your area]"). Fill in the intake form before the call if you can. It takes about [15] minutes.

**Week 1 · Access invites**
We'll send you simple steps to add us to your accounts. Invites only. We'll never ask for your passwords (see "Access we'll ask for" below).

**Week 2 · Your baseline AI-visibility check**
We ask AI tools your agreed questions and record who shows up, what they say about you and where their information comes from. We also check your listings and how readable your website is for search engines. You get a short report: where you stand today and the first fixes we'd make.

**Week 3 · First fixes**
We fix what we can with the access you gave us (like your hours, services and business details) and add structure to your website so search engines can read it. Anything only you can change, we send as exact steps. Your first articles arrive in your inbox for approval.

**Week 4 · First articles go live and your report date is set**
Once you approve them, your first articles go live. We set a monthly report date, and from then on you get one clear report each month: what AI says now, what we changed and what's next.

Changes in AI answers usually take time to show up, and some months move more than others. We'll tell you plainly either way.

## What your intake form asks
- Your business name, address, phone and hours exactly as they appear on Google
- Your website and the booking tool you use
- The services or collections you actually offer, and the 3 you most want more clients for
- Your providers or team members as you want them named, with credentials
- The questions clients ask you most, in their own words
- The neighborhoods or cities you serve
- 3 businesses you'd like to be mentioned alongside
- Words you love and words you never want used about your brand
- Who approves medical, treatment or product claims (name and role)
- Any listings, directories or other marketing companies that already manage your profiles

## Access we'll ask for
We always ask for the lowest access level that does the job.

| Account | What to give us | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Add us as a **Manager** (not Owner) | To fix your business info, services and hours |
| Your website | An **editor or collaborator invite** | To add structure and publish approved articles |
| Google Search Console | Add us as a **user** (Full or Restricted), not owner | To see how search engines read your site |
| Google Analytics (optional) | **Viewer** access | To include website visits in your report |
| Yelp, Apple Business Connect and others | Usually nothing. We'll send you the exact changes to make | Many of these only allow owners to edit |
| Your domain | Nothing unless you have a Glammi Site. We'll walk you through any change on a call | Your domain stays in your name |

We don't need your passwords, your booking software, your payment details or any patient or customer records.

## What we need from you
- The intake form, before your kickoff call if possible
- The access invites in week 1
- Approvals within [3] business days, so your articles don't wait
- A heads-up when something changes (new service, new hours, new provider, new location)
- For med spas and clinics: your claims approver signs off on anything about treatments, results or safety

## How to reach us
Email **megan@glammi.ai**. We reply within [2] business days ([hours and time zone]). Want to talk it through? Ask in your email and we'll send a time.

## What we never do
- Ask for your passwords
- Collect patient health information. Our forms are for general questions only, and anything about a patient's health goes through your own HIPAA-compliant booking or intake tool
- Publish anything under your name before you approve it
- Make medical or treatment claims you haven't signed off on
- Post before/after photos without your documented patient consent
- Write, buy or ask for fake reviews
- Promise rankings or AI mentions
- Name you as a client or share your results without your written OK
- Contact your clients or patients

We're glad you're here.
**Glammi Studio** · glammi.ai · megan@glammi.ai
