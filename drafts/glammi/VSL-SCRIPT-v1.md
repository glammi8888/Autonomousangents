## Notes
- **Why this CTA:** "Free AI visibility check" ties the ChatGPT hook to a concrete thing she gets on the call (you show her real AI answers about her clinic). It works whether she ends up buying a website or AEO.
- **No prices in the video** until the pricing questions are decided (Oct 26 evaluation). Price comes on the call.
- **Claims check:** "more people are asking AI" is a trend statement, not a number. Keep it that way unless you cite a source. No "rank #1", no "guaranteed".
- **Proof:** if you don't have a client site yet, show the Glammi template + one real published article, and say "here's what we build", not "here's a client".
- **Length:** ~300 words ≈ 2:00–2:15 at a relaxed pace.
