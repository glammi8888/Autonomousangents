## Why this works
- **One idea all the way through:** AI gives a short list → we help you get on it → see where you stand for free.
- **The "no guarantee" line builds trust.** Med spa owners have heard big promises before; honesty makes you the safe choice (and matches Glammi's own articles).
- **The website is the backup offer,** mentioned once ("if your website is holding you back"), so website-only leads still raise their hand on the call.
