# Amber Grant: what this month's winners have in common (for the Oct 31 re-application)
🩶 Manager, Oct 22, 2026. Source: the winner write-ups Megan pasted (Ebijou, The Caregiving Concierge, CalcMindset Innovations). Pattern read, not a guarantee of what judges score.

## The pattern
1. **One specific origin moment.** Ebijou: a stranger in an airport offered to buy her necklace. Caregiving Concierge: her own juggling of job, family and a loved one's care.
2. **Real proof of demand.** Sold out at shows; professors watched capable students struggle; "other caregivers must be struggling too".
3. **The founder did the hard thing herself.** Kristin had no software training, taught herself and built a working platform in 6 months while working and parenting. (Closest match to Megan: non-developer founder who designed and built ISSUE11 with AI tools.)
4. **A concrete next step the money moves forward.** An atelier; a launch; reaching more educators.
5. **Learning from customers, not the default path.** Ebijou chose in-person because customers wanted to touch the textiles.
6. **A bigger "who it helps".** Women and underrepresented groups in math; caregivers.

## What this means for ISSUE11's application
- Lead with **Megan's own moment** that started ISSUE11 (her words, only what she chooses to share).
- Show the **builder story** honestly: full V1 design, locked onboarding, a working app preview in Replit, built by a non-developer founder with AI tools.
- **Proof of demand: be honest.** Use only real numbers (waitlist sign-ups, beta testers, Instagram response) or say "launching Dec 11, private beta Nov 11". No invented stats.
- **Name exactly what $10K funds**, tied to launch (examples to confirm with Megan: print proof copies via Lulu, licensed music for audio sessions, beta testing, launch marketing).
- **The bigger why:** women who can picture the life they want but struggle to take the next step; process over outcome (design/POSITIONING.md).
- **One "learned from users" line**, e.g. from the Reddit research: people don't need more inspiration, they need the path to feel possible.
