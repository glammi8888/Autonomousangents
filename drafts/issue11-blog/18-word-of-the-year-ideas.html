What Is a Word of the Year?

It's a one-word intention instead of a list of resolutions. Resolutions tell you what to do. A word tells you who to be while you do it. When you're stuck on a choice in June, you ask: what would the Brave version of me do here? The idea was popularized by the book One Word That Will Change Your Life by Jon Gordon, Dan Britton and Jimmy Page, and it works because one word is easy to remember on a bad day.

How to Choose Your Word of the Year

Answer three questions in a notebook, quickly, without editing:

  1. What did this year teach you? Write the hard part honestly.
  2. How do you want to feel next December? One feeling, not an achievement.
  3. What gets in your way most often? Fear, overthinking, people pleasing, no time.

Your word usually sits between answer 2 and answer 3: the feeling you want, aimed at the thing that blocks it. If you want to feel proud and you keep waiting for permission, your word might be Claim. Circle three candidates, sleep on it, and pick the one that makes you slightly nervous.

How to Actually Live Your Word

  • Put it where you'll see it daily. Phone lock screen, mirror, the first page of your journal.
  • Give it one small move a week. "Rest" becomes "phone in the kitchen by 10pm on Sundays."
  • Use it as a filter. Before you say yes to something, ask whether it fits your word.
  • Check in every season. In March, June and September, write one line on where your word showed up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a word of the year?

A word of the year is one word you choose to guide your next twelve months, like Brave, Rest or Home. You use it as a filter for decisions and a reminder of who you want to be, instead of a list of resolutions.

How do I choose my word of the year?

Write down what this year taught you, how you want to feel next December and what usually gets in your way. Your word often sits between the feeling you want and the thing that blocks it. Pick three candidates and choose the one that makes you slightly nervous.

What are good words of the year?

Popular choices include Brave, Rest, Become, Abundance, Open, Strong, Play and Enough. The best word is the one that fits what you want more of this year, not the prettiest one.

Can I have more than one word of the year?

You can, but one works best because it's easier to remember on a hard day. If you need more, pick one main word and one support word, like Brave and Rest.

How do I use my word of the year every day?

Put it where you'll see it daily, give it one small action each week and ask whether new commitments fit it before you say yes. Check in every few months on where it showed up.
