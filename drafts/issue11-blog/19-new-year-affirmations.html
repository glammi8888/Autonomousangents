Do New Year Affirmations Work?

They can, with one catch. A 2016 brain-imaging study found that affirming your values activated reward and self-related areas of the brain, especially when people thought about the future. But a well-known 2009 study found that people with low self-esteem felt worse after repeating "I am a lovable person," because their mind argued back. So the affirmations that work are believable, about values or actions, and a small step ahead of where you are, not a leap you can't buy.

How to Write Your Own

  • Make it believable. If "I am rich" makes you wince, try "I'm learning to handle money with calm."
  • Use "I am becoming" or "I'm learning". They give your brain room to agree.
  • Tie it to an action. "I keep the promises I make to myself" points at something you can actually do this week.
  • Keep it short. If you can't remember it on a bad day, it's too long.

How to Use Them So They Stick

  1. Choose three, not fifty. One for who you're becoming, one for what you want, one for a hard day.
  2. Pair each with a small move. "I ask for what my work is worth" plus "update my rates by Friday."
  3. Put them where your eyes already go. Lock screen, mirror, the top of your new year vision board.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are new year affirmations?

New year affirmations are short, positive, present-tense sentences you repeat to set the tone for the coming year, such as "I am becoming someone I'm proud of." They work best when they feel believable and are paired with a small action.

Do affirmations actually work?

Research is mixed. Affirming your values has been linked to activity in the brain's reward and self-related areas, but very positive statements can make people with low self-esteem feel worse. Believable affirmations tied to actions tend to work better than big claims.

How many affirmations should I use?

Three is plenty: one about who you're becoming, one about what you want and one for hard days. Fewer affirmations are easier to remember and repeat.

When should I say my affirmations?

Morning works well because it sets the tone for the day. Many people say or write them while getting ready, or keep them on their phone lock screen so they see them often.

What is a good affirmation for the new year?

A good new year affirmation is believable and points forward, like "Every week this year, I move one thing forward" or "I'm allowed to begin again, as many times as I need."
