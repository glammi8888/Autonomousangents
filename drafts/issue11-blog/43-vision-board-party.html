What Is a Vision Board Party?

It's a night where everyone brings images, words and ideas about the life they want, then builds a board together. Making it in a group helps: you get ideas from each other, you say your goals out loud and you have people who remember them. Most parties happen between mid-December and the end of January, when everyone is already thinking about the new year.

Vision Board Party Supplies

Keep it simple. Here's what to have on the table:

  • Boards: poster board, cork boards or foam board, one per guest.
  • Magazines: a stack of 15 to 20 for a group of six. Ask guests to bring two each. Our guide to the best magazines for vision boards shows which ones work.
  • Printed images: ask everyone to print five to ten of their own saves from Pinterest or their camera roll.
  • Tools: scissors, glue sticks, double-sided tape, washi tape and markers.
  • Words: letter stickers or printed quotes. Our vision board quotes are ready to print.
  • Extras: a speaker, good light and a clear table for each person.

Pro tip: put scissors and glue in the middle of the table, not at each seat. People share more when they have to pass things around.

How to Host a Vision Board Party (3-Hour Agenda)

  1. Arrive and settle in (20 minutes). Drinks, snacks, music. Let people browse the magazine pile.
  2. Reflect (20 minutes). Hand out three questions: what went well this year, what do I want to feel next year and what am I ready to let go of? Everyone writes quietly.
  3. Pick a word (10 minutes). Each guest chooses one word for the year. Need help? Share a list of word of the year ideas.
  4. Cut and collect (40 minutes). Pull every image and phrase that pulls at you. Don't glue yet.
  5. Build the board (40 minutes). Edit down, arrange and glue. Put your word in the middle.
  6. Share (30 minutes). Each person shows their board and names one thing on it they'll start this month.
  7. Close (20 minutes). Group photo with the boards, then everyone writes their first small step on a card to take home.

Vision Board Party Invitation Wording

Copy one of these and change the details:

  • Cozy: "Come make your 2027 vision board with us. Bring two magazines, five printed photos of the life you want and your favorite snack. Saturday, January 10, 6 pm."
  • Short: "Vision board night. Snacks, scissors, big plans. Saturday at 6."
  • Glam: "Your next chapter starts here. Join us for a vision board soirée: dress cute, bring your dreams, we'll bring the glue."

15 Vision Board Party Ideas

Themes

  • Pajama party with face masks and hot chocolate.
  • Brunch board party with mimosas or mocktails.
  • All-pink or all-white dress code for the photos.
  • "Main character" theme: everyone dresses as their 2027 self.
  • Galentine's vision board night in February.

Games and Activities

  • Guess whose board: mix the finished boards up and guess who made which.
  • Affirmation jar: everyone writes one affirmation for the person on their left. Our new year affirmations help if anyone gets stuck.
  • Magazine swap: trade the images you cut but don't need.
  • Accountability pairs: pair up and agree to check in on February 1.
  • Letter to future you: seal it and open it next December.

Food and Drinks

  • A grazing board, so no one has to stop and eat a full meal.
  • Finger food that won't get glue or grease on the magazines.
  • A signature mocktail named after the year's theme.
  • Mini desserts on a separate table, away from the boards.
  • Tea and coffee for the final sharing round.

How to Host a Virtual Vision Board Party

Can't get everyone in one room? Run it on a video call. Send supplies or a list a week before, keep the same agenda, and share boards on camera at the end. Guests can build a digital board instead of a paper one. Our Pinterest vision board guide shows how to start from your saves.

What to Do After the Party

Most boards go on a wall and stop working by March. Keep yours alive:

  • Hang it where you see it every morning.
  • Take a photo and set it as your phone's lock screen.
  • Pick one image and do one small thing for it each week.
  • Check in with your accountability partner on the first of each month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vision board party?

A vision board party is a get-together where friends make vision boards side by side, usually around the new year, then share their goals for the year ahead.

What do you bring to a vision board party?

Bring two or three magazines, five to ten printed photos of the life you want and a snack to share. The host usually provides boards, scissors, glue and markers.

How long does a vision board party last?

About three hours: time to arrive, reflect, cut and collect images, build the board and share it with the group.

When should you have a vision board party?

Most people host one between mid-December and the end of January, while everyone is planning the new year. Any time you want a fresh start works.

Can you have a vision board party online?

Yes. Share a supply list ahead of time, follow the same agenda on a video call and show your boards on camera at the end. Guests can make paper or digital boards.
