Read this first

Hi, it's Megan. I made this because I had the same problem as you: a phone full of screenshots of a life I wanted, and a journal full of "one day."

Writing down what you want is a good start, but it's only the start. What changed things for me was asking a second question every time: okay, so what's my next move? That's what this pack is built around.

How to use it

Once a week, give yourself ten minutes. Pick one prompt from each section: Want, Become, Move, Proof. Write by hand, in your notes app, wherever you'll actually do it.

End every session with one move. Something small enough to do this week, in under an hour.

Use the AI prompts when you're stuck. They're written so the AI asks you questions. Your answers stay yours.

There's no wrong way to do this. Skip prompts that don't land. Come back to the ones that sting a little, they're usually the important ones.

P. 03 · Journal prompts

Want & Become

Want: get honest about it

  1. If no one would judge me, what would I admit I want?
  2. Describe one ordinary Tuesday in the life you want. Morning to night.
  3. What have I saved or screenshotted the most this year? What's it trying to tell me?
  4. Which dream have I been calling "one day" for way too long?
  5. What would actually feel like enough?
  6. Whose life do I keep watching? Which part of it is really mine to want?
  7. How do I want to feel when I wake up a year from now?

Become: meet her

  1. What does the version of me who has this do on a normal day?
  2. Which habit does she keep that I keep skipping?
  3. What would she stop saying yes to?
  4. How does she talk to herself when something goes wrong?
  5. Where does she put her money, time and attention?
  6. What's one small promise I can keep this week to get closer to her?
  7. Which belief about myself am I ready to let go of?

P. 04 · Journal prompts

Move & Proof

Move: make "one day" this week

  1. What's the smallest step toward this that I could take in 20 minutes?
  2. When and where exactly will I do it? ("Saturday, 10am, at my desk, I will…")
  3. What's most likely to get in the way? What will I do if it does?
  4. Who could I ask for help, an intro or advice this week?
  5. What can I take off my plate to make room for this?
  6. If I only did one thing this week, what would matter most?
  7. How could I make this step feel exciting instead of heavy?

Proof: notice what's changing

  1. What did I do this week that the old me wouldn't have?
  2. What's changed, even a little, since I started?
  3. What did I notice this week that I would have missed before?
  4. Which yes, reply or small win surprised me?
  5. What did the step that didn't work teach me?
  6. How do I feel about this dream now compared with a month ago?
  7. What am I proud of that nobody else saw?
  8. What's the next page of this story?
  9. If I read this a year from now, what do I hope I'll have done?

P. 05 · AI prompts

When the page
stays blank

Copy one of these into ChatGPT or any AI assistant, then paste in what you wrote. One rule: let the AI ask the questions. You write the answers.

  1. "Here's what I wrote about the life I want. Ask me five questions that would make it more specific. Don't rewrite it."For when your dream still feels foggy.
  2. "Based on this, give me three small steps I could take this week, each under 30 minutes."For when you know what you want but not where to start.
  3. "What obstacle am I not mentioning? Ask me about it gently."For the thing you keep writing around.
  4. "Help me plan the next four weeks with one step per week. Keep it realistic for a busy person."For turning a big dream into a month.
  5. "Read my last few entries and point out progress I haven't noticed."For the weeks when it feels like nothing's moving.
  6. "Ask me what the version of me who already has this would do tomorrow morning."For getting out of your head and into your day.
  7. "I keep putting off [step]. Ask me three questions about why, without judging me."For honest procrastination check-ins.
  8. "Give me five journal prompts about [dream] that focus on what I can control."For making your own prompts when these run out.
  9. "Here's my week. Where could I fit 20 minutes for my dream?"For when "I don't have time" is the story.
  10. "Summarize what I've learned this month in three sentences, using my own words where you can."For closing the month with clarity.

Keep private details out of AI chats: full names, addresses, health info, account numbers. Check your assistant's privacy settings.
