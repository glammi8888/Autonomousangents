Editor’s letterThe Rebrand Issue

Rebranding your entire life? Start on one page.

You don’t need a 90-day plan. You need one line, written down, and one small action. Then rest. Every prompt in this issue is built on a real behavior-change method, the kind psychologists study, written in your words.

Paste a prompt into ChatGPT. It asks, you answer, and the answers stay yours. Why now? People start goals more often at new beginnings, like a new year. Psychologists call it the fresh start effect. 2027 is yours.
