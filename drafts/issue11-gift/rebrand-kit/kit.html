Part 2 · 30 ChatGPT prompts

30 ChatGPT prompts to become your best self.

Each one uses a real behavior-change method. Copy, paste, fill the [brackets]. Let it ask. You answer.

1 · See it
01“It’s December 31, 2027 and my year went as well as it realistically could. Interview me about it, one question at a time: work, money, home, body, people. Don’t answer for me.”Best Possible Self: one of the most studied exercises in positive psychology. Writing it makes goals clearer.
02“2027 is my fresh start. Ask me what I’m leaving behind in 2026, then help me name my 2027 era in three words.”The fresh start effect: people start goals more often at new beginnings, like a new year.
03“Give me 30 values. I’ll pick my top five. Then ask where each one shows up in my week, and where it doesn’t.”Goals tied to your own values last longer than goals you think you should have.
04“Here’s my 2027 list: [paste]. For each line, ask what I’d see, hear or have if it came true. Make it specific enough to photograph.”A clear picture tells your attention what to look for. Vague dreams stay vague.
05“Whose life do I check on the most? Ask me to name the one thing I envy, then help me turn it into a goal that’s mine.”Envy is information: it points at a want you haven’t said out loud yet.
2 · Say it out loud
06“Use the WOOP method with me for [wish]: ask for my Wish, the best Outcome, my main inner Obstacle, then help me write a Plan.”WOOP (mental contrasting) beats positive thinking alone: you dream, then plan for what gets in the way.
07“What inner obstacle (a habit, a fear, a story) is most likely to stop me from [goal]? Keep asking until we find the real one.”Naming the obstacle in advance is what turns a wish into a plan.
08“Help me write the money sentence I’m scared to say: the number I want to earn or save in 2027, and why. Then ask what makes it scary.”A number makes a goal concrete, and concrete goals get more effort.
09“I want my business popping OFF in 2027. Ask me questions until we find the one offer to focus on first, and who it’s for.”One focus beats five half-started plans. Fewer goals, more follow-through.
10“Rate how much I believe each line on my 2027 list from 1 to 10: [paste]. For every line under 7, ask what would make it a 7.”Self-efficacy (belief you can do it) grows in small steps, not one leap.

3 · Become it
11“Act as me in December 2027. I’ll ask how I got there. Answer as me, using these details: [paste].”Future self-continuity: feeling close to future you makes it easier to choose for future you today.
12“Write a letter from me in December 2027 to me today, from these details: [paste]. Then ask me which line I want to keep.”A letter from future you makes the future feel real and near.
13“Help me finish this sentence 10 ways, from my 2027 list: “I’m the kind of woman who…” Keep each one true enough to believe today.”Identity-based habits: every small action is a vote for who you’re becoming.
14“Here are words from my Pinterest boards: [paste]. Define my 2027 signature look in five pieces, and one I can wear this week.”Enclothed cognition: what you wear can shift how you think and act.
15“What would looking hotter in 2027 mean for me, based on this: [X]? Make it about how I want to feel, then suggest one habit.”Feeling-based goals stay kinder and last longer than number-only goals.
4 · Make it small
16“Shrink [goal] into a habit so small I can do it in under two minutes, even on my worst day.”Tiny habits work because they don’t depend on motivation.
17“Turn [goal] into three if-then plans: “If [situation], then I will [action].””Implementation intentions: if-then plans make follow-through much more likely.
18“Here’s my normal morning: [paste]. Attach one 2027 habit to something I already do every day.”Habit stacking: an old habit becomes the reminder for a new one.
19“Pair one thing I avoid [task] with one thing I love [treat], so I only get the treat while doing it.”Temptation bundling makes the hard thing come with a reward built in.
20“Build me a 5-minute morning for my 2027 glow up that fits a full-time job. No 5am alarm, no cold plunge.”Routines that fit real life are the ones you keep.

Part 2 · 30 ChatGPT prompts
5 · Protect it
21“I’m too grown to keep explaining myself. Help me write five short, kind ways to say no.”Ready-made words lower the cost of a boundary in the moment.
22“Help me write a text to set a boundary with [person], without a 3-paragraph apology.”Short and kind is clearer, and clearer is easier to keep.
23“Here’s what my inner critic says: [paste]. Ask me to check the evidence: what’s true, what’s old, what a friend would say.”Questioning a thought (from CBT) loosens its grip.
24“I missed [habit] again. Talk to me like a kind friend would, then help me plan the next small step.”Self-compassion helps people restart faster than self-criticism.
25“Ask me who in my circle gives me energy and who takes it. Then help me plan more time with the first group.”The people around you shape your habits more than willpower does.
6 · Keep it
26“Every Sunday I’ll paste my week. Ask what moved me closer to my 2027 list. Name the small wins first.”The progress principle: noticing small wins is one of the strongest everyday motivators.
27“Help me create a 10-second celebration I do right after each new habit.”A good feeling right after an action helps the habit stick.
28“Imagine it’s June 2027 and I gave up on [goal]. Ask me what went wrong, then help me plan around it now.”A pre-mortem spots problems before they happen.
29“Ask me what I’m proud of this year. Keep asking until I have 10 things.”Remembering past wins builds belief for the next one.
30“Read everything I wrote today and write five cover lines for my 2027 issue, as if I’m on the cover of my own magazine.”Seeing yourself as the main story makes the plan feel like yours.
