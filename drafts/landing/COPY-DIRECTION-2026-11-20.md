# ISSUE11
THE NEW WAY OF MANIFESTATION

## HERO

# YOU KNOW WHAT EVERYONE ELSE'S DREAM LIFE LOOKS LIKE. WHAT ABOUT YOURS?

You know where your favorite influencer spent the summer, what she wears to Pilates and how she decorated her new apartment. You've followed her career, celebrated her milestones and probably bought something she recommended.

Meanwhile, you've got a few ambitions of your own that haven't made it past the Notes app.

ISSUE11 is a manifestation app and personal creative hub that puts your attention back where it belongs. Explore the future you want, turn your ideas into a magazine about your life and start developing the habits that could get you there.

## 01 / THE REALITY CHECK

# YOU THOUGHT YOU'D BE FURTHER ALONG BY NOW.

Maybe you thought you'd have your own business by 30. You'd be making better money, living somewhere beautiful or finally doing work that made you excited to get out of bed.

You had plans. Good ones, too.

And it's not as though you've been sitting around doing nothing. You've made vision boards, listened to hours of podcasts, bought the planners and started more morning routines than you care to admit.

But you've also gotten very good at preparing to change your life.

You have so many ideas for the life you want, but somehow another year goes by and very little has changed.

That's the part that hurts. Especially when you know you're capable of more.

And while you're busy wondering why you haven't made more progress, there's an entire internet full of women who seem to be doing everything you've been dreaming about.

You can be happy for them and still be frustrated with yourself.

We think it's time your own ambitions got some of that attention.

## 02 / THE NEW WAY OF MANIFESTATION

# YOUR VISION BOARD IS GORGEOUS. NOW WHAT?

We have nothing against vision boards. In fact, we think imagination is one of the most underrated tools you have.

You should be able to picture an extraordinary future for yourself. The apartment, the career, the financial freedom, the confidence to walk into a room and feel like you belong there.

But manifestation has developed a rather convenient relationship with doing nothing.

Somewhere along the way, people started talking as though thinking about a beautiful life was the same thing as creating one. As though the universe would handle the uncomfortable parts if you visualized hard enough.

We'd like to challenge that idea.

Because the woman running the business you want probably had to send an awkward first email. She made decisions without knowing how they'd turn out. She learned things she wasn't particularly good at and kept going after a few embarrassing attempts.

That's worth visualizing, too.

Imagine the outcome, certainly. Then spend some time imagining the person you'd need to become and what she'd do when things got difficult.

That's the version of manifestation we're interested in exploring.

## 03 / INTRODUCING ISSUE11

# YOU'VE BEEN FOLLOWING EVERYONE ELSE'S STORY. LET'S TALK ABOUT YOURS.

There's something absurd about having an algorithm that knows your taste in furniture, your dream holiday destination and the exact kind of woman you aspire to be, while your own plans remain scattered across six different apps.

We thought you deserved somewhere better to put them.

ISSUE11 is a personal creative hub where your ambitions become the subject of your own magazine.

Collect the images that excite you. Write about the things you want, including the ones you're almost embarrassed to admit. Explore new possibilities, work through your ideas and start giving your future some creative direction.

Your AI Editor uses what you share to develop a magazine that reflects your vision, your thoughts and the changes you're making along the way.

The more you put into it, the more personal it becomes.

You already know what it's like to follow someone else's life. We think creating your own could be considerably more interesting.

**YOUR FUTURE. IN PRINT.**

## 04 / HOW IT WORKS

# ALL THAT POTENTIAL. LET'S SEE WHAT YOU DO WITH IT.

### FIND OUT WHAT YOU WANT.

It's surprisingly easy to confuse what you want with what you've been taught to admire.

The impressive job title, the expensive apartment, the lifestyle that looks wonderful in photographs. Maybe you want all of it. Maybe some of it has very little to do with you.

ISSUE11 gives you somewhere to explore those questions. Bring your ideas, images and thoughts together until you start seeing a clearer picture of what matters to you.

### GIVE YOUR VISION SOME ATTENTION.

You can't spend five minutes thinking about your goals in January and expect them to stay at the front of your mind all year.

Your magazine keeps your ambitions within reach. Return to your images, revisit what you've written and spend time thinking about the life you're creating.

Your attention influences what you notice, what you prioritize and which opportunities you decide to pursue. It's worth being deliberate about where you put it.

### START ACTING LIKE YOU MEAN IT.

Every week, choose one manageable action that moves you toward something you want.

If you're dreaming about starting a business, you might research your first customer. If you want a different career, you might finally look into the qualifications you need.

Some weeks you'll make exciting progress. Other weeks, keeping one promise to yourself will be enough.

The important part is giving your ambitions a place in your actual schedule.

### PAY ATTENTION TO WHO YOU'RE BECOMING.

Your first plan probably won't be your final one.

You'll discover things about yourself, change your mind, make mistakes and occasionally surprise yourself.

Keep writing about what you're learning. Your magazine evolves as your goals and habits change, giving you a record of the person you're becoming along the way.

## 05 / THE EXPERIENCE

# CONSIDER THIS YOUR CREATIVE HEADQUARTERS.

You probably already have a Notes app full of ideas, a Pinterest account full of inspiration and a camera roll full of screenshots you meant to do something with.

ISSUE11 gives all that ambition somewhere to live.

**YOUR PERSONAL MAGAZINE**

A publication devoted to the life you're creating. Your images, goals and reflections become part of an evolving editorial world that feels unmistakably yours.

**THE JOURNAL**

Write about the business you want to start, the person you're trying to become or the decision you've been avoiding. You don't need to have a perfectly formed thought before you put it down.

**YOUR AI EDITOR**

Your Editor works with your journal entries and the material you've collected to develop your personal magazine. The ideas you've been carrying around in your head begin taking shape on the page.

**YOUR WEEKLY FOCUS**

Choose a small action connected to your goals and give it your attention for the week. Building a different life requires making different choices, and this gives you somewhere to begin.

**THE MEDITATIONS**

Musical meditations for practicing visualization and spending time with the future you want. Sometimes it's useful to put your phone on Do Not Disturb and remember what you were so excited about in the first place.

One day, you might want to remember what you were dreaming about before any of it happened.

The business idea you were afraid to tell people about. The apartment you saved photographs of for years. The goals that seemed unreasonable at the time.

ISSUE11 gives you the option to turn your personal magazine into a printed edition.

You can look back at the plans you made, the things you've learned and the ambitions that changed as you did.

There's something rather wonderful about holding a record of a future you once had to imagine.

**A LIFE WORTH PUTTING IN PRINT.**

## 07 / THE FINAL INVITATION

# YOU'RE GOING TO GET OLDER ANYWAY.

Another birthday will come around. Another year will pass. And you'll have another opportunity to look at your life and ask yourself whether you're happy with where it's going.

Maybe you've already had that conversation with yourself a few times.

But you could have a year of decisions behind you. A year of trying things, changing habits, learning what works and giving your own future the attention it deserves.

You could finally find out what happens when you take your potential seriously.

And wouldn't that be worth something?
