# issue11.app copy: one pain, one outcome (Nov 9, 2026)

Megan's brief (Nov 9): "Keep looping until the copywriting makes it very clear which pain point we are solving and only speak of the desired outcome of our ICP." She also said to use Reddit and the web for the ICP's own words.

## The one pain (POSITIONING.md #10 + Oct 15 note, in ICP words)
She knows exactly the life she wants. It's in her saves, screenshots and boards. But saving it feels like progress, the week ends, and nothing in her real life has moved. She's waiting to feel ready while watching other people live it.

## The one outcome
Her days start to look like the life she saved: she is living it now, one small step at a time, instead of "one day".

## ICP verbiage found (paraphrased snippets; sources at the bottom)
- "Knowing isn't doing." (r/NevilleGoddard)
- Saving "another post" feels productive while she stays detached from real action (Reddit, r/DecidingToBeBetter mirror)
- More pins, more saved posts, then the day ends "neither accomplished nor fulfilled" (Medium)
- "Set goals, make a dream board and wait... nothing happens" (podcast)
- "Waiting for the signal that it's time to move" / "waiting all my life" (Reddit, 2026)
- Waiting until you feel ready means you never start (Reddit)
- The dream life felt "too far out of reach" (Her Campus)
- Failed vision boards, tucked away, leave people "feeling incapable" (Substack)

## Loop
Each round was checked against 6 tests: (1) the pain is clear in 3 seconds, (2) it's in her words, (3) it's her outcome, not our features, (4) it makes no promise of results (App Store and FTC trust rule), (5) it doesn't use "becoming her" or shame social media, (6) it's process-focused (the steps, not waiting for the outcome).

- **v1:** "Turn your visionboard into a personal magazine" (live now). Fails 1 and 3: it describes the product, with no pain and no outcome.
- **v2:** "Manifest the life you want, one step at a time." Fails 1 (no pain) and 4 ("manifest" reads as a promise).
- **v3:** "Stop saving your dream life. Start living it." Passes 1, 2, 3 and 6. The risk on 5: "stop saving" can sound like scolding her habit.
- **v4 (final):** "You've saved the life you want. Now start living it." Passes all 6. It names her habit with warmth, not blame, and the outcome is in her hands.
