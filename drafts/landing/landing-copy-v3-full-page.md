## 3. How it works
H2: From saved to started in 3 steps.
1. **Save it.** Add the screenshots and photos of the life you want, heart the images you love in the app, and write it down in your own words.
2. **See it.** Your Editor, the AI built into ISSUE11, designs them into a real magazine with your name on the cover. Change any page until it feels like you.
3. **Start it.** Each week your Editor suggests one small move toward a page you love. Do it, and add a photo of it to your Issue.

## 4. Features
Section label: INSIDE ISSUE11
H2: Six things that make your dream life hard to ignore.

- **My saves** · Your future, out of your camera roll.
  Pull the good stuff out of 4,000 screenshots and give it a home, next to the images you heart in the app.
- **Your Issue** · A magazine about the life you're building.
  Your saves and words become a real magazine with your name on the cover. You're the art director: tell your Editor what to change.
- **Scribe** · Write the day you want, down to the outfit.
  Where you wake up, what you do first, how it feels. It stays private, and you pick which lines go in your Issue.
- **Audio** · Hear yourself living it.
  5 or 10 minute guided sessions, a voice over soft music with visuals. Watch, or close your eyes and listen, and practice the habits of the life you saved until they feel normal.
- **Your weekly move** · A small nudge, never a to-do list.
  Your Editor suggests one move toward a page you love. Skip it, shrink it, or do it and add a photo.
- **Print** · Hold your future in your hands.
  Order your finished Issue as a printed magazine for $29.99 and leave it on your coffee table.
