## 4. Features
Section label: INSIDE ISSUE11 (yes, there's a printed magazine)
H2: Six tools that go further than wishful thinking.

- **My saves** · Where the life you want takes shape.
  Add your photos and heart the images of the life you're going for. Your magazine and your weekly moves are built from them.
- **Your Issue** · Make your own magazine about your future.
  Build it page by page from your photos and words, with your name on the cover. You're the art director: tell your Editor what to change.
- **Scribe** · Write the day you want, down to the outfit.
  Where you wake up, what you do first, how it feels. It stays private, and you pick which lines go in your Issue.
- **Audio** · Meditations that rehearse the steps, not just the dream.
  5 or 10 minute guided sessions, a voice over soft music with visuals. You practice the morning, the workout, the hard email, so doing them for real feels normal.
- **Your weekly move** · A small nudge, never a to-do list.
  Your Editor suggests one move toward a page you love. Skip it, shrink it, or do it and add a photo.
- **Print** · Hold your future in your hands.
  Order your finished Issue as a printed magazine for $29.99 and leave it on the coffee table. Let people ask.
