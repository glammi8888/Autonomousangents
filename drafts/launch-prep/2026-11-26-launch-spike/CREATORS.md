## 6. Offer Codes in App Store Connect (Megan, about 15 minutes, on or after Dec 11)
✅ Checked today (Nov 26, 2026) on Apple's help page: [Set up offer codes](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/set-up-offer-codes).
**Before you can:** Paid Apps Agreement active · the yearly subscription approved · the app **Ready for Sale** (codes only redeem once it's live) · your role is Account Holder, Admin, App Manager or Marketing.
1. App Store Connect → **Apps** → ISSUE11 → **Subscriptions** (sidebar) → the subscription group → the **yearly** subscription.
2. **Subscription Prices** → **+** → **Create Offer Codes**.
3. Reference name: `Creators free year Dec 2026`.
4. Eligibility: **New subscribers** only.
5. If asked about the intro offer (7-day trial): choose that they **can't** stack both (simpler).
6. Countries: United States (add others only if a creator lives elsewhere) → Next.
7. Type: **Free** → Duration: **1 year** → Next → review → **Confirm**. (Apple's page doesn't list durations; Apple's offer docs and a developer forum report show 1 year as an option. If you don't see it, take the longest free option and tell me.) Once confirmed you **can't edit** an offer.
8. Then **Offer Codes** tab → your offer → **Create Custom Codes** → one code per creator, e.g. `SIENNA2027`, letters and numbers only, up to 64 characters, **redemption limit 1**, expiry up to 6 months (pick Jun 30, 2027).
   - Don't use One-Time Use Codes for this: the minimum batch is 500.
9. Codes can take **up to an hour** to work. Redemption: the link with the code (App Store Connect shows the URL format), or the App Store → account → "Redeem Gift Card or Code".
- Heads-up for later: if a free offer covers the whole subscription period, Apple's help text (quoted in a developer forum) says it may not auto-renew afterward. Fine for a gift year. Checked as reported, not on Apple's main page.
