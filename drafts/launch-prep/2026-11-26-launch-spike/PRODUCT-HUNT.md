## ✅ Rules checked today (Nov 26, 2026, Product Hunt help center)
- Launches go live at **12:01 AM Pacific** and run until midnight Pacific. You can schedule from tomorrow up to **30 days ahead**, or save a draft. Source: [Set your goals and pick your launch date](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734355-3-set-your-goals-and-pick-your-launch-date)
- **"Never ask for upvotes."** No paying for upvotes or comments, no giveaways tied to upvotes, no mass DMs, no fake or extra accounts. Sharing the link with your own audience and asking people to try it and comment is fine. Source: [How to promote your launch (and what to avoid)](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734384-5-how-to-promote-your-launch-and-what-to-avoid)
- Featuring on the homepage is a curation call. They look for originality, a clear edge, plain language and a **working product** ("It's live, or it will be soon with a clear date"). Unfeatured launches still go live. Source: [What kind of products get featured](http://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734341-2-what-kind-of-products-get-featured)
- Weekday vs weekend: "no magic weekday". Friday has less competition than Tue to Thu, which helps a solo founder. Same source as the first bullet.
- From the Nov 24 check: free to launch; the product link should be where people can get it (the App Store link).
