## Megan: run the USPTO check in 5 minutes
1. Go to **tmsearch.uspto.gov** (the free Trademark Search). No login needed.
2. Search, one at a time: `ISSUE11` · `ISSUE 11` · `ISSUE ELEVEN` · `ISSUE` · `ISSUU`.
3. Filter **Status = Live**. Then look at **International Class 009** (apps/software), **041** (publishing, education, entertainment) and also **042** (software as a service) and **016** (printed magazines; relevant for the printed issues).
4. For each live hit, note: the exact mark, the owner, the goods/services text, and the status.
5. A **real conflict** = a live mark that looks or sounds like ISSUE11 AND covers apps, journals, magazines or self-development. A hit for something unrelated (e.g. "ISSUE" for clothing) is not a conflict.
6. Paste the results (or screenshots) into this file or the chat. GROWTH updates the verdict.
