## Pinterest (Apify, Pinterest Trends US, Nov 13)
- Wallpapers dominate Pinterest's own growing and seasonal lists right now (e.g. "november desktop wallpaper" +10,000% monthly, "cute thanksgiving wallpaper iphone" +9,000%, "pinkmas wallpaper" +7,500%). "Wallpaper" is a top-10 monthly term. This validates the wallpaper maker.
- No rebrand, glow up or vision board terms appear in the top 200 trend lists (these lists only hold the very biggest terms).
- Pinterest's search bar does suggest "personal rebrand checklist / template / prompts", so people search it on Pinterest, but in small numbers.

## Conclusion
- "Personal rebrand" is a small search (about 40–280 a month on Google). The Kit's content is right, but the name isn't what most people type.
- "Glow up" is searched about 30x more (how to glow up 9.3K, glow up tips 5K) and is one of Megan's own desire lines ("glowing up").
- Recommendation: rename it "Free 2027 Glow Up Kit", keep "rebrand" as the subline, and add a glow up checklist page. The vision board and journal prompt searches are covered by the Vision Board Kit and Journaling Prompts freebies already planned.
- Caveat: these are Google numbers, not Pinterest volumes (Pinterest doesn't publish keyword volumes). The direction is the same on both.
