## What we collect

**Your email address.** When you request access to ISSUE11, we collect the email you type into the form. That's the only thing the form asks for.

**Basic website data.** Like most websites, our host collects standard technical information when you visit: pages viewed, the site that sent you, your browser and device type and your approximate location based on your IP address. We use it to see how the site is doing.

We don't ask for your name, phone number, payment details or anything else on this site.

## Why we use it

- To send you your invitation when early access opens
- To send you ISSUE11 news and launch updates by email
- To understand how people find and use the site

We don't sell your email. We don't share it with advertisers.

## Who helps us

We use a small number of service providers to run the site and the waitlist:

- **Email notification:** each new request is emailed to our team inbox.
- **Spreadsheet:** requests are copied into a private spreadsheet that only our team can open.
- **Email newsletter:** when we send invitations, waitlist emails are imported into our newsletter provider, which sends the emails and tells us whether they were delivered and opened.
