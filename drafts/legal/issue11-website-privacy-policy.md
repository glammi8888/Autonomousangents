**Privacy Policy**
Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the ISSUE11 website (issue11.app) collects and how we use it. It covers the website and the waitlist only. The ISSUE11 app will have its own privacy policy before it launches.

**Who we are**
ISSUE11 is based in Los Angeles, California. Questions about privacy: support@issue11.app.

**What we collect**
- Your email address, when you request access to the waitlist.
- Basic technical information, collected automatically when you visit, such as browser type, device type, pages viewed and approximate location based on your IP address.
- Advertising measurement: we use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). When you visit our site or join the waitlist, it can tell Meta that a visit or sign-up happened, along with technical information such as your IP address, browser and the page you viewed. We use it to see which of our Instagram and Facebook ads bring people to ISSUE11 and to show our ads to people who may be interested. Meta may use cookies and similar technology for this.

We don't ask for your name or payment details on this website.

**How we use it**
- To send you waitlist updates, your invitation and launch news.
- To understand and improve the website.
- To measure and improve our ads on Instagram and Facebook.

We don't sell your email address. Meta receives the information described above through the Pixel. Under some US state laws, using a pixel for advertising can count as "sharing" information for advertising. You can opt out (see Your choices).

**Who we share it with**
Only the services that run the website, our emails and our ads: Framer (website hosting and statistics), Google (where we store the waitlist list), Resend (sends our emails) and Meta (advertising measurement through the Meta Pixel). Meta's use of information is described in Meta's Privacy Policy.

**How long we keep it**
We keep your email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

**Your choices**
- Unsubscribe anytime using the link in any email.
- Ask us to see, correct or delete your information, or to opt out of advertising "sharing", by writing to support@issue11.app. We'll reply within 30 days.
- You can limit ad tracking in your browser and device settings, and adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

**Do Not Track**
Our website doesn't respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, because there isn't an agreed standard for them yet.

**Children**
This website isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child gave us their email, contact us and we'll delete it.

**Security**
We use trusted services and reasonable measures to protect your information. No website can promise perfect security.

**Changes**
If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top. For important changes, we'll email the waitlist.
