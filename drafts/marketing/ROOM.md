## 4. The cross-channel loop (one article → many doors → sign-ups)
```
AEO worker publishes 1 article/day on issue11.app  (log: AEO commit + this board)
   ├─► 📌 Pinterest: 5 fresh pins for that URL, ≥72h apart, keyword-first   → outbound clicks
   ├─► 🧵 Reddit: if a live thread asks that question, 1 disclosed reply draft → referral visits
   ├─► 🎬 Reels/TikTok: 1 script from the article's best line                  → profile link visits
   └─► 🖋 matching lead magnet (Rebrand Kit, Wallpapers, Vision Board Kit)    → email sign-ups
            ▼
   every article + kit page has the waitlist form → waitlist → beta (Dec 11) → app users
            ▼
   💸 Media buyer boosts only what wins organically (after Megan's go)
   💚 Growth Lead reads the numbers weekly → more of the winning article/pin/format
```
Hand-off by files, not by calling each other: new article URLs come from the AEO commits; pins go to `drafts/pinterest/`; Reddit drafts to `drafts/growth/reddit/<date>.md`; Reels to `drafts/social/`; ad notes to `drafts/ads/`. Nobody redoes the hourly Pinterest routine's or the AEO workers' work.
