## The simplest way to set it up (easiest first, then cheapest)
1. Megan opens the identity kit design ("Red and White Bold Modern Personal Brand Identity Kit Presentation", 10 pages) and makes master #1 and #3 from its pages, or approves a layout she likes.
2. She names the text boxes like the "fillable parts" column (e.g. COVER_LINE). That's the whole setup.
3. Agents **copy** the master and replace only the named text and photo. The original is never edited.
4. 3 variations → Megan picks → batch of up to 6 at a time.

Optional upgrade, later: publish the masters as **Brand Templates** with data fields, so agents can autofill them. Brand Templates are a paid Canva feature (Pro/Teams), and autofill access depends on the plan. Check what Megan's plan includes before relying on it. Copying works on any plan, so start there.
