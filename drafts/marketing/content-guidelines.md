## 1. Who we are, in one breath
**ISSUE11 is a process-focused manifestation app that turns your dreams into a personal magazine and helps you take the small steps that make them real.** (Megan, Oct 10)

## 2. Voice
**Three words (from the Glammi guide): Witty · Sharp · Human.**
For ISSUE11 that means: a brilliant friend with great taste who believes in your dream and is honest about the in-between. Editorial like a magazine, dry like a friend's text, warm without gushing.

**ISSUE11 adds four rules on top of the Glammi voice:**
1. **Process, not outcome.** Talk about the steps, the rehearsal, the small move this week. Never the guaranteed result.
2. **Believable, not forced.** No "I'm a millionaire" energy. Say "delulu" (Megan's word), never "delusional".
3. **No homework, no guilt.** No streak pressure, no "you should", no shame for stopping.
4. **The AI stays backstage.** Don't lead with "AI". Lucky Star is mentioned only when it's doing something useful.

### Writing rules (from the Glammi guide, apply to everything)
- **No em dashes.** Use a comma, a full stop or rewrite. **No Oxford comma.**
- **Banned words:** quietly, chaos/chaotic, game-changer, groundbreaking, unlock, leverage, navigate, landscape, ecosystem, seamless, robust, dive deep/unpack, at the end of the day, in today's world, this changes everything, here's the thing, let's be honest, spoiler:, plot twist:, no fluff.
- **Banned patterns:** "Most people think X. They're wrong." setups · hollow closing questions ("What do you think?") · fake humility · saying it twice · hustle or girlboss framing.
- **Strong last line.** The line people screenshot. Dry-funny or unexpectedly true.
- **Short over long.** When in doubt, cut the last paragraph.

## 3. Content pillars (4)
Every post belongs to one pillar. Rough mix in brackets.

**1. THE ISSUE (35%)**: the magazine as the hero.
Cover reveals, "what would your cover line be?", pages turning, readers' dream lives as cover stories (with permission), behind-the-scenes of how an Issue gets made. *Parked idea that fits here: "Issue of the Month" (Notion 💡 Ideas Backlog).*

**2. THE IN-BETWEEN (30%)**: process over outcome.
The gap between dreaming and living it. Small moves, rehearsing the steps, "you can't control the outcome". Useful and honest, never homework.
*Example hook:* "Your vision board isn't the problem. Nobody told you what to do on Tuesday."

**3. DELULU, HONESTLY (20%)**: manifestation culture with wit.
Relatable truths about affirmations that feel fake, the forgotten Pinterest board and consuming manifestation content instead of living. Laugh *with* the reader, never at her, at other apps or at anyone's beliefs.
*Example hook:* "Saying 'I'm a millionaire' to the mirror while checking my bank app."

**4. THE EDITOR'S DESK (15%)**: Megan building ISSUE11 in public.
Glammi example 4 is the model: real, specific, including the broken bits. Founder story details are Megan's to share or not (the Instagram Post 2 question is still open). Later this pillar also carries real user Proof, only with written permission.

## 4. Video style
**Hooks (first 1-2 seconds)**
- Open on the **magazine** or a **specific moment**, never a logo or "Hi guys". Glammi's scene-setter works on video: "3am thoughts while making my vision board for the fourth time:"
- The hook is a true, specific line. No fake stats, no "doctors hate this", no "this will change your life".
- On-screen text says the hook too (many people watch without sound).

**Captions**
- Burned-in captions on every video. Keep text inside the safe zones from `drafts/launch-prep/instagram-launch.md` (out of the top 220 px and bottom 420 px on 1080×1920).
- The caption under the post follows the writing rules above. 3-5 hashtags max.

**The magazine reveal (signature format)**
Blank cream page → "Printing your first issue…" → the cover appears → slow page turn → one payoff line. Always label example covers as examples ("YOUR NAME HERE"). Only show screens that match the real app, labelled "Early design" until launch.

**Format priority (Megan, Oct 13, word for word): "I want to prioritize reels and carousels..."**
Reels and carousels come first. Single image posts, Stories and pins come only when a reel or carousel already exists to repurpose. Every reel can also go to TikTok as-is (no extra work).

**Weekly rhythm (Megan, Oct 13, word for word): "4 reels per week and 3 carousels"** = 7 posts a week.

- **Reels:** hook in the first 1-2 seconds, the magazine reveal as the signature format, 7-30 seconds.
- **Carousels:** 4-7 slides. Slide 1 is the hook (a cover or one big word), the middle slides deliver one idea each, the last slide is the payoff line. They are made to be saved and shared.

## 5. Guardrails (always on)
- **No promised outcomes.** No "you will", no income, body, relationship or health results. Proof is shown as someone's own experience, never a guarantee.
- **No "becoming her".** No "vision boards don't work". No research cited as proof.
- **Nothing fake.** No made-up reviews, testimonials, numbers, user counts or screenshots. No fake urgency.
- **Real people only with written permission**, including users' Proof and any creator feature. Paid or gifted = clearly labelled (#ad / paid partnership). Never imply someone uses or endorses ISSUE11 when they don't.
- **No mocking** therapy, religion, mental health, other apps or anyone's beliefs. Never position ISSUE11 as help for depression or mental health.
- **Shock stays in stunts, never in the app or the App Store listing** (from outrageous-campaigns.md; protects Apple featuring).
- **Don't lead with AI.** Never call ISSUE11 a chatbot, productivity, goal-setting or task app.
- **Images:** only photos Megan owns or has licensed; check an AI tool's commercial license before using its images.
- **Privacy:** no personal details about Megan or her family unless she chooses to share them herself.
- **Agents draft, Megan posts.** Agents never post, schedule, publish, submit or email.

## 7. Pre-publish check (Glammi checklist + ISSUE11)
1. Does the first line earn the second?
2. Can a word be cut?
3. Does it sound like a person, not a content calendar or AI?
4. Banned words, em dashes and Oxford commas all gone?
5. Is the last line strong enough to be the only thing remembered?
6. Process, not outcome? No promise, no "becoming her", no knocking vision boards?
7. Is everything true right now (dates, features, screens, claims)?
8. Would Megan be proud if someone she respects read it?
