## What the answers change
- **50/50 funny and aesthetic.** Our reading of "Cluely": bold, confident, a bit cheeky, and very shareable, while the visuals stay polished and editorial. ISSUE11's line: cheeky about *habits* (starting over, 400 pins), never about people, beliefs or other apps, and never fake stunts.
- **The Gap is not a pillar.** It's the once-a-week convert post, plus the last slide of every carousel.
- **Carousels = MOFU + BOFU hybrid.** Slides 1 to N-1 teach (consideration); the last slide is always a CTA (convert).
- **Solo-proof production.** Every reel format below is tagged by effort, and filmed in one weekly batch.

## Where AI helps (solo multipliers)
- **Scripts, hooks and captions:** the Content Room drafts all 7 posts each week; you edit and film.
- **Carousels:** agents fill the Canva masters you approve (copy only, per the template rule).
- **Carousel → reel:** every carousel also becomes a slideshow reel with trending audio (free extra post, zero filming).
- **Auto captions and cuts:** CapCut or Instagram's editor.
- **AI visuals:** editorial dream-life images for covers and B-roll (Higgsfield / Banana Pro prompts are available as skills). Check each tool's commercial license and label example covers as examples.
- **Not recommended:** an AI clone of your voice or face. It saves time but risks trust in a brand built on "nothing fake".
