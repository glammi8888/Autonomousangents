## The 4 pillars (sharpened from content-guidelines.md)

### 1 · THE ISSUE: your dream life as a magazine
- **Why it exists:** the hook nobody else has; aesthetic ownership ("editor-in-chief of your own life").
- **Funnel job:** Awareness. **Share of posts:** ~2 a week.
- **Signature series: "COVER STORY"**: every episode ends on a cover.
- **Formats:**
  | Format (bucket) | ISSUE11 version |
  |---|---|
  | Classic Wait For It (Wait for It) | Blank cream page → "Printing your first issue..." → the cover appears → page turn |
  | Blind Ranking (Skits) | "Blind ranking my next 5 life chapters" → ends as cover lines on an Issue |
  | Timelapse Build (Wait for It) | Making a real Issue spread from screenshots and pins, start to finish |
- **Carousel:** "What would your cover line be?" (cover-style slides, last slide asks for theirs).

### 2 · THE GAP: why the dream stays on the board
- **Why it exists:** the reason to care (framework: intention-behavior gap, self-regulation, mental contrasting). Feeds the Dream Life Gap Test.
- **Funnel job:** Consideration → Convert. **Share:** ~2-3 a week (most carousels live here).
- **Signature series: "THE GAP"**: one pattern per episode (starting over, saving instead of doing, waiting for the outcome).
- **Formats:**
  | Format (bucket) | ISSUE11 version |
  |---|---|
  | Great Question (Storytelling) | "Why does my vision board stop working after a week? Great question." |
  | Knowledge Gap Story (Storytelling) | "You keep starting over, and it's not a motivation problem." |
  | Making Fun of Stereotypes, behaviours only (Skits) | "Types of manifesters": the Monday restarter, the Pinterest collector, the overthinker... → "which one are you? Take the test" (Convert) |
- **Carousels (education):** Common Belief Buster ("More motivation isn't the fix") · This Is Your Reminder ("This is your reminder to do the small version") · C1 "Why most vision boards get forgotten".

### 3 · DELULU, HONESTLY: manifestation culture with wit
- **Why it exists:** shareability; laughing *with* the audience about the things they all do. Never mocks methods, beliefs or people.
- **Funnel job:** Awareness. **Share:** ~2 a week.
- **Signature series: "DELULU DIARIES"** (working name).
- **Formats:**
  | Format (bucket) | ISSUE11 version |
  |---|---|
  | Comparison Skit (Skits) | "Me making my vision board vs. me on a Tuesday" |
  | Personifying Relatable Things (Skits) | Megan as her dusty vision board: "You haven't looked at me since January." |
  | What Your X Says About You (Skits) | "What your vision board says about you" (all-Pinterest, all-quotes, all-cars...) → tag a friend |
  | Two Characters (Skits) | Frantic "I am a millionaire" girl vs. calm Issue girl (R3) |

### 4 · THE EDITOR'S DESK: building ISSUE11 (later: real Proof)
- **Why it exists:** trust and a face behind the brand; Megan's true story is the first case study.
- **Funnel job:** Consideration. **Share:** ~1 a week.
- **Signature series: "EDITOR'S NOTE"**.
- **Formats:**
  | Format (bucket) | ISSUE11 version |
  |---|---|
  | What I Learned Doing X (Storytelling) | "Before ISSUE11 was an app, it was a magazine I made about my future life. Here's what it taught me." |
  | How I Built This (Storytelling) | "I'm building a manifestation app without knowing how to code." |
  | Come With Me (Storytelling) | "Come design an Issue page with me" (real, imperfect, voice-over) |
  | Wait for It, case study (later) | A real user's Issue 1 → her latest Issue and Proof page (written permission, her own words, never "typical results") |

## Test plan (VIRAL FORMATS, Mode A)
- **Weeks 1-2:** run one format per series as above. Track views, watch time, saves, shares, follows per post (🧡 Data).
- **Week 3:** the best-performing series gets the Sunday slot and 10 variations planned ("run it a hundred ways").
- "Resonating" = above our own average on saves + shares (we have no baseline yet, so weeks 1-2 set it). No invented numbers.
