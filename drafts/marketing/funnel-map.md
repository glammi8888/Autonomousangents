# ISSUE11 Funnel Map (DRAFT for Megan)
Oct 13, 2026 · 📣 Content Room · Built with the `jaredrhod-marketing` skill (fundamentals + lead magnets) and its ISSUE11 overrides. Draft only: nothing is set up, sent or posted.

Jared's sequence: **Content → Lead magnet → Tripwire → Core offer → Profit maximizer**, planned backwards from the last step.
**The feeling we sell** (his "so what?" test): not "a magazine app". So what? You see your dream life clearly. So what? You know what to do this week. So what? **You feel like your life is finally moving, and you can see it.** That feeling leads every headline.

```
REELS + CAROUSELS (4 + 3 a week, free, pure goodwill)
        │  link in bio, every 3rd post mentions it
        ▼
LEAD MAGNET: free "Cover Story Kit" (email only)  ──►  the Kit email list (we own it)
        │  thank-you page + delivery email
        ▼
TRIPWIRE: pre-launch = a spot in the Nov 11 beta (their time)
          post-launch = the App Store free trial (Apple handles payment)
        ▼
CORE OFFER: ISSUE11 subscription (yearly with free trial, monthly without; prices = Megan + Revenue)
        ▼
PROFIT MAXIMIZER: monthly → yearly upgrade · later: a printed Issue? (idea, not in V1)
```

## 1. Content (introduce yourself)
Already planned in `content-guidelines.md`: 4 reels + 3 carousels a week across the 4 pillars.
- **Bank-account rule:** most posts are deposits (pure value, no ask). About 1 in 3 mentions the Cover Story Kit, never more.
- **Own the audience:** Instagram and TikTok are rented land. Every follower we can, we move to the email list.
- **Content ideas = the questions people already ask** (Jared's method): our 10 pain points in `design/POSITIONING.md` are exactly that list. Each pain point is a reel and a carousel.

## 2. Lead magnet (coffee): the free "Cover Story Kit"
**Why this one:** the magazine is our hook, and the kit lets people taste it before the app exists. It's specific (one outcome: your dream life as a cover), fast and matches onboarding screen 27 ("Describe your dream life in one sentence. This becomes your cover story.").

**What's inside (a short PDF + one Canva link):**
1. A printable or editable ISSUE11 magazine cover (Megan makes the master in Canva; people edit their own copy for free).
2. "Write your cover story in one sentence": 3 prompts + 3 example lines.
3. "Your first move": one small step this week, written the way the future you would do it.
4. Last page: "The app does this every week. Beta starts November 11."

**Opt-in page** (a separate page on issue11.com, e.g. `/cover`: one headline, one email field, nothing else to click. The main holding page keeps its FAQ for AI search.)
> **Headline:** What would the cover of your dream life say?
> **Sub:** Get the free Cover Story Kit: your own magazine cover, the one sentence that becomes your cover story and your first small move. Ten minutes, and your dream life has a front page.
> **Button:** Send me the kit
> **Under the button:** Free. You'll also get early access news from ISSUE11. Unsubscribe anytime.

**Thank-you page** (acknowledges what just happened, then the next step):
> Your Cover Story Kit is on its way to your inbox, and there's one more thing...
> **The ISSUE11 beta opens November 11.** Want a spot? [Yes, save me a spot]

**Benchmark (Jared's):** under 25% opt-in = fix the headline first · 25-35% solid · over 35% great.

## 3. Tripwire (first date)
Jared's tripwire is a cheap first purchase. For an iPhone app that's awkward before launch (selling a separate $7 product means payments, which need Megan's approval under CLAUDE.md, plus a new tool). By his own definition, something that costs people **time** or **wallet access** counts as a tripwire, so:
- **Before launch:** a **beta spot** (Nov 11). They give time and feedback. Free, real, and it fills the beta.
- **After launch:** the **App Store free trial** on the yearly plan (Apple takes the card, so it's a real tripwire). Already the agreed direction (ONBOARDING-V3.md, Oct 8).
- *Option for later, not recommended now:* a paid digital "Future Issue" printable. Only if Megan wants a pre-launch revenue test (payments → her approval first).

## 4. Core offer (relationship)
The ISSUE11 subscription through the locked onboarding and paywall (`design/ONBOARDING-V3.md`). Front door always open; special deals (real App Store intro offers, the closing-paywall offer 37b) open and close with honest deadlines. Prices and offers: Megan + Revenue Lead.

## 5. Profit maximizer (marriage)
- **Monthly → yearly** upgrade offer (App Store supports it).
- **Idea for later:** a printed copy of your Issue ("something you can eventually hold", Product Architecture). Not in V1. If Megan likes it, it goes in the 💡 Ideas Backlog.
