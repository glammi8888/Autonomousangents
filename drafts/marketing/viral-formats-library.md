## REELS

### R1 · Blind Ranking: My Next 5 Life Chapters · Awareness
**Why it works:** blind-ranking is an interactive trend; viewers argue in the comments about the order.
**Shot list (20-30 s):**
1. Hook text: **"Blind ranking my next 5 life chapters"** · cards face down.
2. Flip one at a time, place 1-5: *Living by the sea · My first $30k month · Launching my own studio · A year abroad · Buying my dream car.* (Money dreams are fine as **wishes** on the cards; Megan, Oct 13.)
3. Laugh at a bad placement ("a half marathon at number one?!").
4. 🗣 "Okay, this list is a mess. Let's put it somewhere I'll actually look at it."
5. Cut to an ISSUE11 cover (early design) with the 5 chapters as cover lines.
**Caption:** "Rank yours in the comments. Wrong answers only."
**Note:** once the app is live, users can make their own version, so it doubles as a trend for them.

### R2 · Keep Three, Cut Three: Manifestation Methods · Consideration
**Why it works:** opinion + choice = comments; everyone has a method they love.
**Shot list (20-30 s):** six cards: *369 method · scripting · affirmations on loop · vision board · gratitude list · a magazine about my future.*
- Keep/cut with one honest line each, e.g. 🗣 "Cutting scripting. For me it started to feel like homework." · "Keeping gratitude. Two minutes, no pressure."
- Last keep: "The magazine. I actually open it, and it comes with one small move a week."
**Tone rule:** never mock a method or the people who love it ("for me" language). It's taste, not truth.
**Caption:** "Which three would you keep?"

### R3 · Two Characters, One Desk · Awareness
**Why it works:** one creator, two roles; relatable comedy.
**Script (20-25 s):**
- **Character A** (frantic, writing "I am a millionaire" for the 40th time): "Why is nothing changing?!"
- **Character B** (calm, coffee, flipping her Issue): "What did you do this week?"
- A: "...I wrote it 40 times."
- B: "Cute. Pick one small thing and do it on Tuesday."
- B taps the 11 sticker; on screen: *YOUR MOVE THIS WEEK: send the email you've been drafting.*
- Text: **"Dream it. Then do one small thing."**
**Tone rule:** A is lovable, not stupid. We laugh *with* her (we've all been her).

### R4 · Compressed: My Year in One Issue · Consideration (later: Convert)
**Why it works:** fast, rhythmic flip-through, very rewatchable.
**Shot list (10-15 s):** a trending beat, pages flipping: cover → the vision page → the moves page → the proof page → "next Issue" teaser.
**Text:** "One Issue. One year of small moves." (🗣 only with Megan's real Issue, or a clearly labeled example)
**Rule:** no claims about income, bodies or results. Proof pages show the creator's own real, small wins.
**Best timing:** once real users have real Issues (with permission). Before launch, use Megan's own.

### R5 · "Things I stopped doing" (bonus, proven format) · Awareness
Text cards, one per beat: "Stopped: starting over every Monday · Stopped: saving 400 pins I never look at · Stopped: affirmations I didn't believe · Started: one small move a week." End on the magazine. 🗣 only true lines.

### R6 · The Reveal (signature format, from content-guidelines) · Awareness
Blank cream page → "Printing your first issue..." → the cover appears → slow page turn → one payoff line.

## CAROUSELS

### C1 · Hills I Will Die On: Why Most Vision Boards Get Forgotten · Consideration
1. **Hook:** "Why most vision boards get forgotten."
2. **The problem:** pinning the dream feels great for a day. Then it becomes wallpaper.
3. **The insight:** the board shows the destination. It never tells you what to do on Tuesday.
4. **The fix:** keep the vision, add one small move a week, and a place to see your progress.
5. **The reveal:** an ISSUE11 spread (early design): image · your cover line · this week's move · your proof.
6. **Payoff:** "Keep the board. Give it a Tuesday."
**Rule:** never "vision boards are dead" or "vision boards don't work".

### C2 · Bad, Okay, Great: Where Your Goals Live · Consideration
- **Bad:** in your head, where they're safe and never tested.
- **Okay:** in a notebook you open on January 1st.
- **Great:** in a magazine made from your own words and images, that you actually want to open again, with one small move each week.
- **Payoff:** "Your goals deserve better than a notes app."

### C3 · The Dream Life Gap teaser · Convert
1. "You know what you want. So why aren't you getting there?"
2-4. Three short profile teasers: *The Serial Reinventor · The Overthinker · The Overwhelmed Visionary* (final names: Megan decides).
5. "Which one are you? Take the 2-minute test. Link in bio."
