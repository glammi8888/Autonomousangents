Started Nov 5, 2026 from DRAFT v2. Rubric = "Pass criteria" 1–9 in issue11-waitlist-sequence.md, plus C10: "Would a taste-driven founder who hates AI slop and generic SaaS copy call this terrible?" (10 = no way, it reads like a private invitation from a fashion house or magazine). Scores are E1–E5 × C1–C10.

## Change of direction (received before round 1 rewrite)
Megan, word for word: "Don't give the prompts. They should be grateful to have access in the first place lol" and "keep things concise and sweet".
Applied from round 1 on: the Prompt Pack and every gift or freebie are removed from all emails. Being accepted is the gift. No "assignment" wording that points to the pack. Email 1 gets a new single CTA. Body target is now about 40 to 80 words per email: shorter, warmer, never saccharine or needy.
