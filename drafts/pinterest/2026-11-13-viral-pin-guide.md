# What top pins look like right now (Nov 13, 2026)

Source: the top 30 Pinterest search results for "2027 vision board ideas", "manifestation journal" and "future self journal" (US), pulled with Apify (actor `lergassy/pinterest-search-scraper`, security check passed, no Pinterest login). Ranked by reactions. Raw data: `2026-11-13-top-pins.json`. I looked at the 6 top images myself.
Context: Pinterest's own 2026 trend report leans to analog and handmade looks ("pen pals", "poetcore").

## The 5 formats that win
1. **One-line type card** (top 2027 pin, 1,219 reactions): one big phrase on a plain background ("2027 is my year": pink serif on black; "-10 kg": black italic serif on white). People save these to build their own vision boards.
2. **Real handwritten journal page** (top manifestation pin, 1,691 reactions): a photo of a real notebook page with a hand in frame, written scripting ("369 method"). Analog, real, not designed.
3. **iPhone Notes screenshot** (1,358 reactions): a list of journal prompts in the Notes app, dark mode. It looks like a real person's notes, not an ad.
4. **Printable grid or challenge** (1,114 reactions): "30 Day Manifestation Journal Challenge", 30 boxes with one prompt each. Useful, so people save it for later.
5. **One-theme photo collage** (694 reactions): many small photos of one goal (all A+ grades). It works as a ready-made vision board tile.

## Patterns
- **Size:** tall. Most are 2:3 (1000×1500); 2027 vision board pins are often 3:4.
- **No videos** in any of the top 90. Static images win for these searches.
- **Content beats followers:** the top pins come from accounts with 10 to 3,000 followers. Small accounts can win.
- **Text on the image is short** and readable on a phone, with the keyword in the pin title.
- About half link out. Ours should all link to issue11.app or a matching blog article.

## How ISSUE11 uses this (respecting our copy rules)
- Type cards: "2027 is the year I become ME", "Future you is already on her way" style, in ISSUE11 fonts and colors. Never "become her".
- Real magazine/journal photos: Megan's own ISSUE11 pages or printed issue (her photos only), hand in frame.
- Notes-style prompt lists: "Future self journal prompts" in our voice ("future you", "your true self").
- Printable: "30-day future self journal challenge" linking to a blog article or the waitlist.
- Collage tiles per goal (travel, money, home, body, love) for the 2027 vision board boards.
- Don't copy any pin's design or text. These are the patterns only.
