## Part 1 · Organic: apply now

### Our main problem: lots of impressions, few outbound clicks
1. **Measure the right number.** Outbound click rate = clicks to the link ÷ times the pin was on screen. Sort Top Pins by **outbound clicks**, not impressions, which is the default sort. Filter Content type = Organic. *(help.pinterest.com, Pinterest Analytics)*
2. **Double down on what sends traffic.** Pinterest's own advice: if something on your site is driving traffic, "feature it in more Pins, and save it to more boards." → Every week, find the top 3 articles by outbound clicks and make new pins for them first.
3. **Links count from the day they're added.** Outbound clicks are only measured from the date a link is on the pin. Every pin gets its UTM link at creation (already our rule). *(Analytics help)*
4. **Ignore the first 48 hours.** Real-time numbers are estimates and "might decrease". Judge pins after day 3. *(Analytics help)*

### Pin design (most of it already matches what we do)
5. **2:3, 1000 × 1500.** Wider pins "may get cut off" in feeds. ✔ Orbit and notes pins are 1000×1500. *(creative best practices)*
6. **Layout:** visual in the middle, key message at the **top**, extra details at the **bottom**. ✔ The orbit pins follow this: big word on top, logo and URL at the bottom. *(creative best practices)*
7. **Logo or brand name clearly visible on every pin**, without taking over. ✔ The ISSUE11 logo sits bottom-left. *(creative best practices)*
8. **"Bits of motion make it pop."** Supports the orbit video pins. *(creative best practices)*
9. **Show the brand in use; avoid abstract lifestyle imagery that doesn't show the brand.** This is written for ads, but it points at a gap: almost none of our pins show the **ISSUE11 app**. → Test pins that show the app screens (Megan's toddler-posted landing page pins included app screenshots).
10. **The landing page should match the look and feel of the pin.** Written for ads, but it applies to every click: the article's hero image should look like the pin she tapped, so she knows she's in the right place.

### Titles and copy
11. **Title: up to 100 characters; the first 40 are what most people see.** → The keyword and the hook must both land in the first 40 characters. Check every title. *(creative best practices)*
12. **Description: up to 800 characters** (stated for ads). Ours run ~200. There's room for more keywords and a clearer call to action.
13. **For action goals, "clarity over cleverness"** and a clear call to action at the bottom (stated for lower-funnel ads). This is a balance for our polarizing hooks: the hook can be spicy, but the next line must say plainly what she gets when she taps.

### Cadence, boards, seasons
14. **New original pins at least once a week.** ✔ We're at 4 a day. *(how to make pins)*
15. **Every pin gets a URL.** ✔ *(how to make pins)*
16. **Board titles must be clear and descriptive** (they help search). → Do the board kit: a board per topic we post about, named the way people search (e.g. "Winter Arc", "Glow Up Tips", "Law of Assumption", "Vision Board Ideas 2027"). The winter arc pin currently sits on the glow up board. *(how to make pins)*
17. **Launch ahead of the peak: "If the moment peaks in June, launch in April."** Seasonal moments are "high-intent, search-led". Our calendar: *(brand moments guide 2026)*
    - New Year / vision board / word of the year → **now** (peaks late Dec to Jan) ✔
    - Valentine's / self love → start early to mid **December**
    - Spring reset / glow up → start **January**
18. **The week between Christmas and New Year's still converts:** "55% of weekly Pinterest users keep buying online". → Plan app / early-access pins for Dec 26–31. *(holiday guide 2026)*

## Part 2 · Paid: keep for later

**When:** only once organic is growing. Megan: "If our organic content is growing". Trigger: organic analytics show which topics and pins get outbound clicks; the first ad money then goes behind those proven winners.

**Before spending anything:** install the Pinterest Tag and the Conversions API so app installs and sign-ups can be measured. (Academy has courses on both; Production Safety Rule: touching the site/app tracking needs Megan's approval.)

What Pinterest says about ads:
- **Image ads:** PNG or JPEG; max 20MB (desktop tools) / 32MB (in-app); 2:3 recommended; title up to 100 characters (first 40 show); description up to 800. *(creative best practices)*
- **Formats:** video ads (standard or max width), idea ads ("show ideas in action... then encourage next steps"), and Premiere Spotlight (premium takeover). *(advertise)*
- **Lower funnel:** Shopping ads, Carousels and Collections are "the most effective formats". Brands adding Shopping ads saw "15% higher ROAS and 2.6x higher conversion rates". This is product-catalog led, so it's more relevant to Glammi than to the ISSUE11 app. *(lower-funnel creative)*
- **Call to action:** use a clear one ("Shop now", "Buy today", for us "Get early access"). Avoid "Learn more" when the goal is action. Put it at the bottom. *(lower-funnel creative)*
- **Performance+:** auto-crops to 2:3. Pinterest reports "an average increase of 6% in outbound clicks", and "an overall 28% improvement in ROAS during testing for SMB advertisers" with ROAS bidding. *(Performance+, holiday guide)*
- **Testing:** use A/B tests or multi-objective ads on creative. *(creative best practices)*
