# ISSUE11 Pinterest strategy v1 (Nov 15, 2026, draft for Megan's OK)
Goal (Megan): "100k in traffic per month to our website" with Pinterest as the main engine, plus SEO/AEO.
Sources: the installed skills pinterest-growth, pinterest-seo, pinterest-pin-design and pinterest-growth-manager; issue11.app/sitemap.xml (27 live articles, Nov 15).

## The logic
1. Pinterest is a search engine, so pins need keywords: keyword-first title, keyword on the image, a matching board, and a matching page.
2. Every pin links to the page that delivers what it promises, never the homepage. Right now all pins go to /rebrand-kit. Change: use the 27 live articles plus the lead magnets as destinations.
3. Fresh designs drive the traffic: 3–5 truly different pins per page, at least 72 hours apart for the same page.
4. Volume ramps steadily: about 3/day now, 5/day in December, 8–10/day from January. 28 pages × 5 designs = 140 pins, enough for about a month at 5/day.
5. Seasonal first: New Year is the biggest wave, and it is inside the 45–90 day window now. Order of priority:
   - new-year-vision-board, vision-board-ideas, vision-board-examples, word-of-the-year-ideas, new-year-affirmations, new-year-journal-prompts, december-january-journal-prompts, how-to-make-a-vision-board
   - then personal-rebrand, how-to-glow-up, money-affirmations, self-love-affirmations
6. Boards are keyword shelves, about 12, one per topic cluster.
7. Loop: every new AEO article gets 5 pins, which bring traffic, which brings sign-ups (the waitlist / kit form is on every article).
8. Measure weekly: outbound clicks per pin and per page in Pinterest Analytics, plus site visits. Make more of whatever wins.
9. Expect a long game: 3–6 months to traction, then it compounds (skill guidance, not a promise).
