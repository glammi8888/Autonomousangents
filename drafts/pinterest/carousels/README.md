### Adapting the 1080×1350 carousels
- **TikTok photo mode 1080×1920:** keep the 1080×1350 slide untouched in the centre (285px band above and below). Extend the slide's own background colour (or the cover photo, re-cropped taller from the 928×1232 original) into both bands. Keep all text inside the centre 4:5; the bottom ~400px of a TikTok screen sits under the caption, username and buttons, and the right edge under the like/share column, so nothing readable goes in the bottom band or tight to the right edge.
- **Pinterest 1000×1500 (2:3):** scale the slide to 1000 wide (1000×1250) and add 125px of the same background above and below, or re-lay the slide at 1000×1500 the way CAR2–CAR4 are. Pinterest allows 2–5 slides, so cut to 5: CAR16 → 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (drop the ending; issue11.app stays on every slide). CAR17 → 1, 2, 3, 4, 6.
