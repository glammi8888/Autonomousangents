manifestation journal prompts 💌

for one quiet evening:
  • What have you saved the most this year?
  • Describe an ordinary Tuesday a year from now.
  • Which "one day" have you been saying for too long?
  • If nobody would see this page, what would you admit you want?
  • Whose life do you keep watching?
  • What would you stop saying yes to?
  • What's one move you could make in 20 minutes?
  • What's most likely to get in the way this week?
  • What changed since last week, even slightly?
