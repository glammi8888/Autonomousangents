# Desire bank for list pins (Nov 14, 2026)

Megan's rule: list pins are written as the customer's desires, in her own words. Specific, bold and fun. Lowercase, Notes-style. Keep Megan's casing ("OFF").

## Megan's lines (word for word, Nov 14)
- getting engaged
- being rich af (2027)
- business popping OFF
- become my own dream girl
- $20,000 saved
- rebranding my entire life (Lexicon) · glowing up · looking hotter (Megan, Nov 14)

Ticks go only on identity lines (rebrand, glowing up, looking hotter, become my own dream girl), never on money or engagement lines, so a tick never reads as a promised result.

## Rules
- No promised outcomes or dates on our side. These are her wishes in first person ("my 2027 list"), never "you will get...".
- "Become my own dream girl" is fine in first person. Never "dream girl" alone as a name or keyword.
- No faith words. Never "become her".

## Notes pin titles and sublines (Megan, Nov 14)
- Titles: plain Notes titles. "My 2027:" and "Me 🫱🏻‍🫲🏽 2027" (colour emoji, Megan's exact characters). Never big hook titles.
- Sublines: short claim lines, like "claim it". Others in the same style: "yes, i claim it", "same girl, bigger dreams".
