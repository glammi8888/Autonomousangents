**Patterns that win (formats to borrow)**
1. **The free system:** "I made an entire ___ system (FREE)" + a roadmap broken down month → week → habit. The highest comments by far. → Our match is the **free rebrand kit / 2027 vision board kit**, as a carousel that shows what's inside, and on Pinterest the link to get it.
2. **Proof the vision board came true:** "my camera roll is starting to look like my vision board", "I manifested 95% of my vision board". → This is ISSUE11's exact promise. Carousel idea: **"my 2026 vision board vs my camera roll"** (needs Megan's real photos or a real user's, never faked).
3. **Numbered series:** "13/15", "pt 2". People follow to get the rest. → Run the personal rebrand carousel as a series (physical 1/5, mental 2/5…).
4. **"All you'll ever need" lists + "save this":** short, complete-feeling lists with a save prompt. → Fits the phone-notes format, which already wins for us.
5. **Aesthetic-only slideshows:** no text, just a mood (clean girl, cozy). → Cheap to make from the rebrand-kit photos. Test one.
6. **"Lock in… and actually stick to it":** winter arc framed as discipline that lasts. → Same angle as our winter arc article ("mistakes to skip").

**Watch out**
- Several viral posts say "become HER". That phrase stays banned for us.
- Two used celebrity names in hashtags (e.g. Natalie Portman). We don't use real people's photos.
