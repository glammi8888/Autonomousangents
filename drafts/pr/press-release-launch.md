Many women can describe the life they want in detail, but the vision ends up scattered across screenshots, Pinterest boards, Notes app lists and journals they stopped opening. ISSUE11 brings it into one place and makes it something she can see, hold and come back to.

**How it works**
- **Your Issue:** a personal, 40-page editorial magazine about the life she's creating. The Editor, ISSUE11's built-in AI, lays out the pages from her own words and the images she chooses. Her words stay her own.
- **One small move a week:** instead of to-do lists or streaks, ISSUE11 suggests one achievable step each week. When she takes it, the proof becomes a page in her Issue.
- **Guided audio:** visualizations, affirmations and subliminal sessions, voiced by the founder over soft, musical soundscapes.
- **Journal:** she writes straight onto a magazine page and chooses what goes into her Issue.
- **Print:** when her Issue is complete, she can order a printed copy right from the app ($29.99, free standard shipping in the US).
