Then imagine the person you'd need to become, and what that person would do when things got difficult. That second picture is the one that gets you to send the email or book the class. When I build, I write the logic first (what you see, what you can do, where it goes) before any screen, because rewriting a screen costs far more than rewriting a paragraph.
