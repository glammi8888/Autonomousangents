**2. What do the pages ranking for "how to get recommended by ChatGPT" leave out for beauty and jewelry brands?**

They're written for software companies. They tell you to get mentioned in "best of" lists and on Reddit, which matters, but they skip what a beauty or jewelry customer actually asks AI: who's licensed, what it costs, whether it's near me, whether the diamond is lab grown. If your site doesn't answer those questions plainly, one page per treatment or collection, AI fills the gap with someone else's answer. They also skip measurement. You need a fixed set of real customer questions checked across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity on a schedule, or you're guessing.

**3. What do brands decide too quickly, or in the wrong order, when they start trying to get recommended by AI?**

They start with tactics before positioning. They add an llms.txt file or pay for a tool before they can say in one sentence who they're for and why someone should pick them. AI repeats what it can confirm, so the order that works is: be clear on positioning, make your facts identical everywhere, build answer-first pages for the questions customers ask, earn reviews and mentions that say the same thing, then measure monthly. The other quick decision is expecting a guarantee. No one controls AI answers. Anyone promising you'll be "#1 in ChatGPT" is selling a guess.
