# PART 2: THE CONCEPT

Okay, that clicks much better than the site did. "Process, not outcome" is the sentence I needed. The site made it sound like a vision board app with a magazine skin. Your explanation makes it sound like a magazine that fills up as I do things, which I love: 40 pages over 3 months, based on what I actually did. That's a reason to come back that isn't guilt.

**What clicks:**
- My Pinterest saves become pages. I have 900 pins sitting there doing nothing.
- One small move a week. I can do one a week. I can't do a daily practice, and I've proved that.
- No streaks, no guilt. That's key for a quitter like me.
- The Editor doesn't rewrite my words and can be switched off. I'm wary of AI, so that's good.
- A printed copy at the end as a "proof I did it" object.

**What doesn't:**
- "Behavior-change psychology" is still a claim, with no source or example.
- The rhythm of 3 months per Issue means the payoff is slow. I'd want something pretty in week one.
- Journaling and meditation are the two things I've already failed at. I'd probably use saves, the weekly move, and the magazine, and skip Scribe.

**What I still don't believe:** that I'll still be opening it in week 6. The weekly move is the hook, but nothing shows me it will feel good enough to keep me going.

**Price:** $9.99/mo is Spotify money, but I already pay for Spotify and a fitness app, so a third subscription is a stretch. $39.99 for the first year is easier to say yes to, since it's a few coffees. $69.99 after that is a decision I'd make later.
