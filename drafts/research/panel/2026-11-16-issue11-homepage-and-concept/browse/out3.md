**[screens 13-14]** A giant wall of white text from the founder. No photo. I skim it. "Brings us back" is nice, but a faceless founder quote on an unreleased app doesn't earn trust from me. I want to know who these people are.

The price answer finally lays it all out, but I notice the yearly plan has a free trial and nothing here says how to cancel. That's the thing that burned me last time. I'd also notice "iPhone only" is hidden here. I have an iPhone, so fine, but it's buried.

**[screen 17]** "Stop manifesting from the couch." Eh, a little guilt-trippy. The button sends me back up to the email field.

**[screen 18]** Footer. Blog, Privacy, Terms. Nothing here that reassures me.

**Sign-up form:** one email field. Easy, but I don't know what happens next. Do I get a confirmation? What does "approved" mean? Who approves? Will I be charged automatically? None of that is stated. No reviews, no press, no App Store badge, and the app doesn't exist yet. If I were to Google "ISSUE11 reviews," I'd find nothing, and that's a problem.

**Where I'd leave:** I wouldn't rage-quit. I'd get to the FAQ, feel kind of curious, and then close the tab and think "maybe later." The most likely exit point is screens 7 to 12, where it turns into a long feature tour. If anything, my text-back to my friend would be "cute, send me the link again when it's live."

# PART 2: THE CONCEPT

Okay, hearing it from a person helps a lot. Here's what clicks:

- **"Process, not outcome."** That's the clearest line in all of this. The website made it sound like a mystical thing with extra steps, but this is basically "stop expecting the universe to do it, do one small thing." I can get behind that.
- **The weekly move that I can skip or shrink.** No streaks, no guilt. I have a toddler, I will miss weeks. Knowing it won't scold me is a real selling point.
- **The magazine fills up over time from what I actually did.** That's a nice idea. It's a proof-of-life thing instead of a wish list. It might actually feel good to look at in three months.
- **The Editor never rewrites my words and can be turned off.** Good. I don't want an AI putting words in my mouth.

What doesn't click or I still don't believe:

- It's still a subscription for something that could be a note on my phone that says "do one thing for me this week." I'm paying for the pretty packaging.
- "Behavior-change psychology" still reads like a buzzword to me. A bunch of apps say that.
- I did vision boards and affirmations for a year and it didn't work. This looks like a prettier, more structured version, but I'm not sure the actual mechanism is different from a good habit app.
- The meditations and the magazine builder feel like too many features. I'd probably use one or two and ignore the rest.
- The price is $69.99/yr after the founding year, and $9.99/mo is high for something I might use for three weeks. And I don't know how to cancel. Under the last app I paid $7/week for a while before I figured it out. That's the thing I'd need to see in writing.

What I'd use: the weekly move, maybe the saves, and honestly the printed magazine once, as a treat. What I wouldn't: the meditations, probably the journaling, and the heavy design stuff.

# PART 3: VERDICT

**VERDICT AFTER THE WEBSITE ALONE:** maybe later

**VERDICT AFTER THE CONCEPT:** join waitlist now (it's free, I'd use a throwaway-ish email check first, and I'm only in for the weekly-move idea)

**WOULD YOU PAY $39.99 first year then $69.99/yr, or $9.99/mo?** Maybe. $39.99 is okay to try, but only if cancelling is obvious and I can see real reviews first.

**FIRST IMPRESSION SCORE:** 6/10

**WHAT CONFUSED YOU:**
- What happens after I request access: who approves, how long, and is there any charge?
- Is the "7-day free trial" automatic billing after? How do I cancel?
- Why "invite-only" for something anyone can apparently join?
- What the "behavior-change psychology" actually is, since it's repeated and never explained.
- That it's iPhone only, hidden in the FAQ, while I'm on a work laptop.
- Whether the $29.99 print is a one-time thing or something I'll be nudged to buy every issue.

**BIGGEST OBJECTION:** I've been burned by a manifestation app with a trial-then-charge trap, and nothing here proves this one is different.

**WHAT WOULD CHANGE YOUR MIND:**
- A plain "cancel anytime in two taps in Settings" line near the price.
- Real reviews, a Reddit thread, or any user who isn't the founder.
- A name and photo of an actual person or team, plus any evidence for the psychology claim.
- Clear explanation of what happens after I submit my email.
- A free tier or a trial that doesn't need a card.
- Showing the weekly move in action in a short video, not just mockups.

**ONE BLUNT QUOTE:** "I like the idea of one small thing a week that doesn't make me feel guilty, but I'm not paying for another pretty app until someone tells me how to cancel it."
