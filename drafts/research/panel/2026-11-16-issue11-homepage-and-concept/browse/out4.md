# PART 2: THE CONCEPT

Okay, so my friend explains it. What clicks:
- "Process, not outcome." That's much clearer than the site made it. Yes, *that's* why it's different from my vision board.
- An evolving magazine over 3 months that fills up from what I actually do. That's cool, because it's proof of progress, not just pretty pictures.
- No streaks, no guilt. That's huge. Streak apps make me feel bad and I delete them.
- The Editor can be switched off and never rewrites my words. Good, I'm weird about AI touching my writing.

What doesn't click / what I don't believe:
- It's still a lot of apps in one: saves, audio, journal, magazine. I'm not sure I'd use all four. Probably just saves and the magazine.
- The weekly "move" is basically a to-do, even though they say it isn't. Will it really be specific to me, or generic?
- I don't believe the magazine will look as good as the mockups. Templates can look cheap.
- "Behavior-change psychology" with no proof. Who says?

What I'd use: saves → magazine → the print. Maybe the weekly nudge. What I wouldn't: the meditations, probably. I have enough meditation apps I never open.

Price: $9.99/mo is too much for me right now. $39.99 for the first year is okay-ish, since it's like three coffees a month, but then it jumps to $69.99, and I'm not sure I'd still be using it. And a printed copy on top is another $29.99.
