Someone explains a new iPhone app to you in plain words. This is the full explanation.

WHAT IT IS
ISSUE11 is a manifestation app that turns the life you want into your own personal magazine, and then helps you take small real steps toward it. The idea is "process, not outcome": you can't control the outcome, but you are in control of the process.

It has four parts:
1. My saves: you collect images of the life you want (your own photos, Pinterest saves, or curated editorial images) and heart the ones you love. This is your visual world.
2. Audio: curated musical meditations, 5 or 10 minutes, a voice over soft music with visuals. Instead of "imagine you're rich", they rehearse the steps: the morning, the workout, the hard email, so doing them for real feels normal. They keep playing with your phone locked.
3. Scribe: a private journal. You write about the life you want and your reflections. Nothing is shared; you pick which lines go into your magazine. No streaks and no guilt if you skip days.
4. Publish: your saves and your words become a magazine about your future, with your name on the cover. You are the art director and can tell it what to change. It's an evolving magazine: an Issue is 40 pages, it starts with a few pages and fills up over about 3 months from what you actually do. Default rhythm is one Issue every 3 months; you can make more. You can order it printed for $29.99 (free standard shipping in the US, about 2 weeks).

The Editor is the AI built into the app. It is not a chatbot section. It helps you sort your images, lay out your magazine pages and think through what you want. It never rewrites your words, and you can switch it off. Once a week it suggests one small "next move" toward a page you love (example: "Send the pitch you've been sitting on"). You can skip it, shrink it, or do it and add a photo. It is not a to-do list, goal tracker or productivity app.

THE PHILOSOPHY
Desire, then imagine, then embody, then become. Figure out what you actually want, make it vivid, practice being the person who lives that life in small ways, and over time collect proof that your life is changing. The makers call it "the new way of manifestation": "You don't have to see it to believe it. But you have to participate to create it." It is meant for people who tried affirmations, vision boards and meditations and felt nothing changed, and for people who still believe but are stuck starting and stopping. It does not promise results; it focuses on what you can control.

WHO IT'S FOR
Women, mostly in their late 20s and 30s, in a "next chapter" (new city, new job, breakup, a new decade), who save a lot of vision board and glow up content and want to actually live it instead of scrolling other people's lives.
