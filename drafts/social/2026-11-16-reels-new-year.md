## 1 · Every picture gets a chore (New Year vision board)
**Duration:** 25s · **Framework:** List-Stack-Escalate · **Article's best idea:** every image on the board gets one small move written under it ("A calm apartment" becomes "clear the hallway shelf this Saturday"), plus an ordinary day and one proof from this year.

**Hooks (pick one, test another later)**
- **A (primary, pattern interrupt):** "This year every picture on my vision board gets a chore." On screen: **EVERY PICTURE GETS A CHORE**
- **B (curiosity):** "The one thing I'm adding to my 2027 vision board that nobody puts on theirs." On screen: **THE THING NOBODY ADDS**
- **C (identity, 🗣 only if true):** "If last year's vision board is still on your wall and you couldn't name one thing on it, hi, same." On screen: **LAST YEAR'S BOARD, STILL UP**

**Body**
| Time | Shot | Megan says | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | You to camera, holding a blank board | Hook | Hook text |
| 0:03–0:08 | Overhead: hands lay down 3 cut-outs (home, work, a place) | "Three to five areas. One picture each. Fewer is clearer." | **3–5 AREAS. 1 PICTURE EACH** |
| 0:08–0:14 | Close-up: you write on a sticky note under the home picture | "Under every picture, one small move. Calm apartment? Clear the hallway shelf this Saturday." [PAUSE] | **CALM APARTMENT → CLEAR 1 SHELF SAT** |
| 0:14–0:18 | Hands add a photo of an everyday moment (your coffee, your Sunday walk) | "Add a normal day. Breakfast. A Sunday. The big moments get enough attention." | **ADD AN ORDINARY DAY** |
| 0:18–0:22 | Hands tape on a ticket or note from this year | 🗣 "And one proof from this year. Something I did that scared me." | **+1 PROOF FROM 2026** |
| 0:22–0:25 | Back to you, board in hand | CTA | CTA text |

**CTA (soft):** "The full list, and how to make it a party with friends, is in my bio." On screen: **FULL LIST IN MY BIO**

**B-roll to film (one table, about 10 min):** blank board or poster board · 3 to 5 cut-outs from magazines you own or your own printed photos · sticky notes + marker · one real ticket, receipt or note from this year · your coffee/breakfast photo (yours) · 2 short to-camera clips (hook + CTA). Phone overhead on a stack of books for the hands shots.

**Caption:**
Every picture on my 2027 board gets one small thing written under it. Calm apartment, clear one shelf on Saturday. A trip, look up one flight.
Plus one ordinary day and one thing I did this year that scared me.
