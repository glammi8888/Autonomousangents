# Carousel framework references (Megan, Nov 17, 2026)
Megan, word for word: "here are some graphic ideas that can be added to the carousels". She shared 11 reference images in the old Manager chat. This is the exact description written there (the images themselves aren't in the repo).

## Style (all 11)
- Square-ish slides alternating BLACK and warm CREAM backgrounds.
- Blush pink accents: rounded boxes, circles, thin pink lines and arrows, soft pink fills.
- Bold uppercase condensed sans-serif titles (Inter Tight Bold/Black works).
- Small eyebrow top-left "ISSUE11 / <LABEL>" (e.g. "/ THE CORE FRAMEWORK", "/ OLD VS. NEW", "/ THE PROCESS").
- Short labels in caps with a small parenthesis sub-label.
- Lots of space, one simple diagram per slide.
- Educational, editorial, calm, premium.

## The 11 diagrams
1. **THE MANIFESTATION LOOP** (black): Desire (clarify) → Imagine (rehearse) → Act (participate) → Evolve (refine). Centre: "Continuous creation". Footer: "The new way of manifestation."
2. **THE OLD WAY VS. THE ISSUE11 WAY** (cream table, pink header on the ISSUE11 column):
   - Visualize & wait → Imagine + participate
   - All or nothing → Iterative & adaptive
   - "Be in alignment" → Aligned action
   - Perfect mindset → Human & strategic
   - One-time manifestation → A continuous loop
3. **FROM DESIRE TO REALITY** (black staircase with a rising arrow): 1 Desire (define it) · 2 Imagine (rehearse it) · 3 Act (make it real) · 4 Evolve (adapt & grow).
4. **THE REALITY CREATION MAP** (cream Venn): Belief (it's possible) · Identity (I am her) · Action (I participate) → centre "Reality shift".
5. **THE VERSIONS OF YOU** (black nested circles + arrow): Current you (the starting point) → Aligned you (making it real) → Evolving you (in process) → Expanded you (what's next).
6. **CREATE → TEST → LEARN → EVOLVE** (cream loop, "The adaptive loop"): Create (a plan / experiment) · Test (in real life) · Learn (what works) · Evolve (adjust / level up).
7. **THE REALITY TRIANGLE** (cream): Imagination (create the vision) · Identity (become her) · Action (participate) → centre "Reality creation".
8. **THE EXPANSION CYCLE** (black infinity loop): Experience (live) ↔ Evolve (expand). Checklist: Try · Learn · Adapt · Level up · Repeat.
9. **SELF-CONCEPT → REALITY** (cream Venn): How you see yourself (identity) + How you act (behavior) → How you live (reality). Footer: "Self-concept → decisions → outcomes".
10. **THE REALITY UPGRADE** (cream rising bars + arrow): Clarity (know what) · Capacity (become who) · Consistency (do the work) · Expansion (sustain & grow).
11. **THE NEW WAY OF MANIFESTATION** (black loop, "A continuous creation system"): Imagine (create) → Participate (act) → Adapt (learn) → Evolve (expand).
