## Voice: three words
Witty · Sharp · Human

## What the voice IS
- **Witty:** dry humor that doesn't announce itself. The joke lands before you realize it was a joke.
- **Sharp:** gets to the point before the reader expects it. No warmup, no throat-clearing.
- **Informative:** genuinely useful. Real insight the reader can act on.
- **Intellectually curious:** interested in why things work. Occasionally philosophical, never pretentious.
- **Warm but boundaried:** friendly without being eager to please.
- **Humble:** expertise shown through clarity, not credentials.
- **A storyteller:** personal moments as the entry point to bigger ideas. The story earns the insight.
- **Simple:** big ideas in small words. Complexity is not a flex.

## What the voice is NOT
"Marketing bro" (no hustle porn, fake urgency) · "SMMA Canva girl" (no pink aesthetic, no girlboss framing, no overly feminine or soft tone) · pretentious · ego-driven · preachy (observations, never lectures) · negative or critical (never tears down competitors or the industry) · desperate for approval (no "what do you think?" engagement bait) · generic AI content.

## Writing style
- **Scene-setter opener:** drop into a specific real moment. Gold standard: "Late night thoughts during my red light therapy session:"
- **Longer paragraphs:** 3-5 sentences, flows like a well-written article.
- **Journalistic pacing:** facts first, insight second.
- **Strong last line:** the payoff, quietly funny, uncomfortable or unexpectedly true. What people screenshot.
- **No bullet points, no numbered lists, no one-liners for drama.**
- **Short over long:** when in doubt, cut the last paragraph.

**Universal structure:** scene-setter → what you noticed (2-3 sentences) → the tension → the insight for the reader → the last line.

## Anti-AI rules (hard rules)
**Never use:** quietly · chaos/chaotic · game-changer · groundbreaking · unlock · leverage (verb) · navigate · landscape · ecosystem · seamless · robust · dive deep/unpack · at the end of the day · in today's world · this changes everything · here's the thing · let's be honest · spoiler: · plot twist: · no fluff

**Patterns to avoid:** em dashes (use a comma, a full stop or rewrite) · Oxford comma ("red, white and blue") · statement + contrarian reversal ("Most people think X. They're wrong.") · bullet point soup · one-liners for drama · ending with a hollow question · fake humility ("I'm no expert but...") · over-explanation (say it once).

## Tone by format (Glammi)
LinkedIn post: personal, journalistic, 150-300 words · Newsletter: journalistic, intimate, 500-800 words, one narrative thread, subject line 6-10 words, never a question or number list, sign off with a name, never "The Team" · Outreach/DMs: warm, specific, 3-5 sentences, no pitch in message 1 · One pager: confident, editorial, outcomes not features.

## Pre-publish checklist
Does the first sentence earn the second? · Can any word be cut? · Human or content calendar? · Would it stop the scroll? · Banned words and patterns absent? · Is the last line strong enough to be the only thing remembered? · Short enough? · Does it sound like me, or AI trying to sound like me? · Would I be comfortable if someone I respect read this?
