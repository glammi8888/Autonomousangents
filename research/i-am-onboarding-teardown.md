## What makes it work
- Easy, personal questions first, with her name used right away.
- Rhythm: a block of questions, then a calm one-line statement screen to breathe.
- Aesthetic choices (icon, theme) just before the paywall, so she's personalized the app and "Try it for free" feels natural.
- Notification permission is primed with a live preview of exactly what she'll receive.
- Day-1 streak shown before she's even in the app.
- Pain questions come late, after she has invested.
- No reward/reply screens after answers (Headway does this better).

## What we don't copy
Religion/beliefs (sensitive data under GDPR), zodiac, employment/relationship (don't serve our loop), mental-health and therapy questions (health-claim risk), uncited "studies show" / "rewire your brain", the bundle.
