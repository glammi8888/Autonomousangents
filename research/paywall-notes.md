# Paywall research notes
Started Oct 8, 2026 (Design Room). Feeds the ISSUE11 paywall (screen 36 in design/ONBOARDING-V3.md). Prices and offers are payment decisions: Megan + Revenue approve.

## Calm (Megan, Oct 8, word for word)
"Calm paywall was interesting.. Monthly payment comes without free trial... But Family plan and yearly plan came with a free trial"
- Screenshot (Oct 8): research/img/calm-paywall-2026-10-08.png ("View more plans" expanded)
  - Headline: "Your plan is ready. Unlock Calm for free" + "Unlimited free access for 7 days".
  - **7-Day Free Trial** group: Individual Yearly **$79.99/yr ($6.67/mo), "Save 61%"** · Family Yearly, 6 members **$119.99/yr, "Save up to 90%"**.
  - **No Free Trial** group: Monthly **$16.99/mo, shown as "$203.88/yr"** (the yearly cost of monthly makes $79.99 look cheap).
  - White "Subscribe" button, small line "$16.99/month. Cancel anytime.", close X clearly visible top-left. Default view shows fewer plans.
  - Tricks: monthly priced high as an anchor; "Save X%" calculated against monthly; plans grouped by trial / no trial; price shown per month AND per year.
- Reading: the free trial is the reward for committing longer. Monthly has no trial, which makes yearly look like the obvious choice (higher value per customer, fewer cancellations).

## Headway / Web2Wave (research/web2app-strategy.md)

## Design Room recommendation (not decided)
- Two plans: **Yearly with a free trial (pre-selected, shown as a monthly equivalent)** + **Monthly, no trial**. Family plan later, if at all.
- Test vs Igor's tiny-intro-price model after launch.
- Before deciding: check current App Store rules for free trials / introductory offers and Family Sharing, and current competitor prices.

## Family plan? (Megan asked, Oct 8)
Design Room recommendation: **not in V1.** Each member gets her own Issue, Lucky Star (AI) and personalized audio, so cost grows per member (unlike Calm's shared audio). Revisit in V1.1 once cost per user is known. On-brand alternative to test then: a **"Bestie / Duo" plan** (2 friends). To verify before deciding: Apple Family Sharing rules for subscriptions (I believe it can't be switched off once enabled).
