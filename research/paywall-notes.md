# Paywall research notes
Started Oct 8, 2026 (Design Room). Feeds the ISSUE11 paywall (screen 36 in design/ONBOARDING-V3.md). Prices and offers are payment decisions: Megan + Revenue approve.

## Calm (Megan, Oct 8, word for word)
"Calm paywall was interesting.. Monthly payment comes without free trial... But Family plan and yearly plan came with a free trial"
- Reading: the free trial is the reward for committing longer. Monthly has no trial, which makes yearly look like the obvious choice (higher value per customer, fewer cancellations).

## Headway / Web2Wave (research/web2app-strategy.md)
- Igor prefers no free trial: a tiny intro price that renews higher. Start with a big discount (≥50%).

## I am (research/i-am-onboarding-teardown.md)
- Returning-user paywall: $4.99/month billed yearly as $59.99/year (shows the monthly equivalent of the yearly price).

## Design Room recommendation (not decided)
- Two plans: **Yearly with a free trial (pre-selected, shown as a monthly equivalent)** + **Monthly, no trial**. Family plan later, if at all.
- Test vs Igor's tiny-intro-price model after launch.
- Before deciding: check current App Store rules for free trials / introductory offers and Family Sharing, and current competitor prices.
