# Voice Lock Kit: Launch & Validation Plan

The goal is to prove demand **before** polishing anything. Budget: 7 days, $0.

## Day 1: Make your own proof

1. Run the kit on **your own** videos (or, with permission, a creator friend's).
2. Save a real before/after: a generic AI caption vs. the voice-locked version of the same video.
3. Screenshot both. This is your content and your sales page image.

## Days 1–3: Validation posts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

Post one per day. Track comments, saves, and DMs.

**Video 1: "Why your AI captions sound like everyone else"**
- Hook (0–2s): "Your AI captions sound fake, and it's not the AI's fault."
- Body: show the generic caption on screen. "The AI has never heard you talk. So it writes like the average of the internet." Show the Voice Guide briefly. Show the rewritten caption.
- CTA: "Comment VOICE and I'll send you the prompt I used."

**Video 2: "I gave AI my transcripts. Here's what it said about how I talk."**
- Read 3 of your "signature phrases" and your NEVER-SAY list out loud (it's funny and personal).
- CTA: "Want yours? Comment VOICE."

**Video 3: "1 video → 10 posts in 10 minutes, still sounds like me"**
- Screen-record the master repurpose prompt running.
- CTA: link in bio → waitlist.

## Green-light thresholds (from the video's validation rule)

| Signal | Go | Iterate | Kill |
|---|---|---|---|
| "VOICE" comments across 3 posts | 30+ | 10–29 | <10 |
| Waitlist sign-ups | 25+ | 10–24 | <10 |
| DMs asking to buy | Any | — | — |

"Iterate" means changing the hook or angle and posting 3 more before deciding.
